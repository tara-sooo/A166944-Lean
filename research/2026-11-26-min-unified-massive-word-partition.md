## 2. Exact word equivalence and physical slot count

At one fixed source (K,C,q,c) and sector s=l,h, take all original stable departure slots (a_v>0), including those with no assigned focal wait. For each v consider the unique actual continuation from its true postdeparture state (v,J) through first completed gain crossing L_s. Determinism and stable future room ensure every stable member of that slot has exactly the same continuation. Define WORD omega_v=((p_i,w_i))_i of selected labels AND actual positive integer waits, excluding the source jump. For two stable slots their full prefixes are unchanged translated paths (same projected edges with constant time lag) IF AND ONLY IF their words agree. If the words differ then, at the first unequal edge or wait, they share the same prehorizon, their arrival lag is the original Delta=v'-v, and the selected labels differ or the exit lag changes. The word stopping rule cannot cause a strict prefix to be compared to a longer word without a defect, because the gain is determined by labels and both end upon first crossing L_s. If v=v' their words must coincide. Thus unlike equality of final lags, word agreement is an equivalence relation exactly characterizing the persistent pairs, including same-slot and self pairs.

Let a_omega=sum_(v:omega_v=omega) a_v, n_stable=sum_omega a_omega. Define the even radical R_omega=lcm(L0,2q,2p_i for all i in word). By general strict even-modulus barrier of aa04b0, R_omega>L_s; any two slots of this word have lag divisible by R_omega, and |lag|<=4H by the original deficit slab. Hence there are at most 1+floor(8H/R_omega) slots in one word, certainly <=1+8H/L_s. If R_omega>4H, the word has exactly one slot. The larger 8H width bound is harmless. For all positive high-focal sources R_omega>4H by the previous high density proof, so a_omega=a_v on that source. In low sector no assertion that all radicals are large.

This equivalence uses actual sequences, not the relaxed condition that lag is divisible by a radical: divisibility alone need not force the candidate MIN trajectory to follow that word. The full original b_v,n_c and original stable subset a_v remain frozen. Words are allowed to differ across sector lengths and scales; no independent product of their populations.

## 3. Exact massive clock partition and complementary first defects

For each grid, original tag, actual right witness crossing k with n_k crossed atoms, and sector s, write W^M_s for the actual retained massive focal wait assigned to that crossing on this tag (only good/usable grids). It is zero unless its source tag is stable, and W^M_l+W^M_h<=c_a P n_k. Put rho^M_s=W^M_s/(c_a P n_k). For the MASSIVE part ONLY use ALL original stable partner tags at that source in the same class, with no smaller-or-equal-slot, rank-on-partner, near-mass, or far-radius restriction on partners. The original good-grid/source focal masks are still used. This is an enlarged pair domain relative to old moderate defect energy; no claim of pointwise dominance by old defect measure. Normalization remains n_c, not n_stable. For each original ordered focal/partner incidence and atom take charge rho^M_s P/n_c. If their words differ, route to the first actual defect, choosing the same nonperiodic-side rule as aa04b0 (focal on a tie); both source tags have enough future room. Because initial nonzero lag divisible by L0 persists up to first defect, at least one selected label p has 2p not dividing it by two-way MIN comparison. All routed labels p>y0. If words agree, leave the corresponding fraction as compatible CLOCK, not defect energy. Instability fraction omitted at source. Thus for each source tag and sector exactly
    W^M_s = W^M_s a_omega/n_c
              + c_a * (sum routed pair charges at its n_k atoms)
              + W^M_s (n_c-n_stable)/n_c.                (1)
Here source word omega belongs to its own slot. This includes diagonal terms correctly: they belong to the compatible part and are not sent to a nonexistent defect. Denote the sum of first terms over sources and sectors by C_P^M; D_P^M=sum routed pair charges; U_P^M=sum final terms. Average all three over the SAME M grids (bar denotes average). Exactly
    gamma W_M <= Cbar^M+c_a Dbar^M+Ubar^M <= W_M,
    Ubar^M <= W_M/(8ell)=o(NT).                         (2)
Since W_M<=3NH, the difference between W_M and Cbar^M+c_a Dbar^M is o(NT). No dependence on number of partners.

Each normalized full-pair row and column sum at an original source atom is <=1, because denominator n_c and total focal and partner tags <=n_c, and fractions rho_l^M+rho_h^M<=1. The old capacity argument by original tagged destination is still valid even for near partners and after enlarging the pair domain: source K in [E-L_s-q+1,E] for first defect prehorizon E, with q<=P/a in low case, q<=B otherwise. Thus the averaged defect measure Dbar_s^M(C,p) <=2 C_s' n(C,p), with C_l'=(1/M)sum_usable(L_l+P/a+P), C_h'=L_h+B+P_max from d7cc573. The total over sectors is Dbar^M. This is the same linear capacity with potentially many more eligible partner pairs. It is not a theorem that Dbar^M=o(NT).

## 4. Compatible clock: concentration and a larger physical cutoff

First remove all compatible clock fractions whose word population a_omega<=tau n_c. Their total is <=tau times the assigned massive clock, i.e. O(tau NT), without removing corresponding other terms of (1). Remaining compatible fractions have a_omega>tau n_c, and there are fewer than 1/tau such words per class. High focal compatible part has a single physical slot per word, necessarily a_v>tau n_c. Low concentrated word may occupy several slots, but at most 1+8H/L_l; in particular it contains at least one slot with
    a_v >= a_omega/(1+8H/L_l)
         > tau H/(A q u ell²) * L_l/(9H)
         >= H/(9 P_max u ell³ tau),                     (3)
where L_l<=H eventually, q<=P/a, and we used the chosen L_l. This is a bound on some actual stable slot, not on every focal slot. For high, at most 1/tau qualifying slots per class; for low, at most 1/tau qualifying words and O(H/L_l) slots per word. These are population-dependent support bounds, not an independent spatial sieve or a proof that all these words have small total mass.

There is a stronger all-mass payment for compatible CLOCK only. Let b=tau^(-1/2), and consider source contributions from focal events with n_e<=b m(e), including former moderate events if using the alternative full route. In a low source, all such focal events share the same cutoff m_0=H/(256P_max u ell³). If a slot has any such assigned focal contribution, stability gives b_v<=2b m_0. A word has <=9H/L_l slots (L_l<=H). Therefore the number of source tags in that word contributing any such focal wait is <=18 b m_0 H/L_l. Dividing by n_c>H/(A q u ell²), using q<=P/a and definition L_l, gives
    #such focal tags in word / n_c <= (18/256) b tau²
                                 = O(tau^(3/2)).
For each tag its total assigned such wait at source <=c_a P n_k. Multiply by the compatible fraction a_omega/n_c and sum over words in the same phase class. Since sum a_omega<=n_c, total compatible clock of such focal events at this class/crossing is <=O(tau^(3/2)) c_a P n_k n_c. This is a COLUMN/word packing bound; it allows larger partners in the SAME word to carry much more mass than focal small slots. Bounding each small focal slot's compatible partner fraction by its own b_v would be incorrect. Sum over all source classes/crossings and average grids, using total original raw boundary weight <=N(H+P) per grid. Cost O(tau^(3/2) NT).

For high such focal events, their words are singletons. For any good high source with any such assigned focal event, b_v<=2b H/[256 max(P_max,L0 p_e)u ell³], and A q/max(P_max,L0 p_e)<=1 eventually: self has q=p_e and A=L0/2; nonself q<=P/a, A<=ell/2<<a. Hence b_v/n_c<=O(b/ell)=o(1) uniformly. Their compatible clock is at most this fraction of the assigned wait, giving O(NT/(ell sqrt(tau))). Combining low and high, we may delete all compatible fractions with focal n_e<=m(e)/sqrt(tau) at total o(NT). With default tau=1/log ell this raises the required physical cutoff by sqrt(log ell). This payment does NOT remove the corresponding entire original actual focal clock; its differing-word fractions must still be accounted through D^M (or D^all below). The previous cutoff separating which events use old moderate route is unchanged.

For any remaining compatible source, its original focal true arrival mass is >m(e)/sqrt(tau), and its word is concentrated. The earlier stable-cohort intersection lemma gives an actual common physical cohort of size (1-o(1))n_e over the focal entropy window, including all its good witnesses. In particular the common cohort's prime roots have span >>u n_e: they are distinct primes in [X,2X), and elementary uniform interval upper sieve bounds a length d interval by O(d/log(2+d)) primes. Since n_e>=m(e)>=B/(poly(u,ell)) for all p_e<=B, log n_e>>u, giving the stated span. At any shared earlier state all these roots have gain at least zero; the smallest root has already progressed at least twice the span. This is a necessary depth for the common physical ancestry, not a bound on its future clock. No assumption that low word partners at distinct slots have already physically merged.

Write C_rem for the averaged compatible clock remaining after these two payments (and optionally any independently proved compatible-clock payments), counted as exact fractions of original focal waits. It is nonnegative and no larger than Cbar^M.

## 5. New defect-key payments and weaker hybrid sufficient condition

At each eligible destination p>y0 use graded h(r)=log²(2+r)/(1+log log(2+r))², sum_C h(r)=O(X/p) from earlier sieve support. For each massive sector s pay Dbar_s^M(C,p) if its effective mass Dbar_s^M/(2 C_s') <= H tau h(r)/(C_s' u ell). Cost O(tau NT) per sector, using sum_p 1/p=O(ell), and optional rough/mid enhancements as before with same budget. Since this energy is multiplied by only c_a=3+o(1), no extra factor 1/tau is needed in its payment; compared with d7cc573's moderate absolute cap it is larger by 1/tau. This does not establish the remaining effective masses are small. Let D_M,rem be the summed two residual massive defect measures. All earlier moderate defect payments and its exact residual D_mod,rem=D_l,rem'+D_h,rem' unchanged. Under finitely many twins and original actual o(NT) masks, (2) and d7cc573 yield
    NT/2-o(NT) <= C_rem + (3+o(1)) D_M,rem
                           + [(3+o(1))/tau] D_mod,rem.    (4)
The factor 1/gamma=1+o(1) has been absorbed. Thus sufficient on unbounded dyadic X:
    liminf [C_rem+3 D_M,rem+(3/tau)D_mod,rem]/(NT)<1/2.     (5)
This HYBRID objective is no larger than the preceding W_M+(3/tau)D_mod,rem, even before asymptotic errors: C_rem+3D_M,rem<=Cbar^M+c_a Dbar^M<=W_M by (2). The proof of the new sufficient condition does not assume a bound on the added massive defect energy or on C_rem. The new term D_M is complementary to compatible clock, not an additional charge on the whole old massive clock. It allows a strictly smaller objective on ensembles with appreciable payable compatible/defect fractions; no proof of strict separation on realized asymptotic ensembles. In the rate-free corollary of d7cc573 one may keep its old adaptive tau and use (5), provided the corresponding compatible+massive-defect strict bound holds on the same subsequence.

Alternative FULL route: replace W_s^M in sections 3-4 by ALL retained good usable focal waits (both moderate and massive). All source stability, sharp capacity, high richness, raw thin-class and partner instability masks hold, and sections 3-4 did not require massive lower bound. The full pair domain and normalization 1/n_c give the same exact partition, coefficient c_a, and same capacity. The compatible payments of section 4 remove compatible clock of all focal n_e<=m(e)/sqrt(tau), including every moderate focal event. Define C_all,rem and D_all,rem after same compatible and constant-coefficient defect masks. Then
    liminf [C_all,rem+3 D_all,rem]/(NT)<1/2                 (6)
also suffices, with NO diverging coefficient. It is a different enlarged defect measure, NOT a claim of pointwise domination of the old moderate energy after changing its pair domain. Only (5) has the explicit no-larger-than-old comparison. Formula (6) may be useful if one can directly bound full-partner defects. Both residual inequalities open. Main and independent branches unchanged; no Lean compilation.

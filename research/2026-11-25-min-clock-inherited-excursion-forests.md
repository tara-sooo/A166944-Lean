## 1. Setup and exact first-contact coarea

Use original all-odd dyadic sources M in [X,2X), N=X/2, B=ceil(sqrt(7X)), u=log B, H=4B, s=M+2, C0=2M-2, original cutoff gate K(M)=floor((7M-1)/4). Each original actual completed wait, including the crossing wait, ends in a selected prime event, with stop e_M<=s_M+H+2B or earlier twin terminal. True deficits d=2t-C in [0,12B+6]; original gain <=12B+6. Fixed-cap MIN determinism. Retain the actual old terminal pair weights and the unique long-age LOW nonreset donor anchors of 24bfe41 and a068c67. At anchor g let d_g>6,D_g=d_g-6, r_g the minimum eligible prime at its unique rough recipient slot, and x_i(g)=w_i/max donor row in [0,1], m_g=sum x_i. Arbitrary fixed nonnegative x_i<=1 are also allowed. Internal donor pairs need not have any OLD terminal charge between themselves.

For each internal unordered pair i,j in g, its true first common state (tau,J*) and deficit d*=2tau-J* are independent of which anchor is considered. Let mu_ij(g) be their common minimum through g, including the first true contact. Inherited high levels are exactly lambda=7,...,mu_ij(g), empty if mu<=6. Their respective last actual stationary upward crossings L_i(lambda)=(z_i,C_i) and L_j(lambda)=(z_j,C_j) survive through g, are distinct, and occur at or before tau. Indeed each donor is at deficit >=lambda at tau and throughout the common path to g; its last upcross must have occurred at or before tau and is the same as its last upcross through tau. An actual stationary recipient may cross lambda at the exact first meeting tick, when the other donor jumps; the two crossings are still distinct. No shared state is assumed before tau. Both upcrosses are in original completed waits by the anchor time. This makes L_i(lambda) independent of which contributing anchor g is used. The first-contact type is also fixed, and root injection (d*=6) and reset (d*=6s<=6) cannot support inherited high levels. Two simultaneous distinct incoming jumps are subject to pq | (d*-6s) and pq<=12B+6 as before; jump-stationary is not restricted to any large label.

Define actual aggregate inherited pair-level weight
    b_ij(lambda) = sum_(g containing i,j, mu_ij(g)>=lambda) (r_g/B) x_i(g)x_j(g).
It is nonnegative, nonincreasing in integer lambda>=7 and zero above d*. The exact coarea is
    I^sharp = sum_(i<j) sum_(lambda>=7) b_ij(lambda).           (1)
For a fixed pair its target slots are distinct original completed rough recipient slots. The gain bound used in a068c67 gives
    b_ij(lambda) <= 1.5(12B+6)/B <=27=:U_0,                 (2)
without a factor B/R. Every such pair shares an original anchor, so both starts lie within 6B of its clock and |i-j|<=6B. There are O(XB) distinct possible internal donor pairs. This is a different ensemble from the original charged pair support, whose earlier H+3 separation bound need not be used here.

## 2. Absolute payments for low levels and recent independent upcrosses

Choose ell=max(1,floor(u^(11/4))). Pay all aggregate inherited charge at levels lambda<=6+ell. For each pair this has at most ell levels. In the remaining charge pay every level for which at least one of the two actual last upcrosses occurred in the ell ticks immediately preceding or at first contact (tau-z<ell). For a given pair, at most 2ell upward levels per endpoint can be born in those ticks, because each stationary tick crosses two adjacent integer levels and an event tick never crosses upward. Thus the additional number is <=4ell per pair. By (2) and the O(XB) possible pairs,
    P_low+P_recent=O(XB ell)=o(XB u^3).                    (3)
These are absolute contributions of I^sharp itself, independent of the number of later anchors and of a future reference length. They do not delete original trajectory occupation or pay cross weights W_g one-for-one. The residual has lambda>6+ell and both independent upward origins at z_i,z_j<=tau-ell. Each remains at deficit >=lambda from its last upcross through first contact (and to every anchor contributing positive b). In particular neither source can have crossed below lambda during these precontact intervals. These are two real independent histories, not solely ancestor multiplicity.

## 3. NEW first-contact forest of excursion tokens

Assign each unordered internal pair once to a common cell of two shifted source partitions of width16B, shift8B, possible by the 6B separation. Each cell has at most 8B+1 odd roots. Each original source belongs to one cell per partition, hence at most two such cell graphs. A bookkeeping common fixed-cap reference can extend all original sources in a cell to T=max_cell(s)+6B or actual twin terminal, inside all original K(M) for sufficiently large X. No original charge is created by this extension. It permits tracking shared suffixes beyond the earlier original endpoint when reasoning about connected components. All charged first contacts and upcrosses are in the actual original paths.

For a fixed cell and integer lambda, make a vertex token (M,z,lambda) for every positive remaining pair-level endpoint, where z is that source's last true upward crossing of lambda through its first contact. This token represents the particular above-level excursion of that source beginning at z; the excursion ends at its first downward crossing below lambda, or at twin terminal/reference stop. A source can have multiple tokens at one lambda after separate upward crossings, and they must NOT be identified. Each token has a definite horizon C=2z-lambda or 2z-lambda+1 according to the parity of lambda (C even). The level key (z,C) is thus unique given z and lambda. Initialize components by equality of that physical upcross key, not by singletons. All their member tokens actually share that true state and its above-level excursion continuation. They need not have had any original charged edge between themselves. Initialize offline, but each such class is activated only at its own upcross time; every edge involving it occurs no earlier than that time.

Process the residual weighted edges in increasing actual first-contact time tau, batching all distinct physical keys at that time before merging. At a key (tau,J*) merge the current components containing endpoints of the edges assigned to it. Components associated with expired excursions never take part in a later event. At every actual edge, endpoints must be in different precomponents: if linked through earlier events or same-upcross class unions, deterministic common reference would make their true states agree at some earlier tick, contradicting first physical contact. An initialization union at tau itself cannot create a false earlier connection, since it joins only equal upcross keys, and any component linked via an earlier charged edge already has its upcross before that earlier edge; the charged pair itself has distinct upcross keys. All connected tokens share the same reference continuation and expiration once they meet. Therefore a component at an event cannot contain two distinct excursion tokens of the same source: those excursions would be disjoint, not both active at the meeting. This also proves each component is incident to at most one physical key at a given tick, and its cardinality never exceeds the number of cell roots. Simultaneous multiway events use BEFORE-event sizes. Retain components in bookkeeping after expiry but do not connect them to new excursions. Every edge has distinct original source endpoints at its meeting.

Let n_v^0 be the initial upcross-class size of a token and n_v^-,n_v^+ its pre/post size at a merge. The exact telescoping identity is
    sum_events log(n_v^+/n_v^-)=log(n_v^final/n_v^0)
         <= log(8B+1).
A component may already have many donors at the upcross, so the ratio starts from its true class size, not from 1. A light child has n_v^-<=n_v^+/2, hence its logarithmic contribution is >=log2; any edge joins at least one light child.

The total token memberships across ALL levels and cells is O(XB), not O(XB log B) or O(XB²). Each is witnessed by one actual original stationary tick which creates only two level tokens, with a factor at most two for cells:
    #tokens <=4 sum_M(e_M-s_M)=O(XB).                      (4)
There is no duplication per later anchor because (1) first aggregated the pair-level weights. The exact sum of log growth can be much smaller than the crude token-count times log B bound. This forest is constructed on the actual inherited support only, not the full original ancestry; optional earlier pair-level payments freeze remaining weights and determine a new support before the forest is built.

## 4. Absolute excursion entropy quota

Set a=u^(7/4). At an event and endpoint token v let its constraint be the total current incident inherited edge weight at that event, and allocate capacity
    c_(v,event)=U_0 a log(n_v^+/n_v^-).
Repeatedly pay all incident remaining weights of any constraint whose current degree is <=capacity. Degrees decrease, each constraint pays at most once, and an edge is paid once. Sizes/caps are frozen from the original support forest before this deletion. Therefore
    P_exc <= U_0 a sum_tokens log(n_v^final/n_v^0)
           =O(XB u^(11/4))=o(XB u^3).                    (5)
This is an absolute inherited-energy payment over all levels and contacts. At every surviving edge both endpoints have weighted degree >capacity at that actual level and first-contact key. By (2), each needs more than a log(n_v^+/n_v^-) distinct charged opposite partners. At a fixed level and event those partner tokens correspond to distinct original sources. In particular a light endpoint needs >a log2 actual distinct high-excursion partners, all with both upcrosses at least ell ticks earlier after (3). All events with total post-size <=1+a log2 are fully paid (each edge has a light endpoint whose degree <=U_0(n^+-1)). This is stricter in type than the earlier aggregate old-contact forest: the partners must actually carry a surviving independently created high level at that SAME first contact, not simply share any old future energy.

Optional refinement, paper only: at a fixed event allocate an endpoint's capacity across the actual opposite PREcomponents, proportional to their sizes divided by the sum of opposite sizes. The total remains the same and the peeling proof still applies. A residual edge requires actual partners in the specific opposite high-level component. This need not give a diverging threshold for a tiny component. The audit uses the unsplit capacity.

Combining (3),(5) yields the new sufficient reduction
    I^sharp <= I_exc,res + O(XB u^(11/4)).
Here I_exc,res is the actual remaining inherited charge, not raw ancestry energy or old terminal charge. No small-o bound for it is obtained.

## 5. NEW near-upcross inherited term transports to shared moment

An independent deterministic comparison uses the existing donor anchor g and a fixed lambda in [7,d_g]. Partition its donors by last-upcross key; write A_z=sum of their x_i in the class with clock z. At fixed lambda different keys have different z, and for any two such keys
    C_i-C_j=2(z_i-z_j).
The inherited part at that level is exactly sum_(z<z') A_z A_z'. The shared and diagonal moment at the level satisfy
    sum_z A_z² = sum_i x_i² + 2 sum_(i<j with same key) x_i x_j.
For a positive integer h, restrict inherited charge to 0<|z_i-z_j|<=h. Each clock has at most 2h different clocks in this band. Applying 2ab<=a²+b² on each unordered class pair,
    sum_(0<z'-z<=h) A_z A_z' <= h sum_z A_z².
Sum over lambda and g with r_g/B to obtain the global comparison
    I_near^sharp(h) <= h (Z^sharp+2 S^sharp)
                     <= O(h XB)+O(h E_+^full).           (6)
This is an actual weighted pair-band estimate, no assumption of independence or arithmetic uniformity. It costs no separate later-anchor reuse factor. For fixed h it is a constant-factor transport to the same upstream positive energy used in a068c67; for growing h the explicit factor h remains. It is NOT an unconditional little-o estimate at the target scale; E_+^full is still open. Similarly if at most k upcross classes occur at a given g,lambda, the whole inherited term there is <=(k-1)/2 times the class-square sum, by Cauchy. This handles a bounded number of classes conditionally on the upstream moment, not a general class-count bound.

Paper-only absolute near-thin refinement: for each g,lambda and a vertex i, restrict to opposite keys within h whose class mass A_z<=m/(2h), where m=u^(11/4). The total weight of these neighbors is <=x_i m<=m. Orient each such edge toward a vertex whose opposite class is thin (ties deterministic); pay these weights once. Sum r_g/B across original source memberships and all lambda<=d_g<=12B+6. The per-source total is O(B) by the original completed-target gain budget. Hence the payment is O(XB m)=o(XB u³), even for growing h, and only near edges with both class masses >m/(2h) remain. This proof counts actual normalized mass, not candidate arithmetic keys, and is a separate optional priority before (6). It was not implemented in the audit.

A far upcross pair has exact origin horizon distance >2h and distinct actual histories. At tick z_later-1, the later-crossing donor is below lambda and has horizon C_later, while the earlier-crossing donor is at or above lambda and thus has strictly smaller horizon (both even). This statement uses persistence of the earlier last crossing and applies when both have started, which they have by their respective upcross times. They may meet at z_later itself (one jumps and one stays), which the recent filter (3) removes. It does not impose a lower bound of 2h on their horizon separation at later times. The remaining far-origin case still needs an arithmetic/occupation argument.

The entropy and near comparison can be combined on disjoint remaining pair-level portions, but numerical comparisons below are independent (near measured on raw I^sharp). Any prior or later reduction that changes the positive graph can require recomputing capacities before new peeling. Equations (3)-(6) relate donor-square moments and must not be added directly as reductions to original W_g without the cross-transfer coefficients of a068c67 or another proved inequality.

## 7. Next research target

For the heavy excursion merges, use actual first-contact jump/recipient predecessor geometry at persistent high lambda. In particular after the recent filter both donors independently survived >=ell ticks before contact, so a short LOW tail cannot account for their last upward crossings if ell is much larger than the primorial length. Investigate the two last-upcross classes and their separate intervening gain, especially the jump-stationary small-label case with large recipient mass. For far origin separations, use the exact 2:1 horizon/clock gap, their common deficit threshold, and actual first-crossing cuts to locate separately accumulated gains. Also retain the open full positive upstream moment and cross-to-donor coefficient/recipient reuse from a068c67. No claim of closure of I^sharp, E_+^full, or any previous whole-energy residual; twin infinitude remains open.

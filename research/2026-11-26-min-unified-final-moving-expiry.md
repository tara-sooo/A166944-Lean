## 2. Pay moving focal waits in ALL sectors without the rough sieve

At each projected horizon C each tag has at most one completed event; each moving tag contributes at most 2B actual wait. There are O(X) possible C. Consequently
    W_mov,all <= O(X B M_*) = O(NT B^(-1/8+o(1))) = o(NT).
Indeed NT=Theta(X²/u^1.25) and X=Theta(B²), so the ratio is O(u^1.25 M_*/B). The polynomial exponent absorbs every fixed logarithmic factor. Unlike the preceding high-only sieve argument, this works for all p and all waits, without the expensive threshold. Every original assigned focal wait contributes at most its own clock on each grid, since original right-witness assignment is disjoint and normalized partner mass <=1, and later resampling preserves the total compatible clock. Split compatible clock by the original assigned waits (linearly, preserving original masks and word fraction), if needed. Pay all remaining exact forward and backward fractions whose ORIGINAL focal wait is moving, including low waits and compatible waits. The old high moving payment is included; apply new mask only to remaining fractions. This makes no inference about the partner's original waits.

## 3. Both ordered roles at the actual source, all directions

At a fixed original source crossing and phase class n, there are <=M_* moving tags at the recorded prehorizon C, including all possible departing slots. Moving status is constant through the waiting ticks of that original event; use predeparture state before the jump. For the forward kernel, all ordered clock fractions with a moving focal or moving partner have total <=2 c_a P M_* by row and column capacities. Any positive class is large n>=n_min; hence this is <=2 c_a P n M_*/n_min. Summing over original source atoms/classes and averaging over the unchanged grids gives O(NT M_*/n_min)=o(NT). This applies to low and high together, including a nonself high partner whose earlier original waits need not match the focal history.

For backward same-slot resampling use the full original slot size b>=b_min for any positive source. At most M_* of its tags are moving at C; all fractions involving either such role have clock <=2 c_a P M_* <=2 c_a P b M_*/b_min. The same summed raw boundary count N(H+P_max) gives O(NT M_*/b_min)=o(NT). These can alternatively be combined in the joint kernel with the more restrictive original denominators preserved. Use the two separate valid bounds here. Thus in every surviving forward or backward pair BOTH source participants have nonmoving histories at the actual source, in addition to the original focal wait being nonmoving by section 2. In particular all their recorded horizons at distance at most k+1 in nonLOW depth behind source are projected-heavy A(C)>m0 and their original root is outside this depth. This is a simultaneous condition on each actual history, not a lower bound on every true physical slot. No old denominator altered.

## 4. Source-cohort last light and final moving run

Fix one original grid atom and actual positive backward source slot G of full b tags; regard only its original histories through common predeparture physical state S=(v-1,C). All G members present at any earlier shared state deterministically continue to S, with no loss of the induced source cohort. Pair first meeting U is unique. Count full original pairs for upper bounds, even if their exact charge has been paid.

First consider pairs with a common projected light horizon (A<=m0) at or after first meeting and no later than S, allowing the projected horizon at the meeting itself. For a fixed member i all such partners must visit i's LAST light recorded horizon before S: since the pair shares its suffix and the light horizon is at/after U, this last light is at/after U as well. Even if its recorded arrival at the meeting horizon preceded the meeting, every partner visited that projected horizon. At most m0-1 distinct partners can do this. Thus there are at most b(m0-1)/2 unordered such pairs in G. Multiplying by 2 c_a P/b for both orientations gives <=c_a P m0, and summing positive slots/atoms/grids gives O(NT m0/b_min)=o(NT). This is also implied by the old induced-parent smallness mask eventually: every original source member visiting U continues to that light horizon, so its induced population <=m0<<tau b. We include the explicit bound to separate cases rigorously; no claimed novel extra saving from this already payable sector.

For remaining pairs there is NO common light horizon at/after U before S. For each i in G define L_i to be its latest eligible start at or before S on its own recorded history. Such start exists (root). Its FINAL MOVING RUN is from L_i through the last recorded state/ticks at depth <=k+1 after L_i, clipped at S; its last true vertex is E_i. There is no intervening eligible start after L_i through S by definition, so this is one contiguous interval on the actual history, with every state moving. It is not necessarily the first GOOD prefix or a globally final run for the full trajectory beyond S. If two tags have no common light after meeting, neither can have an eligible start strictly after the meeting before S: a root injection cannot occur twice for the same tag. Thus each member's L_i occurs no later than U (in actual time). If it is moving at U then its final run contains U. If it is nonmoving at U it has already passed E_i, and remains nonmoving on the shared suffix through S. These implications are the reason to exclude later-light pairs first; no unjustified use of monotonicity for moving.

## 5. Both-moving first meetings at arbitrary absolute depth

For any unordered pair with both members moving at first meeting U and no later light, both final moving runs contain U. Charge the pair to the earlier of their final expiries E_i,E_j. At that actual true state both are present in their respective final runs: their common suffix includes both expiries, both runs already started by U, and neither expired before the earlier expiry. If expiry times tie, the common state is identical. Write e_V=#members with E_i=V and m_V=#members whose FINAL RUN includes V. Each of the b members has exactly one expiry, m_V<=M_*. The number of unordered pairs with intersecting final runs, even permitting first meetings before those runs, equals
    sum_V [e_V(m_V-e_V)+binom(e_V,2)] <= b(M_*-1).       (1)
The equality follows because two final runs sharing a physical state share subsequent states until the earlier expiry, and conversely any two runs present at such an expiry intersect. The desired both-moving/no-later-light pairs are a subset of these intersections. We do not equate the expression with the desired pairs, as the extra later-light cases may be included. This is a SOURCE-COHORT expiry identity, using one final run per tag for this source, not a global count of all moving intervals of a tag.

Each unordered pair contributes at most 2 c_a P/b at this source atom. By (1) total both-moving clock <=2 c_a P M_*, and summing over original positive slots and grids gives
    B_bothMov <= O(NT M_*/b_min)=O(NT B^(-1/8+o(1)))=o(NT). (2)
This pays all both-moving first meetings at arbitrary absolute depth, including recurrent returns to light horizons before first meeting. It includes all old both-GOOD pairs (GOOD implies MOVING), whether they meet in their original GOOD prefixes or later (only remaining old charges need new payment). It is not a bound on the global NUMBER of distinct pairs first meeting while both moving: the normalization is used source by source. Neither many reset episodes nor long future suffix length incurs an extra factor.

## 6. Mixed first meeting: a relative nonmoving-population test at expiry

Keep a pair without later light at which exactly one tag i is moving at first meeting. Its partner j has already expired from its own final run and stays nonmoving through S. The pair is present at E_i, with i moving there and j nonmoving there. Define, in the same fixed source cohort G,
    h_G(V)=#members of G present at V and NONMOVING there
using original history predicates. For every i, the total number of partners of this mixed type is <=h_G(E_i), independent of how many separate meetings occur along i's final run. This is a direct one-role bound; no equality to h_G is claimed because some such partners may have met before the final run, or while both were moving.

Choose eta=ell^(-1/4). Pay every surviving mixed pair with h_G(E_i)<=eta b. For each i qualifying, the number of its mixed partners is <=eta b. Each mixed unordered pair has a unique moving member at first meeting, so no ambiguity about which expiry to use. Across G at most eta b² unordered pairs; applying the two-orientation atom bound gives <=2 c_a P eta b, hence total O(eta NT)=o(NT). This is sharper than using the full source-cohort population at E_i: a large moving cohort at that state need not contribute to the number of nonmoving partners. The mixed mask uses the original induced source cohort, not all historical visitors. It is valid even if the nonmoving branch at birth was large. It does not cover every mixed pair; if h_G(E_i)>eta b the argument gives no small fraction for that moving tag.

A more precise per-source upper envelope for ALL mixed/no-later-light charge, before later masks, is
    (2 c_a P/b) sum_(i in G) h_G(E_i).
It may be intersected with the original pair-specific capacity, and on already paid small-occupancy cases restrict the sum to h_G(E_i)>eta b. This is not a globally small bound in general; an arbitrarily large nonmoving cohort can meet fresh moving arrivals. The actual exact pair weights can be smaller than the envelope.

## 7. Combined residual and implications

Apply sections 2-3 to original focal waits and source roles of exact old charges, and sections 4-6 to remaining backward fractions. Overlaps paid only once. Total extra clock cost O(NT[(M_*+m0)/min(n_min,b_min)+eta+u^1.25 M_*/B])=o(NT). All old masks (shortened band, early/late, minority, lifetime, root separation etc.) remain frozen. With new residuals B_new,D_l,new,D_h,new the conditional lower bound under finitely many twins is
    NT/2-o(NT) <= B_new+(3+o(1))(D_l,new+D_h,new).
Thus the corresponding liminf <1/2 sufficient objective is no larger than before. Necessary conditions on the remaining backward pairs now include: no projected light horizon from first meeting through source; at least one member NONMOVING at first meeting; if exactly one moving, its final expiry before or at source contains >eta b NONMOVING members of the original source cohort. BOTH members are nonmoving at source. The latter also holds for both forward roles; all original focal waits are nonmoving. All these statements apply at arbitrary history depth and all wait bands. The remaining both-nonmoving and large mixed-expiry contributions, and the forward residual total, have not been bounded by the required constant margin. No claim that the new little-o masks cause positive constant savings. Main and independent branches not modified. No Lean compilation.

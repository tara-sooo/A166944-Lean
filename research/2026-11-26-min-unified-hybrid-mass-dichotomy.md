# Integration VIII: raw sieved-cut mass, expensive-large versus short-cut dichotomy, and boosted projected cohorts

Parent [6345576](https://github.com/tara-sooo/A166944-Lean/commit/6345576f49f35e8f546aca353acf054a34a91871); latest direct [daba226](https://github.com/tara-sooo/A166944-Lean/blob/daba22699bcd9379435764fd65b67f7660fa66ab/research/2026-11-26-min-direct-growing-order-sieved-recovery.md). Import growing-order normalized local mass and L2 bounds, not the direct sparse-canonical/crossing assumptions. New paper results: every surviving positive net-level source has either expensive polynomial-label tick supply or a constant fraction of all surviving cut mass on short labels, with a much stronger projected cohort gate than the earlier P-boundary witness. Moderate subpopulation has a still larger gate and vanishing raw focal clock under finite twins. These are new upper-envelope reductions, not a strict contraction, Lean verification, or external novelty claim.

## 1. Uniform local cut mass

Keep the frozen actual endpoint domain, U, P_* and L_* of integration VII; L=floor(D/sqrt(ell)), D=eta H/log(2+N), ell=1+log u, u=log B, H=T+B, T=floor(X/u^.25), N=Theta(X/u). Source level y>D_sh, actual last upcross x with x<=b-L, density <=5 in x per tag per ordered role and <=5/max(b0,b_source) per ordered pair after the old denominator unfreezing. Preserve all valid source cuts. Both original pair members share actual future room through x+L. Let theta be ANY fixed number in (0,1), choose R largest prime with odd primorial P_R<=X^theta, S=floor(R/loglog R), V=S². R~theta log X, r=pi_o(R)~R/log R, S=o(R). Use the direct hybrid cuts J=2j: J divisible by 2P_S, middle R barrier residues, extra sieve excludes +/-1 at R<f<=V. Base density alpha, extra product Q>=1/4-o(1), smoothing length lambda=ceil(P_R^(4/11)). Direct daba226 proves, for intervals of O(X) integer coordinates,
    sum_I f_sv=Q |I|+O(X exp(-.01 r)),
    sum_I |(f_sv)_lambda-Q|²=O(X exp(-.001 r)),             (1)
where f_sv is indicator divided by alpha. This applies at fixed theta as stated there, and the errors are o(L) in mass, o(L) after Cauchy-Schwarz on intervals of length O(L). In particular any interval of length comparable to L has #(actual cuts)=alpha(Q|I|+o(L)). Over the entire O(X) horizon range the number is O(alpha X), and every actual original tag life of length <=H has O(alpha H) cuts, even if its life is short. These statements use normalized mass, not raw density lower bounds for every short gap. No full-period Parseval at P_V. alpha=X^{-o(1)} because log P_S=o(log X), r=o(log X); hence alpha lambda=X^(4theta/11-o(1)) diverges polynomially.

For a disjoint union of completed jump intervals of total gained length F and event count m inside an interval of gained length O(L), the sum of normalized cut mass therein is <=
    Q(F/2+(lambda-1)m)+o(L).                               (2)
Proof in j-coordinate: expand intervals left by lambda-1, use nonnegative smoothing to majorize raw mass, then Cauchy-Schwarz on their union inside an O(L) enclosing interval. If m is large, the union still lies inside that enclosing interval, so the same o(L) is uniform. In (2) the bound F/2+(lambda-1)m is allowed to exceed its actual union length. No factor alpha lost by converting the normalized estimate back to actual cut count.

## 2. New per-source dichotomy, independent of sparse canonical populations

Take the FULL completed events wholly inside [x,x+L], gain G=L-O(B) and actual wait W>=(G-5B)/2 by persistence. Choose A=max(V+1,ceil(lambda ell²)), so A=X^(4theta/11+o(1))<B eventually. Large means selected p>A; short means p<=A. Let F_large be actual large gain in this suffix, and m_large its count. The source is classified LARGE if F_large>=11G/12, SHORT otherwise, ties fixed. This classification is per actual source x (the two original pair members share it), not a new independent partition of tags. Both branches have a usable witness supply:

* LARGE: short waits <=3(G-F_large)<=G/4. Hence large waits >=G/4-5B/2. Removing large waits w<g/8 costs at most G/8, leaving >=G/8-5B/2>=L/10 actual occupied ticks at p>A and w>=g/8. Such ticks have r(C)>=g/16 by w<=2r. The old high-clock sieve works uniformly at polynomial labels p>A, not just p>=B/u.
* SHORT: the suffix has total normalized cut mass QG/2+o(L) by (1). Large events number <=G/(A-1), and their normalized cut mass is <=Q F_large/2 + O(G/ell²)+o(L) by (2). Therefore short events contain normalized mass >=QG/24-o(L), eventually >=QG/48. In particular at least alpha Q L/64 DISTINCT actual cuts J are crossed by R<p<=A. This counts raw cuts, not events padded by lambda. In contrast, VII gave only a gain-or-event-count alternative and no large expensive-clock branch.

If at least half of these short cuts are moderate R<p<=V, call the source MODERATE-RICH; otherwise NONMODERATE-RICH. This deterministic additional split uses the same actual cut set, with either chosen subset size >=alpha Q L/128. A moderate event has g<V<2P_S eventually and can cross at most one surviving cut, so a MODERATE-RICH source requires Omega(alpha L) DISTINCT moderate crossing events. Compared with VII's L/lambda count scale, this is a polynomial factor alpha lambda stronger, but only for this specified branch. NONMODERATE-RICH need not have many distinct events when labels exceed 2P_S.

Under finite twins, for sufficiently large X every moderate crossing is cheap w<=2S with drop>=g-4S by direct offset certificate; any S-rough moderate strict crosser instead constructs actual twins J-C±1>S. No such assumption needed for the dichotomy itself. Thus MODERATE-RICH entails Omega(alpha L) actual cheap decreasing events, not arbitrary low-label waits. Their gain lower bound Omega(alpha L R) may still be o(L), since alpha is very small. No contradiction by comparing it with the total gain alone.

## 3. Exact source transport, no extra normalization by number of partners

For each original remaining positive net-level fraction, in LARGE branch distribute uniformly among its expensive-large physical tick witnesses (j,C,p); in the other branches distribute uniformly among actual short cuts of the chosen subtype, retaining the actual crossing event (C,p,n) and J. This is an exact partition of the positive relaxed measure, not a second copy of the old future-witness route. One can keep old witness masks by priority, or intersect with this transport via product of normalized choices. If choose product, every new marginal is bounded by the unmasked new marginal; all deletion costs remain valid. The negative L_* stays frozen; never subtract L_z.

For fixed original tag and ordered role, a witness lies at gain distance <=L from source x, and total incoming density per x <=5. Denominators are witness counts >=L/10 and >=alpha Q L/128 respectively. Hence absolute marginal capacities
    tick <=50,    cut <=640/(alpha Q) = O(1/alpha).          (3)
If x lies in a partly included source event, using actual crossing slot only adds at most B to the span; constants can be enlarged. Tick uses physical occupied tick of an actual event and cut uses its unique actual crossing on that tag. Integrate over all source slabs; no slab or grid multiplier. Both pair members execute actual witness, original true birth preserved.

Apply original live-cohort log variation from integration VI to source->witness. Delete when variation>epsilon=ell^(-1/8); cost O(N(L+B)log N/epsilon)=o(NT), using total active tagged variation <=N(log N+2), source either-role density<=10. After deletion b_source/b_witness in [exp(-epsilon),exp(epsilon)]. Per ordered pair marginal bounded by constant/max(b0,b_W) at ticks and constant/(alpha max(b0,b_W)) at actual cut slots. Thus old induced two-sided root rank cut at actual true birth with threshold rho b_W, rho=ell^(-1/4), is payable O(rho NT): <=4rho b_W² bad ordered pairs per witness, sum expensive tick b_W<=3NH, and crucially
    sum_(actual physical J,C,p,n) b_W <= O(alpha NH)       (4)
by the uniform raw cut count per original tag in Section1. Rank denominator comparison is physical, not projected. Equation (4) avoids a spurious 1/alpha loss in the rank payment. Rank restrictions at source and other old witnesses remain.

## 4. New projected supply thresholds

For an actual cut J and p there are at most two prehorizons C in [J-p+2,J], one per sign, since p divides C±1 and the interval length <p. No count of actual phase multipliers is needed in a PROJECTED key (J,C,p). Let load at each such key be total retained marginal clock in level units. By (3), effective mass equal to load divided by C0/alpha (absolute C0 sufficiently large) is <= distinct original focal tags executing the key. On moderate keys p<=V, possible key count O(alpha X pi(V)); on all short nonmoderate keys <=O(alpha X pi(A)). Pay if respective effective mass <=
    m_mod=H/(u ell pi(V)),
    m_short=H/(u ell pi(A)).                              (5)
Both payments cost O(XH/(u ell))=o(NT). Same bounds if one uses either role with adjusted constant. This is a pointwise new threshold on new cut marginals, not a monotone comparison of exact old and new populations. At balanced theta=1/2, m_short = X^(9/11-o(1)), since log A~u; m_mod = X^(1-o(1)) (V~(log X)^2/(logloglog X)^2). For general fixed theta, m_short=X^(1-4theta/11-o(1)); can make its exponent arbitrarily close to 1 by choosing theta small BEFORE taking X to infinity. Compared with the old projected primorial crossing gate H/(B ell)=X^(1/2-o(1)), these are polynomially larger. The common physical pair cap is still Section3; effective mass (5) may combine different physical arrival/exit phases. We do not infer a physical cohort of that size.

In LARGE branch aggregate the expensive tick marginals to projected event (C,p). Per original tag capacity <=50 w<=100p. Uniform two-form sieve gives O(X/[p log²(2+p)]) possible C at each p>A, since r(C)>=g/16 and p<=B; log p=Theta_theta(u), and sum_(A<p<=B)1/p=O_theta(1). Define effective mass load/(C1 p) with sufficiently large absolute C1, pay if
    m_large(p)=H u/(p ell).                               (6)
Total O_theta(XH/(u ell))=o(NT). This is a new projected expensive-large event threshold, gaining factor u B/p over old H/(B ell) boundary gate in scale (different key/measure). One can retain earlier physical tick thin cuts independently. Actual p label can be large as B, so (6) is not almost macroscopic uniformly.

Root interval upper sieve implies projected keys with effective mass>m have origins spanning at least c m log(2+m) (all actual focal tags with nonzero load lie in original H-lifetime range). This yields spans >>_theta H/(ell pi(A)) or H/(ell pi(V)) for short/moderate keys, and >>_theta H u²/(p ell) for large expensive keys. These are origin span restrictions on projected mass, not true first-birth or physical synchrony. In particular root counts alone allow all three thresholds to be met.

## 5. Original-clock deletion for moderate crossing events

Independently of the positive measure, under absence of offset twins>S all actual moderate crossers have w<=2S, g>=R-1, so sum of their ORIGINAL actual waits <= (2S/(R-1)) NH=o(NT). This holds even if every tag executes many such events. All corresponding actual focal-row weights in the original joint backward/forward clock ledger can therefore be removed at o(NT) cost on every grid (original good-grid assignment has no reuse). It does not delete the transported positive record mass at those cuts, the partner column of an arbitrary source, or any forward defect destination merely because it is cheap. A moderate-rich positive source must route to witnesses that themselves carry negligible original focal clock, with projected mass at least (5). Thus the new witness set is separated from the original clock source population. The open part is to bound transport into those heavy cheap witnesses, rather than their own raw clock.

One may add the direct crossing-only corridor tail bound (10) of daba226 on the original independent boundary route, but it has N_canonical and B_cross counts, not the prime-root ensemble N. No sparsity of either count follows from finite twins. No substitution of a tag count for a per-path canonical-visit count.

## 6. Updated sufficient route

Let P_new be the surviving unmatched positive envelope after old masks and the new alternative witness transport/payments; if retaining several transports take product marginals. Total deletions o(NT). Under finitely many twins,
    NT/2-o(NT) <= U/2 +(P_new-L_*)/2 + c_a(D_l+D_h).
The strict liminf criterion remains [U+P_new-L_*+6(D_l+D_h)]/(NT)<1. New positive source supply is constrained by either polynomial large expensive events satisfying (6), or actual sieved short/moderate cuts satisfying (5) with original pair and induced rank retained at true witness. It is not a claim that these projected masses force a contradiction. The new moderate count branch is sharper than VII, but NONMODERATE-RICH and LARGE remain. Next: exploit near-macroscopic projected cohorts in short branches using phase decomposition and original true birth ranks, or bound projected expensive large gain by the old high focal sieve/lag restrictions; the latter do not automatically apply to shared backward future words (no nonzero lag). For moderate-rich use actual cut-free intervals between these cheap drops and deficit supply; don't charge their raw clock as if it were their received measure.

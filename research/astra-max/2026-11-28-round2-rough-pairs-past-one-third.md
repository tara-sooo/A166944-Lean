## Coefficient

Set \(a=.2062,\ c=.334,\ d_U=.5999,\ B(d)=2\log(d/a-1)/d\). Denote \(U=\mathbf1_{P^-\ge R^a}\), \(C=\mathbf1_{P^-\ge4R^c}\), \(E=U-C\), and put \(m=n+2\). In the identity \(C(n)C(m)=C(n)U(m)-E(m)C(n)\), the positive term counts the prime row of mass 1 and semiprime rows of mass \(dt/[t(1-t)]\) with smaller exponent \(t\in[c,1/2]\). Lower-sieve at \(a\) using \(d_0\) or \(d_2(t)-.0001\). Each contributing \(d\) has \(2<d/a<4\); the first linear-sieve coefficient \(B(d)\) increases here. The positive coefficient is bounded below by
\[
 P=B(d_0)+\int_c^{1/2}B(\max\{d_0,d_2(t)-.0001\})\,\frac{dt}{t(1-t)}.
\]
For the subtracted term, with chosen level exponent \(d\), majorize roughness of the other neighbor by sifting at \(\min(4R^c,R^{d/2.1})\). Its sieve parameter is in \([2.1,3)\), respecting \(z\le Q^{1/2}\) in Wu Lemma 2.2 and giving \(2/d\). For \(t\le1/3\) use \(d_U\); for \(1/3<t<c+O(1/L)\) use \(d_0\). The latter thin slice can only contain semiprimes apart from a boundary of vanishing mass. Both upper coefficients are on the \(2/d\) plateau. Writing \(M(s)=\omega(1/s)/s\), the resulting upper coefficient is
\[
 V=\frac2{d_U}[M(a)-M(c)]+\left(\frac2{d_0}-\frac2{d_U}\right)\log\frac{2c}{1-c}.
\]
Here \(M(a)\le.561522/a\) by Wu Lemma 2.10, and \(M(c)=1+\log((1-c)/c)\) exactly. The limit permits the fixed multiplier 4 in the roughness.

The rational certificate partitions \([c,1/2]\) into 160 cells \([l,r]\), taking \(d=\max\{d_0,\min((2+3l)/5,1-r)-.0001\}\) throughout each, with exact mass \(\log[r(1-l)/(l(1-r))]\). Positive series plus rational tail bounds enclose every logarithm. It gives \(P-V\ge .005684586\), before arbitrarily small fixed linear-sieve construction errors and \(o(1)\); choose the approximation parameters and then \(R\) for the stated .005. ## Where the single exchange stops

Even assigning the ideal \(d=5/8\) to every row on both sides, the uniform one-intermediate-threshold coefficient is nonpositive at \(c=2/5\) throughout its first-plateau regime \(2\le s=d/a\le4\). Indeed \(M(c)=1+\log(3/2)\) and \(M(a)\ge1/(2a)\). The latter follows from \(\omega(u)\ge1/2\) on \([1,2]\), then interval induction from \((u\omega(u))'=\omega(u-1)\). For \(s\ge2\), \((1+\log(s-1))/s\le.568\): maximize \(1+\log y-.568(1+y)\) at \(y=1/.568\). Hence \(2d M(c)(1+\log(s-1))/s<.999\). This specifically rules out a positive coefficient in this formula even at that ideal level. A surplus from more information on the rejected pairs or a dynamical gain is needed at the requested .4 scale.

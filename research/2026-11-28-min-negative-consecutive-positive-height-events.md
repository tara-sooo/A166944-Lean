## 1. Main theorem and input

Write L=log R, theta1=.2855, theta2=.2856. For any fixed 0<epsilon<1 there are fixed positive constants c1, delta, a (delta may be extremely small) such that every sufficiently large slab [R,2R] contains at least a R/L^(2+epsilon) distinct canonical roots A=pv-1, p,v prime, R^theta1<=p<=R^theta2, whose first three completed events have labels
    p, q, 3,
where K=floor(c1 log L), K^delta<=q<K, and heights
    h1=p,
    h2=p+u-(q-1)/2 >= p+(q+1)/2,   q<=u<=2q,
    h3=h2+w-1 >= h2+1,              2<=w<=6.
All four visited centers including the birth can be taken non-twin, and all physical state sets through those three events pairwise disjoint across retained roots. Completion is by original age (3p-7)/2+2K+6 <=2R^.2856. In particular the actual height increment after the first event survives TWO further completed events, and each has positive net height change. It is an additive unbounded polyloglog increment, not an improvement of the polynomial exponent. No claim of indefinite iteration, common absolute observation retirement, or near-square-band canonical firing is made.

Use the uniform per-box lower from c20b70c and 1382ffc. In disjoint boxes P<p<=1.2P, 1.1R/P<v<=1.4R/P with dyadic P>=R^theta1,2P<=R^theta2, let B=12 prod_{5<=ell<K prime} ell, K=floor(c1 log L) so B<=L^epsilon for sufficiently small c1. For ANY reduced v0 modulo B with v0=7 mod12 and v0=1 mod5,7, we have at least
    a0 N0,  N0=R/(phi(B)L^3)                            (1)
prime pairs in this box with v=v0 modB and P^-(pv-2)>=4R^theta2. Here a0>0 is fixed independent of v0 and B in this range (may depend on the fixed sieve exponents). Uniformity follows by using Wu 2004 Lemma2.5 with the unrestricted smaller prime beta and arbitrary bounded alpha on larger primes in a log-power AP, SW for its unsifted main, same ordinary Iwaniec well-factorable lower at D=R^.5713, same fixed positive f(s) and coprimality correction as c20b70c. ## 2. Local residue plans with long completed waits for both signs

Choose a fixed large harmonic mass S>0 later, then a sufficiently large fixed Z0>=11, and put Q=6 prod_{5<=ell<=Z0 prime} ell. Let delta=exp(-phi(Q) S) in (0,1). The selected prime set J_K consists of K^delta<=q<K with
    q=5 mod6, q=1 mod each prime 5<=ell<=Z0.
For large R these q exceed Z0. This is one fixed reduced AP modulo Q. Fixed-AP PNT and partial summation give
    sum_{q in J_K} 1/q -> log(1/delta)/phi(Q)=S.           (2)
S,Z0,Q,delta are all fixed before R tends to infinity. The tiny delta is an existence parameter, not a practical numerical threshold.

For each q in J_K independently select an even integer U in [q+2,2q] with U not=4 mod6, and define
    r = -U - 1/2 mod q,   V = 1-r^(-1) mod q,
    Uminus = 3q+2-U.
Omit the at most four choices where r=0,1,3,-1 modq. There remain >>q choices, with absolute uniform constant, and distinct U give distinct r and V (interval length <q). V is reduced and not1. Uminus is odd and in [q,2q]. At every nonselected prime ell<B's cutoff impose v0=1 modell (and v0=7 mod12). At q in J_K impose v0=V modq with the chosen U. Each choice vector yields a reduced class mod B to which (1) applies.

For a given p, the plus neighbor of x0 is divisible by q iff p=r modq, and the minus neighbor iff p=-r modq. These two residues are distinct and neither is the excluded source residue 2/V modq: at p=r the source pv-2=r-3 modq, at p=-r it is -r-1 modq. For a match the exact first positive eligible wait after (x0,n0) is respectively U or Uminus: the height/clock condition is q|2p+2u+1-2b_s (b_+=0,b_-=3), and u even for plus, odd for minus. The displayed U and Uminus satisfy it in (0,2q], hence are the unique least positive waits with that parity. Every nonselected prime <K is excluded from both x0 neighbors by v0=1, including 3 (and 2 by parity). Thus if there is at least one selected match and NO eligible q'>=K in the first 2K ticks while at x0, the actual second event must be one of the selected primes, with its long prescribed wait q<=u<=2q. Competing selected primes are harmless because every one's prescribed wait has the positive-height property. The true minimum rule chooses the earliest eligible tick, then smallest prime.

## 3. Uniform sieve majorant and remove no-match roots

For a fixed choice vector, let F be the fraction of reduced p classes modulo B satisfying pv0-2 nonzero at all odd prime factors of B. It is independent of the vector:
    F=prod_{3<=ell<K prime}(1-1/(ell-1)) ~ c/log K,
    (B/phi(B)) F bounded above and below by positive absolute constants.
Here at 3 one of two reduced classes is forbidden, at 2 none; the statements follow from Mertens and convergent O(ell^-2) corrections. For any subset of these allowed p classes of relative fraction f among ALL reduced classes modulo B, the number of prime p in a box is <=C P f/L, uniformly, by elementary one-form upper in a progression (or SW at B). If also a specified reduced class modulo a prime t outside B, t<=R^.05, the count is <=C P f/(t L): sum the elementary progression upper over the chosen B classes. The parameter lengths P/(B t) are polynomial in R, and the bad-prime factors are at most modulus/phi(modulus), hence constants uniform. If the prescribed t-class is zero, there are no such prime p since t<P.

For each p, the number of prime v in its class B and with pv-2 rough is <=C X/(B L²)*(B/phi(B))², X=R/P. With a further single v residue modulo prime t outside B, t!=p, t<=O(P), it is <=C X/(B t L²)*(B/phi(B))². These are the dimension-two elementary Selberg upper as 1382ffc: v and pv-2 distinct affine forms; good primes exclude B,p,t and 2, parameter interval length >>R^(1-2theta2)/polylog, and actual omitted factors <=C(B/phi(B))², since the factor from any one additional t>=K is bounded. If a class forces a prime form divisible by a small prime it may simply be empty. Combining with the p upper, for an allowed p subset of relative fraction f and no outside t, the bound is
    C N0 (B/phi(B)) f.
With a reduced p residue and a v residue modulo an outside prime t<=R^.05 the bound is C N0 (B/phi(B)) f/t². If one selected match of specified sign at q is required, f=F/(q-2), as q-2 reduced p classes are allowed at q. Two specified residues at distinct primes in B each add the appropriate reciprocal (ell-2) among allowed classes, unless the conditions are inconsistent. These are deterministic counts, not independence assumptions about the sieved sequence.

No selected match leaves exactly fraction f=F prod_{q in J_K}(1-2/(q-2)) among reduced p classes. The preceding bound is <= C N0 exp(-2 sum 1/q), with absolute constant independent S,Z0. Choose S large enough that this is <=a0 N0/8 for sufficiently large R, using (2). Choose Z0 later independently of R; increasing Z0 only changes delta and the AP, not the fixed limiting harmonic mass S. This removes the no-match roots without using a lower distribution theorem on restricted p.

## 4. Exclude competing labels outside the small modulus before the second event

Any eligible outside label t>=K before elapsed 2K at x0 obeys
    t|2p+2u+1-2b_s,   v=1-s/p modt,   t!=p.
For K<=t<=R^.05 the height pin fixes p in a single class modt. Use section3 with f=F and both outside residue conditions. For fixed t,u,sign the bound is O(N0/t²). Summing u<=2K and t>=K gives O(N0 K/(K log K))=o(N0), using the elementary prime reciprocal-square estimate from dyadic Chebyshev. For t>R^.05, each fixed p,u,sign has O(1) possible prime divisors of the positive integer O(P), each reciprocal <=R^(-.05). The v upper and p count with f=F yield O(N0 K R^(-.05))=o(N0). Here t<=O(P) from its divisor pin, so the progression length for v is still polynomial. This holds uniformly over choice vectors. Remove these exceptional roots (we excluded even potential eligible labels, not only actual winners). All remaining matched roots have the asserted second event q and h2>=p+(q+1)/2.

## 5. Force the third event to be 3 and strictly increase height again

After the selected q event, the position y=x0-(q-1) satisfies y=2 mod3, since q=2 mod3 and x0=0 mod3. Thus 3 is eligible at the next clock multiple of 6 within w<=6 ticks. By n0=4 mod6, our U choice (even and not4 mod6) and Uminus=3q+2-U (odd), n0+u is never 5 mod6. Consequently the next 3-clock wait w is in {2,3,5,6}, so if 3 is the next actual winner the third event's increment is w-1>=1.

We exclude all competing t>=5 that could be eligible while at y in the first six ticks after the second event (including the final clock, although at the 3-clock the winner would still be 3). Overcount by summing every possible selected first match q and sign, whether or not it was earliest. Write g=q-1 and u the corresponding deterministic U or Uminus, with 1<=z<=6. Any such candidate satisfies
    t|2p+2(u+z)-g+1-2b_s,    p(v-1)-g+s=0 modt.          (3)
t=p impossible by g even and 0<=g<p-1. t=q impossible because the arrival after firing q has spatial residue 0 or2 modq, not +/-1 for q>=11. The fixed primes 5<=t<=Z0 are not selected and v=1 modt; q=1 modt implies y=0 modt, so none divide y±1. We bound all other possible competitors in three groups.

(a) Outside B, t>=K. For fixed q, its sign and selected plan, u is fixed and requiring the match costs f=F/(q-2) in section3. For t<=R^.05, the two conditions (3) impose one p class and one v class modt, as before, giving O(N0/[(q-2)t²]) per z,sign. For t>R^.05 use the O(1) divisors of (3) for each p and v upper. Summing over q and signs using (2), and z<=6, gives O(N0 (S+1)[1/(K log K)+R^(-.05)])=o(N0).

(b) Selected t in J_K distinct from q. Average over all independent uniform choices of the plans at the selected primes. Conditional on the plan at q, (3)'s time condition fixes a unique p residue modulo t independent of the plan at t. If it is zero there is no prime p. Otherwise its spatial condition fixes one value V_t=1+(g-s)/p modt. The distinct available V_t have cardinality >>t, hence this is compatible with probability O(1/t) over the choice at t. For a compatible choice vector the p fraction relative to all reduced classes is at most F/[(q-2)(t-2)], and the deterministic upper from section3 is <=C N0/[(q-2)(t-2)]. Thus expected count per q,t,z and signs is <=C N0/[(q-2)t(t-2)]. Sum using (2) and t>=K^delta: O(N0(S+1) K^(-delta))=o(N0). This averaging is only over freely chosen CRT progression classes; no assertion of independence of arithmetic events on the actual orbit. We did not condition on q being first, only on its necessary match.

(c) Nonselected Z0<t<K. With v=1 modt, spatial eligibility implies t|q or t|q-2, so t|q-2 after excluding t=q. Height condition fixes p in at most one class at t. For fixed q,t,z,sign the count is <=C N0/[(q-2)(t-2)]. We need the uniform shifted-prime harmonic bound
    sum_{q in J_K} (1/q) sum_{t>Z0 prime, t|q-2} 1/t
       <= C (S+1)/Z0 + o(1).                            (4)
To see this, split selected q into dyadic bands [Y,2Y]. For t>sqrt Y, each q-2 has O(1) such divisors and reciprocal weight O(Y^-1/2), negligible in the harmonic sum since K^delta tends to infinity. For Z0<t<=sqrt Y, t coprime Q, count q in the fixed class modQ and class 2 modt using the elementary prime upper in progression Qt. Since Q fixed and Y>=K^delta, parameter Y/(Qt)>=Y^.4 eventually; uniform absolute constant gives <=C Y/(phi(Q)(t-1) log Y). Divide by Y to bound q reciprocals, and sum bands: sum 1/log Y <= C log(1/delta)+o(1), and log(1/delta)/phi(Q)=S. The t weights sum <=C/Z0. This establishes (4) with constant independent Z0 and delta (once R sufficiently large depending on them). Consequently this nonselected competitor count is <=C N0(S+1)/Z0+o(N0) for every choice vector. Choose Z0 sufficiently large after S that it is <=a0 N0/8 for large R. Then Q and delta are fixed by section2.

Combining sections3–5: uniformly per box the lower (1) is >=a0 N0 for every choice vector. No-match, first outside competitor and third nonselected/outside competitor total are <=a0 N0/4+o(N0) (constants chosen above). The remaining selected-third competitor average is o(N0). Thus average GOOD count over all vectors is >=a0 N0/2 for all sufficiently large R. Sum boxes; choose at least one common vector attaining this total (or sum disjoint progression classes, an even stronger count). At least >>R/[phi(B)L²] distinct actual roots survive. Disjoint p boxes and uniqueness of smaller prime factor of pv prevent repetition. No individual lower for any prescribed p residue was assumed. Every survivor actually fires 3 next: there is no other eligible label before the certified 3-clock, and minimum label is 3 at that clock. Height increases by w-1. This proves the three-event pattern and height lower. With K~c1 log L and fixed delta, q>=K^delta >> (log L)^delta. Alternatively take K=floor(log L/loglog L) (as R tends to infinity); then B=L^o(1), lower R/L^(2+o(1)) and q>=(log L)^(delta-o(1)); proof unchanged with e.g. fixed B<=L^.5. No effective threshold for these asymptotic constants is claimed.

## 6. Full non-twin and physical separation through the three events

The birth is non-twin (pv composite), x0 has the small selected factor q and y has factor3. The final center is x0-(q+1). To ensure its roughness is low as well, exclude every root with an even g in [0,K+1] and qmin(x0-g)>=R^.1. For each fixed p,g, four affine forms in v
    v, pv-2, p(v-1)-g-1, p(v-1)-g+1
are all R^.1-rough, have nonzero pairwise determinants (the undivided constants 0,-2,-p-g-1,-p-g+1 are distinct) and polynomial coefficients. The earlier uniform elementary four-form upper yields O(R(log L)^4/(pL^4)). Sum p reciprocal O(1) and O(K+1) offsets: O(R(K+1)(log L)^4/L^4)=o(R/L^(2+epsilon)). This also rules out any postarrival center matching an initial source in the retained family, since all sources are R^theta1-rough. It does not assert the next fourth event is small or positive.

Postarrival duration <=2K+6, displacement <=K+1. Distinct HIGH anchors with any shared physical state in those intervals require |dx0|<=K+1, |dn0|<=2K+6, hence |p-p'|=O(K+1); equal p would imply same root (x0 and x0' multiples of p). For fixed p,p',offset, CRT yields O(R/p²) sites; count possible p' by O(K+1), sum offsets and all integer p>=R^theta1. Total O(R^(1-theta1)(K+1)^2)=o(R/L^(2+epsilon)). Delete both endpoints of every such pair. Birth waiting states occur at distinct source positions and cannot meet the postarrival centers just excluded. Thus entire canonical prefixes through third landing have pairwise disjoint physical states. In particular the post-third state has a canonical ancestor in the exact reverse recurrence c20b70c, with age and height verified constructively; no assumed reachability of high local ports.

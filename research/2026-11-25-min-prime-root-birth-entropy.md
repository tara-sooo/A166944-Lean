## 1. Original physical DAG and hierarchical entropy

Keep X dyadic, B=ceil(sqrt(7X)), u=log B, ell=1+log u, N=Theta(X/u), T=floor(X/u^.25), H=T+B, F=T²Bu, L_H=B u^1.5. Original prime-start, gain-stopped MIN paths, with actual terminal snapshots and source-ordered continuation, remain unchanged. A physical vertex v has actual original visiting cohort A_v, size n_v; its completed continuing cohort is the largest-source suffix of size b_v. Every noninitial vertex has its actual incoming cohorts partitioned by the exact predecessor physical key w; denote their sizes a_w=b_w. Original prime-root physical injection is a singleton with no incoming edge. Every original source exits once. The DAG has unique completed successor wherever b_v>0.

At a noninitial v, partition the incoming cohorts first by their selected incoming prime p. Write n_p=sum_(w with selected p) a_w. Define Psi(n)=n log n (Psi(0)=0). The increase
    Delta(v)=Psi(n_v)-sum_(w->v) Psi(b_w)
is nonnegative and splits exactly into
    Delta_mix(v)=Psi(n_v)-sum_p Psi(n_p),
    Delta_same(v)=sum_p Psi(n_p)-sum_(w->v) Psi(a_w).
Both are nonnegative. At singleton injection take all three increases zero. Since every arc is counted once, the global budget is
    sum_v Delta(v)=sum_v [Psi(n_v)-Psi(b_v)]
                  <= N(1+log N).
The inequality follows from the derivative of Psi and sum_v(n_v-b_v)=N. It handles arbitrary original terminal losses, not just a lossless merger tree.

For each visiting tag M at v, with incoming label p and predecessor w, define two per-tag entropies
    g_mix(M,v)=log(n_v/n_p),
    g_same(M,v)=log(n_p/a_w).
The sums over actual tags are Delta_mix and Delta_same respectively. Thus their joint global sum is <=N(1+log N). This identity differs from the one-sided descending-rank increment of 39398362: it is available at BOTH endpoints of a first physical meeting, irrespective of which source is smaller, and pays different-label versus same-label births without counting both partition entropies twice.

If two tags first meet at v, their actual predecessors differ. In the mixed-label case p!=q, at v=(t,C), s=t mod2, d=2t-C, the original incoming phase gives pq | C+2s and 2pq | d-6s. In the same-label case p=q, their prehorizon is the same A=C-p+1, but predecessor arrival ticks a<b are distinct. They exit at the same t, with 0<b-a<2r(A) and both original waits positive. This is the true waiting birth, not two coincident physical predecessor keys. These conditions continue to apply if one predecessor is an original prime root. No fictitious incoming label at that root is needed.

For completeness, for fixed same-label birth v and outgoing p, every actual predecessor arrival lies in an interval of length <=2r(A) immediately below t. A nonroot predecessor incoming prime q must divide A or A+2 with the corresponding parity, and its arrival clock is one residue mod 2q. Hence for each admissible q and side there are <=1+floor(r(A)/q) possible distinct predecessor ticks; at most one additional prime-root predecessor key. For q>=r(A)/Z this gives O((1+Z)u/ell) possible predecessor slots by the distinct prime divisor bound, but not a bound on their actual source masses. If two predecessor arrivals have the same incoming q and same parity, their lag is a positive multiple of 2q; in particular q<r(A). The proof here uses the actual label/slot partition and keeps all possible smaller q rather than assuming they are rare.

## 2. Exact natural first-birth rectangle with ordered stops

At fixed physical first-meeting candidate v let the full original source fibre be sorted increasingly. Its largest source's deterministic suffix contains every smaller tag's suffix until its actual terminal vertex. A natural high event has h=r for original completed p>=Q, r>p/sqrt(log p), otherwise zero (including actual terminal snapshots). Nonanalytic diagnostic uses its inherited criterion instead.

At any later actual physical vertex x along the largest suffix, let A(x) be the original tags from A_v that still have a COMPLETED outgoing event at x. It is a largest-source suffix of A_v, possibly empty. Write n(x), n_p(x), a_w(x) for its full, original incoming-label, and original predecessor-cohort sizes, with the labels/keys frozen at v. A tag that has stopped at x is not counted even if a larger tag has a completed event there. The exact number of unordered pairs first meeting at v, still sharing the completed event at x, is
    P_mix(x)=choose(n(x),2)-sum_p choose(n_p(x),2),
    P_same(x)=sum_p choose(n_p(x),2)-sum_w choose(a_w(x),2).
Indeed first birth at v means distinct predecessor keys; all such original pairs share the smaller tag's recorded suffix. If the largest source's event is natural high, any other completed tag at the same state has the same natural h. Thus for any fixed set J of common suffix hit indices, the full natural ordered energy from those first births is exactly
    E_type(v,J)=2 sum_(x in J) h(x) P_type(x).             (1)
Equivalently sum over distinct predecessor source pairs of twice the natural high suffix mass of the smaller tag on J. This remains exact for a birth residence J defined by a fixed band and consecutive above-level run from v, with ordered terminal clipping. It is an upper envelope for arbitrary retained pair-specific weights, not equality on a residual with holes. No repeated counting of pairs that already shared a predecessor.

## 3. Two-sided weighted birth-residence prefixes

Use the deficit bands and last-entry decomposition of 39398362: D_j=2^j D_0, D_0=ceil(B/sqrt u) (min8); hit band I_j=[2D_j,4D_j), real lambda in (D_j,2D_j). For a pair e first meeting at v, its birth residence is the maximal initial consecutive above-lambda segment after v, empty if d_v<=lambda. Actual retained ordered mass W_e at band hits in that segment is integrated d lambda/D_j. Later ascent-origin runs, low hits and async are separate.

For each original tag M at v, let rho_M(v,j,lambda) be twice the full original natural high band-hit mass in its above-lambda birth run. By source ordering the sequence rho_M is nondecreasing in source, even for different terminal positions. It is computed using actual full natural suffix, not the paid ledger. For either type, each pair's actual birth residence weight W_e satisfies
    0<=W_e<=min(rho_M,rho_M').
The same full-fibre min envelope also gives (1). For a fixed focal M, its opposite population consists of all tags with different incoming label in the mixed case, or with the same label but a different actual predecessor key in the same-label case. It includes original tags removed by previous masks.

Set theta_j=(j+1)^(-2), beta_j=(H L_H/u) theta_j. At fixed v,j,lambda and focal tag M of type tau, assign budget b_tau(M)=beta_j g_tau(M,v). Order the full opposite population by increasing source. Its possible pair masses min(rho_M,rho_M') are nondecreasing. Admit the longest initial prefix with sum of these natural envelopes <=b_tau(M). If an envelope is zero it costs zero. There is no minimum prefix length. Alternatively split the opposite population into its distinct occupied label cohorts (mixed) or predecessor-key cohorts (same), use the same prefix in each with equal budget share b_tau(M)/number of cohorts. This second rank family also has total envelope <=b_tau(M). A pair is eligible for a whole-layer payment if either of these two rank families admits it in EITHER orientation. Charge the actual W_e to one admitting endpoint, once. At fixed v,j,lambda the sum is <=2 sum_(M,tau) b_tau(M); integrate over a level interval of length D_j with normalization 1/D_j. Summing all births and bands gives
    E_prefix <= 2(H L_H/u) (sum_j theta_j) sum_v Delta(v)
             = O(N H L_H)=O(F/u^.25).                   (2)
No factor for the number of physical states, label choices, predecessor slots or terminal cutoffs. The proof only uses natural upper envelopes and the actual source entropy. It does not depend on where previous quota payments were made.

In particular both ends of a surviving birth layer fail the two prefixes. If the full opposite population's natural envelope sum is <=its focal entropy budget, the entire focal incidence is paid. Compared with the preceding one-sided rank cap, small local residence or membership in a smaller incoming branch increases the accessible opposite population; the actual natural prefix sum, rather than the last partner's envelope times a fixed rank, determines admission.

## 4. Actual weighted birth core and remaining mass inequalities

After the prefix payments freeze the remaining actual nonnegative weights at this same v,j,lambda separately for the two incoming-label types. On the simple graph of source tags, with an edge for each actual positive remaining birth residence, repeatedly remove all incident edges at any tag of positive current weighted degree <=b_tau(M), assigning their mass to that tag. The entropy budget for this extra pass is separate from (2). Every tag can pay at most once, so total after layer integration is
    E_core <= (H L_H/u) (sum_j theta_j) sum_v Delta(v)
            = O(F/u^.25).                              (3)
The terminal support is the greatest admissible subset in which every incident weighted degree exceeds its own budget, independent of deletion order. This is the earlier weighted peeling theorem on actual layer weights, not a reference-population test.

For any remaining tag M in an actual nonempty type core, let q_M be its distinct positive-weight opposite partner count. Then
    sum_(actual remaining M') W_(M,M') > beta_j g_tau(M,v),
    q_M rho_M > beta_j g_tau(M,v).
For same-label births the full opposite population count is <=n_p-a_w, whence a necessary condition is
    (n_p-a_w) rho_M > beta_j log(n_p/a_w),
    n_p rho_M > beta_j.
The first inequality is stronger for a very small incoming cohort. The second uses log(n_p/a_w)>=(n_p-a_w)/n_p. Both endpoints satisfy the inequality with THEIR OWN incoming branch size, not only the smaller source. For mixed-label births the analogous necessary conditions are
    (n_v-n_p) rho_M > beta_j log(n_v/n_p),
    n_v rho_M > beta_j.
These are actual cross-label versus within-label local mass restrictions, using the true waiting slots in the latter case. They do not supply a general upper bound on the natural envelope in (1). In particular the inequalities may allow large inherited incoming fibres with comparable sizes and long natural residences. The full sum of the two prefix and core costs is still O(F/u^.25). Only the assigned birth portion is removed; no claim about later ascent portions or whole-pair removal in general. No new actual medium bound.

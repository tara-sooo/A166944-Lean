## 2. A much shorter high-focal pursuit

Take Y=B^(1/10), L_h=ceil(B u^4). Then B+P_max=o(L_h/ell), L_h<Lambda_old eventually. Class expensive-high iff p>=Y and w>=g/ell. Pay cheap high waits O(NH/ell). In each original path partition prehorizons into ordinary blocks of length floor(L_h/2). Pay the terminal part of expensive-high events in each block having strictly later expensive-high wait within the block less than L_h/ell. Cost per block <=L_h/ell+3B and O(H/L_h+1) blocks per tag, so total O(NT/ell). These are additional masks, may keep the previous longer-window masks as well. Every remaining high focal event has future high wait >=L_h/ell in its block. At a good right witness the posthorizon is at most C_e+2P_max+B; lost later expensive-high waits before or at that departure are <=3(2P_max+B). The rest occur in the prefix after J ending on first completed gain crossing L_h (prehorzions <J+L_h). Hence its expensive-high wait >=L_h/(2ell). Deterministic continuation means the same word for all stable source tags at that slot.

Local support: for fixed p>=Y and arbitrary interval I of length L_h+B+O(1), necessary p|(C+sigma) and r(C)>=g/(2ell) for expensive-high. Write C=p h-sigma and sieve h, p h-2sigma, with odd sieve primes <=
    z_p=floor(min(p^(1/40),(L_h/p)^(1/40))).
These primes are below p and g/(2ell), and forms have two distinct roots. In a full containing parameter interval of length O(L_h/p+1), dimension-two Selberg upper sieve as 8008fbc gives O((L_h/p)/log²(2+z_p) + z_p^8). Uniformly log z_p >> log u because p>=B^.1 and L_h/p>=u^4, and remainder z_p^8 <= (L_h/p)^.2 is absorbed by (L_h/p)/ell². Therefore support O(L_h/(p ell²)). This is a weaker density than the old u^-2 but is sufficient at this shorter scale. One fixed p supplies expensive-high wait at most O(L_h/ell²), by w<=2p and distinct prehorizons along one path. If a complete translated prefix with nonzero |Delta|<=4H exists, all selected labels divide Delta/2. At most log(4H)/log Y=20+o(1) distinct labels >=Y, so its expensive-high wait O(L_h/ell²) contradicts L_h/(2ell). Equivalently focal prefix radical >4H. Thus all eligible stable far partners at good witnesses of every retained high focal event have an actual first defect at common prehorizon E with 0<=E-J<L_h. No extra partner mask. Shortening the window costs O(NT/ell) and does not enlarge the massive cutoff.

## 3. Low-focal pursuit by grid, with a vanishing discarded fraction

For p_e<Y, every good grid is nonself and q<=P_j/a. The moderate cutoff is H/(256 P_max u ell³), hence b_v/n_c <= A q/(128 P_max ell). Put f=ell^(-1/4), k=ceil(f M), keep low-focal contributions only on grids j<=j_*:=M-1-k (eventually nonempty). This is only a loss of usable witness scales, not a separate clock payment. Each low focal event still has >=(1-epsilon-k/M)M usable good grids. Set
    L_j=ceil(H ell³ P_j/P_max)   for j<=j_*.
For all these grids L_j>>B and max L_j < Lambda_old eventually: L_j>=H ell³ P_min/P_max >> B, while P_j*/P_max <=(O(1)/u³)^k <= exp(-c u f). Thus L_j*/Lambda_old <= O(u ell^9 exp(-c u f))=o(1), indeed smaller than every fixed negative power of u. The radical persistence bound with this pursuit length (>L_j) yields
    f_persistent/n_c <= (8H/L_j) b_v/n_c
                     <= A q/(16 ell^4 P_j)
                     <= A/(16 a ell^4)=o(ell^-2).
So the defect-bearing fraction is >=3/(8ell)-o(ell^-2) at every usable good low witness. All horizons use the same original source room and same first-defect rule as before. The average low capacity scale is
    Lbar_l = (1/M) sum_(j<=j_*) (L_j+B+P_j)
           = O(L_j*/M+B).
The equality uses geometric separation; L_j*/M>>B eventually. This is an improvement by a superpolynomial-in-u factor over Lambda_old, plus the geometric M factor. It is not claimed that all low-focal pursuit lengths are B polylog. Keeping almost all grids (rather than only the bottom half) preserves the asymptotic coefficient and the old common averaging measure. This choice of f is convenient, not optimized.

## 4. Sharp source capacity and fractional common pair measure

Previously a whole right cell was bounded by 9P per tag-atom. We use ONLY retained moderate focal events for which the grid is good and the corresponding short-horizon result holds. At one crossing on a fixed tag let n_k be the number of crossed atoms. If q<=P/a, n_k=1 and cell gain <=P+q-1. Its entire wait <=3(P+q)<=3(1+1/a)P. If q>=aP, the good events assigned to this right witness consist only of the crossing itself, since a good nonself witness must be small. Its wait <=3g, g<=(n_k+1)P, n_k>=a-2, so w<=3(1+2/a)P n_k for a>=4. Middle witnesses have no good events. Thus in ALL cases the total good moderate wait assigned to this witness is <=c_a P n_k, c_a=3(1+2/a)=3+o(1). This sharper estimate would not apply to an arbitrary raw crossing cell.

Let W_l(tag,k,P) and W_h(tag,k,P) be the actual retained moderate good low/high wait assigned to this witness, counting low only on j<=j_*. They depend on the original focal tag's incoming path and masks; no assertion that all members of a source slot have equal pasts. Freeze fractions rho_l=W_l/(c_a P n_k), rho_h=W_h/(c_a P n_k). They are nonnegative with sum<=1, defined as zero when no good focal wait is assigned. For every original ordered source pair-incidence in E_P^o, split its P/n_c charge into two fractional pieces rho_l P/n_c and rho_h P/n_c (using its focal tag's fractions). Route a piece to the SAME first actual defect chosen by the old rule, only if before reaching its appropriate pursuit distance L_j or L_h; otherwise omit that piece. Do not create a new first defect after any mask. When both pieces route, they arrive at the same original event. All pair row/column coefficients in each sector are <=1 since rho<=1; their sum also <=1 at each original atom. Thus at each original tagged destination and each projected destination,
    D_l,P + D_h,P <= D_P^old
pointwise, even before averaging. The comparison would fail in general if one independently counted the full old pair weight in both sectors or independently normalized grid averages on different subfamilies. We deliberately use denominator M for BOTH.

For any positive rho_l or rho_h the source tag is eligible in E_P^o because the corresponding moderate focal event has a good witness and long entropy stability. Its stable defect-bearing partner fraction for the corresponding short length is >=alpha_l=3/(8ell)-o(ell^-2) or alpha_h=3/(8ell). Multiplying by the frozen fractions, then summing n_k source atoms, gives routed mass >=alpha_s W_s/c_a (s=l,h). This establishes per-grid whole-clock domination without an additional application of the old 9P bound. Average over all grids: each retained moderate low event is counted on >=(1-epsilon-k/M)M grids; each high on >=(1-epsilon)M. Therefore
    W_mod,l <= c_a/[alpha_l(1-epsilon-k/M)] sum Dbar_l,
    W_mod,h <= c_a/[alpha_h(1-epsilon)] sum Dbar_h.
Combining,
    W_mod <= (8+o(1)) ell sum_(C,p)(Dbar_l+Dbar_h).       (1)
The leading coefficient is 8, not 24. This is not a claim that all original raw clocks satisfy the sharper bound, only those with the previous good-grid geometry and added masks. Since both source fractions sum<=1 and lengths<=Lambda_old, their averaged sum is pointwise <= Dbar_old. Even without shortening lengths, the same fractional good-cell argument would improve the old coefficient; shortening is needed for the new capacities.

## 5. Two stronger destination caps

At first defect at prehorizon E, source boundary K belongs to [E-L_s-B,E], with L_s=L_j or L_h. The row/column interval argument of aa04b0 applies with these new lengths: for each original destination event per tag and grid the routed sector charge <=2(L_s+B+P_j). Average before taking projected group populations. Therefore
    Dbar_l(C,p)<=2 Lbar_l n(C,p),
    Dbar_h(C,p)<=2 Lbar_h n(C,p),
where Lbar_h=L_h+B+P_max=Theta(Bu^4); using P_max rather than its mean is a valid upper envelope. No claim of true synchronous mass. Each destination p>y0 as before. Define m_eff,s=Dbar_s/(2 Lbar_s). Use graded support h(r)=log²(2+r)/(1+log log(2+r))² from aa04b0: sum h(r) over eligible prehorizons at fixed p is O(X/p). Pay sector s when
    m_eff,s <= H h(r)/(Lbar_s u ell³).
Cost O(NT/ell²) per sector, using reciprocal prime sum O(ell). On p>=sqrt P_min replace ell³ by ell², and for rough r>=g/log(2+p) replace h(r) by log²(2+p), as in aa04b0. These are effective mass caps at the corresponding capacities. Compared at fixed p,r to the OLD Lambda_old-scale cap, the high cap increases by Theta(Lambda_old/(Bu^4))=Theta(B/(u^(21/4)ell^6)), the low cap by Omega(Lambda_old M/L_j*) (superpolynomial in u). These compare normalized effective masses with different denominators, not actual cohort sizes. Indeed absolute charge cutoffs 2 Lbar_s m_eff,s are the same as previously at each p,r. The new capacity gives more information when expressing the remaining effective mass in terms of actual tag populations. No claim that the larger normalized cutoff alone increases the absolute charge budget.

For strict comparison of residual criteria apply the old paid destination key mask to both new sector charges first. Its total old payment O(NT/ell²) bounds their combined payment pointwise. Apply the new sector-specific caps next, at their separately proved O(NT/ell²) budgets. The residual new combined measure is pointwise <= old residual. Let D_l,rem,D_h,rem denote their totals. Let W_massive,rem be the old massive clock after additional masks. Under finitely many twins, (1) and all o(NT) payments imply
    NT/2-o(NT) <= W_massive,rem + (8+o(1))ell(D_l,rem+D_h,rem).
Sufficient condition:
    liminf_(dyadic X) [W_massive,rem + 8 ell(D_l,rem+D_h,rem)]/(NT)<1/2.   (2)
As usual the o(1) is harmless on any subsequence of bounded normalized objective with strict margin. The new sufficient condition is implied by 05055532's old condition on the identical original ensemble, old frozen mask and old pursuit Lambda_old; both the leading coefficient and defect measure are no larger.

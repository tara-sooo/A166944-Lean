## 2. Stability excludes coincident departures and short lags

The selected sign of p at E is fixed, so Delta'=0 mod 2p and h is a nonzero even integer with |h|<2r(E). If Delta'=0, order t_x<t_y without loss. The earlier tag occupies (t_y-1,E) and remains there at time t_y; the later tag arrives at (t_y,E) by its actual preceding jump. This jump exists because each pursuit starts AFTER a completed source crossing and all events before the first defect have been shared translated edges. The incoming waiting and jump branches are distinct. At U=(t_y,E), write their actual continuing populations b_1,b_2 and full original visiting population n_U>=b_1+b_2 (including possible terminal snapshots or other arrivals). At least one of the two actual tags has variation token log(n_U/b_i)>=log 2 on its incoming transition. Both predecessor prehorizons are within the respective original stable source window: E-J_source<L_s<=D_s/4, J_source-C_source<=B, preceding jump at E-q+1 is no earlier than C_source, and B=o(D_s). No root injection is needed. This contradicts both stable token sums <=1/ell<log 2 eventually. Hence Delta'!=0. The two completed waiting intervals are disjoint (if they overlapped then deterministic continuation would force the same departure). Their order is preserved by the common p exit congruence; equivalently the least eligible p-exit after t is a nondecreasing rounding function, and each tag actually selects that exit. Thus Delta and Delta' have the same sign. In particular
    |Delta'|>=2p,   |Delta'-Delta|<2r(E),
    |Delta|>2p-2r(E).                                  (1)
When 0<|Delta|<2p, necessarily |Delta'|=2p, since rounding to the same residue modulo 2p can produce only zero or one full period in this interval. The zero case was excluded by stability. In that case |Delta'|-|Delta|=2p-|Delta|>0. This is an unconditional restriction on the OLD stable pair domain, not a new asymptotic deletion. For example if r(E)<=p/4 then no such pair has |Delta|<=3p/2. The argument does not assert that every same-label defect expands the lag; for larger lags either direction is possible.

## 3. Common previous label and a second CRT support

Let q be the last common selected label before E, INCLUDING the source crossing if no intervening translated jump exists. In both original histories the immediate preceding completed event has label q and prehorizon E-q+1, posthorizon E. Its departure times are exactly t_x,t_y. Since this jump is either the shared source or a translated edge, 2q|Delta. As 2p does not divide Delta, p!=q. Also t_x and t_y have the same residue c modulo L0, hence the same parity s. The exact previous exit congruences give
    q|(E+2s), t_x=t_y mod 2q,
    p|(E+sigma), sigma in {-1,1}.
The sign of q at E-q+1 fixes a single residue for t modulo 2q. The sign of p at E fixes a single residue for v modulo 2p. These statements include q=3 and q dividing L0; p does not divide L0.

Refine exact same-label charge by (E,p,q) where q is this actual preceding label, and separately by pursuit sector. For fixed distinct p,q and parity and sign choices, spatial CRT gives #E=O(X/(pq)), since E lies in an interval of length O(X) and pq<=B²=O(X). All refined charges are disjoint portions of the old same-label sector, and the old distinct-label sector is left unchanged. The phase c is an auxiliary partition for capacity, NOT a separate thin-payment key; no factor A in the support budget. This is the same two-prime spatial count previously available only for different labels AT the defect.

There is additional true-time support at a fixed E,p,q,c. Any tagged event f executing p at E with immediate previous q and recorded arrival t congruent c mod L0 has the two required residues for t mod 2q and t+w mod 2p, with 1<=w<=2r(E). The parity of w is fixed by c and the p-exit residue. For each admissible w, the possible distinct t form one progression modulo
    L=lcm(L0,2p,2q)>=2pq
if compatible at all. Original deficit slab bounds their span by 4H for large X, so at most 1+floor(4H/L) distinct arrival times per w. Hence the number of distinct recorded (t,v) slots in this refined phase class is <=r(E)(1+floor(4H/L)). This counts actual slots, not tags, and no uniformity of mass among them is inferred.

## 4. Minority-cover capacity in the two directions of the ordered pairs

For each fixed (E,p,q,c) let G be the full original set of tagged events with selected p at E, preceding q and recorded arrival t congruent c mod L0. Include all such events, not just stable ones, for an upper capacity. No event belongs to two such groups for this E,p. Every actual same-label pair in this phase has two members in G with distinct waits AND distinct true exits. The former follows from the definition of defect and the latter from section 2. By d16b4e0 its source crossing lies in the accepted backward radical suffix excluding p, and E-J_source<L_s. For each f in G define its individual raw energy capacity at grid P, sector s,
    U_f=2[min(G_rad(f),L_s+b_s)+P],  b_l=P/a, b_h=B,
zero on unusable grids for that sector. This bounds TOTAL energy involvement of this original tag/event in EITHER role, not merely when chosen as the destination: both actual histories are translated before the defect and the same source-atom row+column bound holds for both. One may use the discrete or modular refinements of d16b4e0 as capacities, provided they include both roles and the required original lag tests. Every charge under consideration uses two distinct members of G.

Put U= sum_(f in G) U_f, U_w=max_(w0) sum_(f:w(f)=w0) U_f, U_v=max_(v0) sum_(f:v(f)=v0) U_f, with zero maxima when G empty. A pair cannot have both endpoints in the maximum-wait subset. Every pair therefore has at least one endpoint outside that subset, so its total charge is at most the sum of involvement capacities there, counting a pair twice only for an upper bound. The same argument works for the maximum-exit subset, since equal true exits impossible. Therefore
    D_(P,s)(E;p,p;q,c) <= U-max(U_w,U_v).               (2)
This is a weighted vertex-cover upper bound on the entire phase's exact charge. Among covers using only the two restrictions distinct wait and distinct exit, it is the best complement-of-independent-set bound: for the graph joining any two vertices with distinct waits and exits, every independent set is contained in one wait class or one exit class. Indeed if it contains two vertices with the same wait and different exits, any further vertex of a different wait would have to have both exits, impossible; if no such two vertices, all vertices are in a single exit or a single wait class. Actual feasible pairs may have further restrictions, so additional smaller covers are possible. No assertion of an optimal cover for actual pairs.

Sum (2) over destination phase c then average over the SAME m original grids. This bounds exact Dbar_s(E;p,p;q). In particular let
    mu(E;p,q)=sum_c ( |G_c|-max(max_w #G_c(w), max_v #G_c(v)) ).
Here the populations are DESTINATION recorded-arrival groups and true exit groups, not the earlier source n_c. Choosing each phase's largest group by population (independent of grid) and using U_f<=2(L_s+b_s+P), we have
    Dbar_s(E;p,p;q) <= 2 C_s mu(E;p,q),              (3)
where C_l=(1/m)sum_usable(L_l+P/a+P), C_h=L_h+B+P_max. This allows a huge projected event to be payable when all but a small minority of its members share the same wait or true exit in each preceding-label/phase refinement. The weighted capacity (2) is at least as good when summed and averaged, using the nonconstant radical suffixes. Neither (2) nor (3) bounds the total summed relaxed capacities over all possible keys by o(NT).

## 5. Additional thin payment and late physical concentration

For Q=B/u and nu=# of the distinct labels p,q that are >=Q, pay the remaining exact averaged same-label refined charge of each sector and (E,p,q) whenever
    Dbar_s(E;p,p;q) <= H u^nu/(u ell³).              (4)
Sum the disjoint label bands using the support O(X/(pq)) from section 3, sum_(3<=p<=B)1/p=O(ell) and sum_(Q<=p<=B)1/p=O(ell/u). Total O(XH/(u ell))=O(NT/ell) per sector, after the previous masks. There are two sectors; c_a=O(1) so this is also o(NT) in clock units. No phase multiplicity, and no new charge for a previously paid key. In particular the two-high same-label sector (large chosen p and large preceding q) pays every refined key with
    mu(E;p,q) <= H u/(2 C_s ell³).
With tau=1/log ell, C_h=Theta(B ell), so the high-pursuit minority threshold is Theta(B u^(3/4)/ell^4), just as for the earlier distinct-label bilateral cap, but now on the MINORITY within one refined group. This does not require an upper bound on the largest wait or exit population. Similar sufficient payments follow by comparing (2) with (4). The case with only one high label or neither has its own displayed threshold; do not apply the high-high threshold to those bands.

Further, for a surviving late same-label pair with common prefix radical >=S=H/ell^.25, d16b4e0 already gives b_v,b_v'>n_source/sqrt(ell) at its two actual source physical exit slots. Stability of each original source tag implies that at least (1-exp(delta)delta)b_v of that slot's original members share its whole actual segment from source predeparture through the destination postdeparture (3fe50cf6 cohort intersection; the segment lies in the D_s window). In particular they all execute the immediate preceding q jump and the destination p jump with the same recorded t and v, because the preceding jump is within the segment, including when it is the source jump. Thus the two distinct (t,v) cohorts at this refined destination have populations each >(1-o(1))n_source/sqrt(ell). The two waits and exits are different. Both cohorts are in the same c phase and same q refinement, so neither one maximum-wait group nor one maximum-exit group can cover them both. This gives a necessary destination-side concentration and in particular mu(E;p,q)>=(1-o(1)) n_source/sqrt(ell). The source class lower bound n_source>H/(A q_source u ell²) further yields a bound using q_source<=B. This is a lower bound on the minority for those residual late pairs, not an upper bound or proof that no such pairs exist. It does not use a claim of stability of all destination members.

## 6. Resulting route and limitations

Let D_same,rem' be the exact remaining same-label energy after (4), and D_distinct,rem the unchanged old distinct-label remainder. Then D_same,rem'<=D_same,rem and the difference is O(NT/ell). Under finitely many twins the old certificate gives
    NT/2-o(NT) <= B_rem+(3+o(1))(D_distinct,rem+D_same,rem').
The corresponding liminf objective <1/2 suffices and is implied by the previous one. This additional payment is little-o, not a demonstrated constant saving. The improved raw occupancy test (5) of a3f065b still holds, now with smaller admissible same-label targets: a key can be excluded if the minority capacity (2) or (3) is below (4). Use the old exact forward sector normalization; do not change its coefficient. The residue-exact prefix tree can incorporate the preceding label (the last prefix label, or source q) and the noncollision requirement for same-label children via actual true departure times. If using binary prehorizon target masks, project allowed keys to their prehorizons as before; that relaxation may lose some of the new saving. The required global upper bound on surviving birth and forward charges is not established. No Lean compilation. Main and independent branches not modified.

## 2. New partner-side stability cleaning in RAW boundary measure

Use the original true-tick entropy tokens e_M(j) of 3fe50cf6, whose total over original tag-transitions is <=N(2+log N). Define D_s=floor(T/(u ell^6)), delta=1/ell. An original tag-event at C is called stable here if its original root and endpoint horizons are each at least D_s in gain from C, and its total entropy token sum over original pre-transition horizons in [C-D_s,C+D_s] is <=delta. Otherwise call it bad for stability. These are local conditions on the ORIGINAL incidence, not just its full group. This shorter window still satisfies D_s>>B+P_max.

For each grid, measure bad crossings by RAW incidence weight P for every strictly crossed boundary K (not by actual event wait). Let Q_bad(P) be the resulting sum over unstable original incidences. The first/last D_s trims cost O(N(D_s+B+P)) in this raw measure: a crossing's K is in its gain interval, each original tag crosses K at most once, and a horizon strip of length L enlarged by B contains at most O((L+B)/P+1) multiples of P. For the entropy part, each token at horizon J can influence only crossings with prehorizon within D_s of J, whose K all lie in [J-D_s,J+D_s+B]. Their total raw weight per tag <=O(D_s+B+P). Exchange sums and apply Markov to get
    Q_bad(P) <= O(N(D_s+B+P) + N u(D_s+B+P)/delta)
             = O(NT/ell^5)                                   (1)
uniformly over all grids. This is a different measure from the previous linear clock trim; its proof does not infer a small raw boundary weight from a small wait.

At projected key (K,C,q) let n_bad(C,q) be the number of unstable original tags executing that event, across all phases. Pay the entire raw projected key if n_bad>theta n/8. The paid raw measure is <=8Q_bad(P)/theta = O(NT/ell^4). Transferring to BOTH directional original clock cells costs at most 18 times the raw measure, by the 9P per tag capacity in each direction. All counts are frozen, and the same stability key predicate holds at all K crossed by this event. Thus this mask costs O(NT/ell^4) per scale without any bound on the group mass. Add it to the previous local geometric, thin and phase-rank masks BEFORE averaging; their mean remains O(NT/ell). The usual epsilon threshold pays O(NT/sqrt(ell)) actual clock, and every retained e has >=(1-epsilon)M good grids. Also retain the direct original clock entropy trim of 3fe50cf6 with window D=T/(u ell²), or use the shorter D_s directly at cost O(NT/ell^5). This guarantees focal stability. No assumption of independence between different scales.

## 3. Stable far partner mass at all good witnesses

At a retained focal event e, write n_e for its full original true-tick group size at arrival and set R_e=16 max(P_max,p_e), n_cut(e)=H/(16 R_e u ell³), as in fe87bc2. On any good witness, stability gives b_v<=exp(delta)n_e<=2n_e eventually. Distinct slots separated by 2q, so at most floor(R_e/q)+1<=2R_e/q of the smaller-or-equal-mass slots are within radius R_e. For n_e<=n_cut(e), the total mass of these near slots is <=4R_e n_e/q<=H/(4q u ell³)<theta n(C,q)/4. Thus even using the SAME radius R_e at every witness (rather than a different R_j), the far smaller-or-equal-mass partner count is >theta n/2. The bad projected key mask ensures n_bad<=theta n/8. Subtracting all unstable partners, regardless of their phase rank or distance, leaves
    f_stable(C,q,v;R_e)
      = # stable original partners in slots b_v'<=b_v, |v'-v|>R_e
      > 3 theta n(C,q)/8
      > 3H/(8q u ell³).                                    (2)
The same bound holds at smaller radius R_P(q)=16 max(P,q), used for the normalized energy below. The number of distinct qualifying slots is >= f_stable/b_v >3H/(16q u ell³ n_e). A stable tag in a slot certifies a long common physical path for nearly all members of its slot, not necessarily stability of every original member.

## 4. Common anchor strands, stable for both focal and partners

For bookkeeping choose S=floor(D_s/8), and partition horizons into central blocks [K,K+S), K a multiple of S (this auxiliary grid need not be primorial). For every original tag with actual strict crossings of K-S and K+2S, group it by its true first anchor departure (C,p,v) and last anchor departure. The earlier anchor poststate is below K for large X since S>>B. Deterministic evolution implies every member has the same true-tick path from the earlier anchor predeparture through the later anchor poststate. Call such group active if it has at least one stable original tag-event with prehorizon within [K-2P_max-B,K+S+2P_max+B]. Such an event may be a self or nonself witness of any focal e in the central block. Each active witness is between the two anchor crossings, whose actual pre/post horizons are within its D_s window; endpoint trims ensure their existence.

The full tag group at any stable anchor-interior reference state has intersection over the entire anchor segment of size >=(1-exp(delta)delta) times its full mass by the entropy lemma. In particular if an active group executes a witness departure v with stable representative at that witness, its anchor membership m_g satisfies
    (1-exp(delta)delta)b_v <= m_g <= b_v.                   (3)
For the lower bound, use reference state (v-1,C) with full mass >=b_v. The intersection includes both anchor pre/post states; shared continuing trajectories therefore execute both anchor jumps, including the later poststate. The same applies to the focal anchor group using the focal arrival. All tags in one anchor group execute all its internal witness jumps, even if their own local entropy mask would have paid some incidences. Two different active groups at fixed K cannot share a true state between their anchors: first meeting would lie after their distinct earlier anchor exits and before the last anchor exits, hence within both stable representatives' D_s windows. At least one incoming continuing branch would contribute log2>delta, contradiction; root injections excluded by the endpoint trims. If earlier anchor exit agrees, determinism and common endpoint reach make the groups identical. This is the same true-state noncollision argument of 3fe50cf6, applied to the enlarged active region, not a statement about projected states.

For one focal e, all its good witnesses (including self if applicable) and their stable partner witnesses lie inside the same block's anchored span. For each witness and each qualifying slot choose one stable representative. It identifies a unique active anchor strand executing that slot. Distinct slots give different strands. Every stable partner tag in that slot has the same anchor group: if their anchors differed, they would share the departure state in violation of noncollision. Their memberships are disjoint across active groups at K, and sum of their m_g over a witness's partner strands is >=(1-exp(delta)delta) f_stable (by (3), or by including the stable partner tags themselves). Thus (2) becomes a weighted population lower bound on actual distinct long partner strands, all with masses <=b_v<=2n_e. This compresses the repeat problem from arbitrary tag-level phase lists to a fixed finite set of long physical paths in each central block. The distinct phase populations do not have to agree across witnesses. Every active group tag origin lies in a common interval of length O(H), so their disjoint union has O(H/u) tags by the uniform elementary prime interval upper bound. This latter population bound alone does not force overlap of the partner sets: the lower bound (2) can be much smaller.

## 5. Exact repeat and lag constraints in a block

Consider a fixed pair of distinct active anchor strands for a focal e, and any two of their common projected departures (same C,q, possibly different actual v). Suppose the posthorizons are J_1<J_2, and signed lags Delta_i=v'_i-v_i. From the two actual elapsed waits between those departures, both in [0,3(J_2-J_1)], we have the exact bound
    |Delta_2-Delta_1| <= 3(J_2-J_1).                       (4)
No assumption of the same intervening selected labels. At each common departure Delta_i is a nonzero multiple of 2q_i (nonzero from noncollision). When the same partner strand qualifies as far at two different good witnesses of e, they lie within the annulus span of e. If any nonself witness exists, p_e<=P_max/a; all good witness labels are <=P_max/a, including self when present. Their prehorizons lie within C_e+-2P_max, so J_2-J_1<=(4+1/a)P_max. Hence 3(J_2-J_1)<16P_max=R_e for large X. The sign of a qualifying far lag cannot change anywhere among their shared projected departures within that span, including departures not qualifying as far. If all good witnesses are self there is only one actual event and no repeat assertion. Thus repeated partner strands can be separated into earlier and later strands without a sign switch on the focal witness span.

A stronger arithmetic obstruction applies to a completely translated segment. Starting at a common postdeparture horizon J_1 with lag Delta, the actual two continuations have an unchanged translated edge if and only if their selected labels agree and exit lag stays Delta. The exact MIN translation lemma of 1a4005f2f336 gives that such edges have selected q dividing Delta/2; equivalently at a common input with nonzero even lag, if BOTH next labels divide Delta/2, the two-way lexicographic MIN comparison forces same edge and unchanged lag. One may directly verify the forward implication from the shared exit residue class mod 2q. A split or a same-edge lag change is a first defect; original path termination is separate.

Define even modulus L(Delta)=2 product_(odd primes q dividing |Delta|/2) q, with empty product 1. All labels in the unchanged translated prefix divide L(Delta). The strict primorial barrier actually holds for ANY even modulus divisible by the selected odd q: for C even and q|(C+-1), a strict crossing of multiple of L by [C,C+q-1) would require even distance a=K-C in [0,q-2], congruent to +-1 mod q, impossible. Hence no such translated edge can strictly cross a multiple of L. The union of consecutive half-open gain intervals in a completely translated prefix is a single half-open interval of length less than L (indeed length at least L would contain a multiple). Consequently, between shared posthorizons J_1<J_2 with lag Delta at the first, if
    J_2-J_1 >= L(Delta),                                    (5)
there must be an actual translation defect before or at the latter departure. The prefix length bound also holds with the smaller even product of only its actual distinct selected labels. The argument allows defects followed by rejoin, including a rejoin at the same nonzero lag; equality of endpoint lags alone does NOT certify an unchanged translated prefix.

Since |Delta|=O(H), any unchanged translated segment can contain at most floor(log(cH)/log Y) DISTINCT prime labels >=Y for absolute c. In particular for Y=B^alpha, alpha fixed positive, that count is O_alpha(1). More generally, choose any subcollection of focal shared witnesses with pairwise distinct labels >=Y. A single partner strand which executes more than that count of these witnesses must have at least one translation defect between its earliest and latest such departures, or (5) can force a defect even with repeated labels. The actual repeated labels need not be distinct; do not turn this into a bound on the number of all witness occurrences. There is no proof all moderate small-label focal events have many distinct labels >=Y, and partner sets may switch across witnesses.

## 6. Clean normalized far energy and sufficient route

In E_P of fe87bc2 replace f_R by f_stable at the same radius R_P(q)=16 max(P,q), retaining its frozen unpaid rank slot and near-mass cutoff (floor(R_P(q)/q)+1)b_v<=theta n/2. Denote the new energy E_P^s. For EVERY projected key, the removed energy (before multiplying by P) is <=n_bad(C,q): exchange partner and focal slots, and for each unstable partner tag the sum of the focal coefficients b_v/n is at most one, even with the rank and distance restrictions. Thus
    0<=E_P-E_P^s<=Q_bad(P)=O(NT/ell^5)                      (6)
uniformly. The same column-capacity argument works for any other partner-side deleted original tag-boundary measure; cannot substitute a small actual wait measure without its own raw boundary payment. This is linear in partner measure, not quadratic in the number of focal tags.

At each good witness of a retained n_e<=n_cut event, (2) gives f_stable/n>3theta/8 and the near-mass cutoff holds. One-direction actual-clock cell capacity 9P then yields
    W_mod <= [24/(theta(1-epsilon))] mean_j E_(P_j)^s.       (7)
In particular a sufficient condition is liminf [W_massive+24 ell mean E^s/(1-epsilon)]/(NT)<1/2, or mean E^s=o(NT/ell) plus a strict massive-sector clock bound on the same scales. The constant 24 is larger than the previous 18, due to the removed bad partners. By (6) the little-o energy tests with or without stability cleaning are equivalent; this is NOT an independently proved smaller asymptotic upper bound. The benefit is the existence of long actual stable partner strands and the precise repeat/defect dichotomy of sections 4-5.

## 1. Frozen measures and parameters

Original grids P_j with common denominator m, P_max<=B^.25, a=u^.25, c_a=3(1+2/a), phase wheel L0 largest even primorial<=ell, high threshold Y=B^.1, tau>=1/log ell tends to zero, original high pursuit L_h=ceil(B exp(1/tau)). Every retained high focal event has p>=Y and w>=tau(p-1). Use the original assigned good waits, original source phase n_c and actual stable partner sets, and all old masks through 3249997. No recomputation of original word partition, counts, assignment or route. A high-sector exact forward charge, in ENERGY units at a source crossing with n_k atoms, has original weight W_i^h/(c_a n_k n_c) per ordered pair per atom. Split this linearly by individual original assigned focal waits, using their original weights. The chosen nonperiodic destination and focal/partner actual first-defect events remain unchanged. Only remove new exact fractions.

Set delta=ell^(-1/8), sigma=ell^(-1/4). For sufficiently large X divide [Y,B^(1-3delta)] into disjoint exponent bands with first upper exponent beta_1=1/4, subsequent upper exponents spaced by at most delta, last beta=1-3delta, including .75 as an endpoint when in range. The first band is [Y,B^.25], subsequent bands open at lower endpoint and closed at upper. If necessary omit empty bands; we work asymptotically where 1-3delta>.75. There are K=O(1/delta) bands. For each upper exponent beta write U=B^beta, L=ceil(B^(beta+delta)). Every event in this band has gain <U and wait <=2U. L/U>=B^delta, P_max<=U. The near-maximal top band p>B^(1-3delta) keeps the OLD high pursuit and all earlier masks, no new short pursuit asserted. This refines both the previous medium and part of its top-self band; existing masks remain frozen on their original definitions. The first band's upper exponent .25 ensures even a small nonself source witness label q<=P/a is <=U; for all other bands any retained original focal high event with p>P_max is necessarily self (or would violate the nonself geometry). In all shortened bands source label q<=U and its posthorizon J satisfies J-C_e<=U+2P_max for the original focal event e at C_e. The old L_h is strictly larger than every L (max L<=B^(1-2delta)+1).

## 2. Pay terminal portions of each actual band

For each original tag and each band independently, partition prehorizons into fixed ordinary blocks of length floor(L/2). Use all actual completed band events with w>=tau(p-1), regardless of whether some were excluded as original focal events. For an event e, let V_e be the sum of actual waits of strictly later such events in the SAME block and band. Mark e terminal if V_e<sigma L. Within a block marked events form a suffix in band-event order, with total wait <=sigma L+2U (include first marked event's own wait). A tag intersects O(H/L+1) blocks. Thus the sum of all marked original waits across shortened bands and all tags is
    O(N sum_beta (H/L+1)(sigma L+U))
       = O(NH K (sigma+B^(-delta)) + N K B^(1-2delta))
       = o(NT).                                      (1)
Here K sigma=O(ell^(-1/8)), K B^(-delta)->0, H=Theta(B²/u^.25), and L<<H uniformly. This is a bound on ACTUAL waits, not on the count of tagged candidate events. Each original wait on each grid is assigned at most once and the exact ordered-pair energy over all its partners and atoms is at most its wait/c_a. Hence pay exactly all surviving high forward fractions from these marked waits at total energy O(the RHS of (1)), preserving backward and low sectors and every previous payment. The block tests do not depend on grid or partner. It is not required that later waits contributing V_e survive their own masks.

For each unmarked original focal e in band beta, V_e>=sigma L. At any old good right witness source with posthorizon J, the portion of those later actual waits lost before or at J is <=3(U+2P_max) by the gain distance bound and w<=3g. These events end no later than the actual witness crossing J if their prehorizons precede J; hence no unaccounted partial event. Since U/L<=B^(-delta) and P_max<=U, the loss is o(sigma L). The remaining events have prehorizons <C_e+L/2+O(1)<=J+L/2+O(1), and their gains <U, so all end before J+L eventually. Therefore the actual focal continuation from J to first completed crossing of gain L contains >=sigma L/2 expensive waits in this same band. Old stability/future room already gives a completed continuation through L_h on both partners. No need for all other events in this continuation to have small labels.

## 3. Same old first defect is inside the shorter pursuit

Assume an original stable pair has nonzero source lag |Delta|<=4H and translates unchanged until the first completed gain crossing L after J. Every selected label in its common prefix (including source) has period dividing Delta. At most log(4H)/log Y=20+o(1) distinct high primes can occur.

For fixed expensive band label p in [Y,U], apply the same two-form upper sieve as d7cc573/3249997 on a prehorizon interval of length L+U+O(1)=O(L). Required r(C)>=tau(p-1)/2. Use cutoff z=floor(min(p^(1/40),(L/p)^(1/40))), excluding 2,p as before. Since log p>=u/10 and log(L/p)>=delta u, log(2+z)>>delta u eventually (delta<.1); z<tau(p-1)/2 since tau>=1/log ell. Error z^8<=(L/p)^.2 absorbed by (L/p)/(delta u)^2 uniformly because L/p>=B^delta. Thus # eligible C=O(L/[p delta² u²]) and fixed-label total actual expensive wait=O(L/[delta² u²]) (w<=2p). The same weighted upper bound holds for spatial rough candidate positions on a tag even if that tag's actual wait is not expensive. The path visits each C at most once. Summing over the bounded number of high labels in the translated prefix gives O(L/[delta² u²])=o(sigma L). This contradicts section 2. The possible last crossing with a very large label causes no problem: richness was certified on band events COMPLETED before J+L, and the sieve only counts band prehorizons in [J,J+L). In particular actual first defect must have common prehorizon
    J<=E<J+L.                                        (2)
The proof uses the actual same earlier source pair, not a reselected smaller word class. It applies to every old surviving high fraction from this unmarked band, including those assigned to the partner as the chosen nonperiodic side. Even if some source originally used a longer pursuit, its already-defined first defect is the one in (2). No extra forward charge is manufactured from a previously compatible pair. All old pair masks remain valid unchanged.

## 4. Shortened capacities, both roles and a global upward payment

Fix an original tagged event f at prehorizon E and grid. On either actual participant's original history, a band source crossing of a surviving charge to f has q<=U, posthorizon J with E-J<L and prehorizon C>=J-U. Thus every source atom K is in [E-L-U,E]. With the old row and column bounds in energy units, total charge involving f in EITHER one fixed role is <=L+U+P, and both roles together <=2(L+U+P). This uses the old n_c and original per-atom total capacity, not a new denominator.

For the focal role there is a stronger sieve cap. Scan backwards as in 2fdd238, using the new pursuit bound (2) and source radical limit 4H. All source crossings in the actual band are in the accepted contiguous suffix. The whole suffix has length <=L+B if the earliest accepted event is arbitrary; to apply the local sieve only use its intersection with [E-L-U,E], of length L+U. Source witnesses in that interval account for assigned band waits in the interval, plus at most a single initial right-cell fringe of wait <=3P. Every band label in the suffix divides its bounded radical. Apply the local sieve of section 3 to those band labels, obtaining focal per-tag event capacity
    O(L/(delta²u²)+P_max) = O(L/(delta²u²)).
The last equality uses L>=B^(.25+delta). For self-band partner roles, the common source projected q and necessary r(C)>=tau(q-1)/2 allow the same argument with 2q per-source column cap. Nonself partner roles need not have expensive band waits in their own history; only use the raw O(L) bound above for them. For mixed source geometry in the first band, both component bounds apply without double counting, or just use the full raw O(L) envelope. No use of actual selected label of f in obtaining the shortening or local bound.

Define the upward threshold Z=B^(beta+2delta) for this band. Pay all exact remaining charges for which EITHER of the two actual selected labels at the common first defect is >=Z. Route that exact charge solely for this payment proof to an actual participant with such a large label (choose focal on ties). This is a reindexing of exact nonnegative fractions, not another copy of charge. On one original tag, number of completed events with label >=Z is <=H/(Z-1), simply because every such event consumes gain >=Z-1. The two-role cap above bounds ALL exact fractions paid at each tagged large event, even if both roles or many original pair keys were used, without needing the original source wait to lie on the chosen participant. Sum over original tags and the same grids:
    D_up,beta <= 2(L+U+P_max) N H/(Z-1)
                 = O(NT B^(-delta)).
Then sum over K=O(1/delta) bands:
    sum_beta D_up,beta = O(NT K B^(-delta))=o(NT).      (3)
This is a global exact energy upper bound, not a local thin cutoff. It uses the original total gain budget and does NOT multiply by the number of destination labels, phase classes or relaxed pair keys. With the old c_a=O(1), it is o(NT) in clock units as well. It applies even if the large-label participant was not the old chosen nonperiodic side. No requirement of two large labels at a defect. For analytic proof (3) the raw O(L) cap suffices, including nonself partner orientation; the sharper sieve cap is still available for other residual key estimates.

## 5. Remaining support and sufficient condition

For every remaining shortened-band charge, both actual defect labels are <Z. The last common selected label q_prev before E is either the source q<=U, or a later actual translated event with its gain q_prev-1<=E-J<L, hence q_prev<L+1. This also bounds the second label in every same-label predecessor key from e5d1157. Thus BOTH labels used for the two-prime CRT support are <Z eventually, for both same and distinct-label defects. In particular beta<=1-3delta implies Z<=B^(1-delta) << B/u. ALL high-sector residual two-label keys with even ONE label >=B/u must originate in the near-maximal top source band p>B^(1-3delta), after the additional payments. The statement holds for the two actual labels and the immediate predecessor label, not just the old chosen nonperiodic one. For the first band source p<=B^.25, upper exponent of any remaining defect label is .25+2delta. For subsequent bands it is at most (log p_source)/u+3delta. Therefore across shortened bands the positive increase in label exponent is at most 3delta, with the first band exception as stated. This does not bound downward label changes or assert closeness of actual primes, nor does it exclude top-source contributions to small destination labels.

The exceptional top source band has reciprocal prime sum O(delta+1/u) by the usual prime harmonic bound (e.g. partial summation from the prime number theorem), but that alone does not bound actual tag-weighted high clock on those sources. No such global saving claimed. Shortened capacities of section 4 and previously established minority/bilateral capacities may be used with the old pair-key absolute cutoffs (restricted label support as above). We have not shown that the remaining upward-compatible, downward or near-maximal-top charges are small in total. The low forward and backward birth charges are untouched. Let D_h,new be high exact residual after (1) and (3); then D_h,new<=D_h,old and difference o(NT). Under finitely many twins the old certificate gives NT/2-o(NT)<=B_rem+(3+o(1))(D_l,rem+D_h,new), and the corresponding liminf objective <1/2 suffices. Unlike a mere smaller per-key cap, (3) pays an entire family of exact charges without assuming population bounds, but this is still little-o, not a proved positive constant margin. No Lean compilation. Main and independent branches not modified.

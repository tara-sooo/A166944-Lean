## 1. Exact compositional reset trees

Recall shortReset_merge_birth from NegativeMINCore: if p>=11 is odd prime, y divisible by 4p, A=y+p-1 is rough at p (all odd factors of A+-1 >=p), and y is rough at 11, the actual orbit from birth(A) fires p at age (p-7)/2 and reaches birth(y) after three more ticks. This is physical state equality, including the signed clock. Its elapsed birth-to-birth time is (p-1)/2. The previous fixed-r fan used r one-edge parents of x; here every subset of an ordered prime list gives a different root.

Take p1<...<pr, p_i=1 mod 12, p_j=1 mod p_i for i<j, and superincreasing shifts p_i-1>sum_{j<i}(p_j-1). Put b(S)=sum_{i in S}(p_i-1), A_S=x+b(S). Require x divisible by 12 and all p_i, x+-1 free of primes <=Z>=p_r, and for every nonempty S require A_S+-1 free of primes <min_{i in S}p_i. If i=min S, y=A_{S\{i}} is divisible by 4p_i: x is, and all remaining shifts are multiples of 4p_i. The destination is rough at 11 by the corresponding source condition or the final target condition. Thus the actual orbit from birth(A_S) meets birth(A_{S\{i}}) exactly. Induction on |S| gives the chronological firing word of S in increasing prime order and arrival at birth(x). Total elapsed age is b(S)/2, consistent with the absolute clock. All 2^r roots are distinct by superincrease and are multiples of six.

Let Delta=sum(p_i-1), T=floor((Z-8)/3), W=6 floor(T/6). If x and x+Delta are in the same observation window, all roots have met by the common observation start because x is the latest birth of the fan. The age of x through retirement is <=3T/2; from the canonical height pin, any first firing by that age has label <=3T+7<=Z, impossible by roughness. Thus the full observed phase at x has at least 2^r actual roots and contributes T min(M,2^r) to exact clipped occupancy. This uses the previously verified full-deadline and terminal-phase formulas. No freely chosen edges or independently restarted clocks.

## 2. Simultaneous CRT, then polynomial-scale lower sieve

Let B0=12 product_{5<=q<=2^{r+2}, q prime} q. Choose p_i recursively in the class 1 mod B0 product_{j<i}p_j. This gives superincrease (the modulus exceeds the sum of previous shifts, including i=1). All p_i>=11. For q<=p_r the required local congruences are compatible:
- q=2,3: x=0 mod12, all shifts multiples of 12.
- q selected p_i: force x=0 mod q. Any source condition involving q has min label >q, hence all its shifts are 0 mod q; so the two prohibited residues are not zero.
- 5<=q<=2^{r+2}: every shift is 0 mod q, avoid only +/-1.
- all other q<=p_r: at most 2^{r+1} forbidden residues, at most q/2. Indeed there are 2^r subsets including empty, each contributes at most two; only sources with q<min label matter.
Let B=12 product_{5<=q<=p_r prime}q, and let C be all locally allowed classes mod B, rho=|C|/B. For each a in C sieve x=a mod B against x+-1 for p_r<q<=Z. On an interval of length ~R, X=R/B, g(q)=2/q for these q, zero for the already handled primes; CRT gives |r_d|<=2^omega(d) for squarefree d. The dimension-2 product hypothesis holds uniformly since removing local factors only lowers product ratios; V(Z)>=c/log^2 Z. Apply the fundamental lemma with D=R^{1/2}, Z=floor(R^delta), sufficiently small fixed delta>0, so s=log D/log Z is sufficiently large. Sum remainder <=sum_{d<=D}tau(d)=O(D log D) per class. This yields
    # admissible x in [R,2R-Delta] >> rho R/(log Z)^2 - O(B R^{1/2} log R)
whenever Delta=o(R), B=R^{o(1)} (for fixed r automatic). The lower-bound coefficient is positive by choice of fixed delta, not by assuming a lower-bound sieve at the square-root cutoff.
Remove x too close to the right edge of each window or slab: O((R/T+1)(Delta+6)) integers, negligible when Delta=R^{o(1)} and rho>=R^{-o(1)}. Distinct good x contribute at distinct physical bins, and simultaneous clipping is feasible by the established full-deadline formula.

Already for fixed r this produces >=c_r R/log^2 R stationary centers with 2^r actual roots for T of order R^delta, not merely logarithmic T. The constants may depend strongly on r.

## 3. Growing-depth construction, quantitatively uniform

Write L=log R. Let K>=1 be an absolute Linnik exponent (p<=C m^K in any reduced class mod m; no optimized numerical value needed). Choose fixed kappa>0 so kappa log(2(K+1))<1/2, and r=floor(kappa loglog L). Taking the least prime in each chosen class, log p_i <= K(log B0+sum_{j<i}log p_j)+O(1). Put S_i=sum_{j<=i}log p_j. Iteration gives S_r=O((K+1)^r(log B0+1))=O(r[2(K+1)]^r)=o(log L), since log B0<=O(r 2^r) by the factorial bound. Therefore p_r<=L^{o(1)}, Delta<=L^{o(1)}, and log B<=O(p_r log p_r)=L^{o(1)}, i.e. B=R^{o(1)}.

Density of allowed classes is not 1/B: use all CRT classes. Selected primes contribute product p_i^{-1}=exp(-S_r)=L^{-o(1)}. Small unselected primes contribute product(1-2/q), bounded below by a positive constant times (log(2^{r+2}))^{-2} by Mertens. Remaining primes exclude at most 2^{r+1} residues, with fraction <=1/2; log-density cost <=O(2^r sum_{q<=p_r}1/q)=O(2^r(1+loglog p_r))=o(log L). Including 1/12, rho>=L^{-o(1)}. The analytic remainder B R^{1/2} log R=R^{1/2+o(1)} is negligible against rho R/L^2. Hence for every sufficiently large R, at least R L^{-2-o(1)} distinct centers in the slab each have an actual stationary fiber of size >=2^r throughout a T~R^delta/3 observation window, with
    2^r >= (log L)^{kappa log 2}/2 -> infinity.
For M~L^2.1 no loss within the constructed fan. In particular the maximal actual terminal multiplicity grows at least as a positive power of log L at polynomial observation time. The constructive clipped terminal mass lower is T min(M,2^r) times the above count. Its normalized size is only L^{-2-o(1)} at this level of density precision; it does not establish L^{-1/4}. The original near-square-root upper-sieve scale is not used at polynomial time. Existing twins need not be removed for this unconditional construction; no infinitude follows.

## 4. Test the approach against the original target: nested-family obstruction

The full subset construction has an intrinsic ceiling independent of the density estimates. Put P_i=product_{j<=i}p_j. Nested congruences imply p_{i+1}>=P_i+1 and P_{i+1}>=P_i^2. Also P_r|x. Thus
    2^{r-1} log p_1 <= log P_r <= log(2R), 
    2^r=O(log R).
Even granting a near-square-root rough frontier of the maximal ordinary upper-sieve size O(R/L^2), counting one such full subset family per center gives O(R/L) roots, which is o(R/L^.25). This is a limitation on this particular nested-prime architecture, NOT a bound on all reset trees or on the union of several distinct families at one center. The parity problem also prevents the polynomial-horizon lower sieve from simply moving its cutoff near sqrt(R). The needed next structure must permit multiple non-nested reverse branches/prime reuse across nodes, or use nonterminal phases rather than a single terminal fan. The general good-phase lower and independent uniform upper-sieve/Mathlib bridge remain open. No claim of level 4.85.

In particular sum_i r_i g_i=W(S*), since every pair has two actual endpoint incidences at the same horizon and the same r. The degree includes async as well as sync, so one need not assume the old async quadratic estimate separately.

    Q_X = R_h + P_abs + E_short + C_tot.                  (B)

This sum may include pieces with the other endpoint removed; the overcount only increases the upper bound. No energy claim is made for the removed high incidences. All arguments refer to exact original tag events; masks do not alter clocks.

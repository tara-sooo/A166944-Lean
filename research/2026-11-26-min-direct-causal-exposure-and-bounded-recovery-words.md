# Causal exposure and bounded-length high-label recovery words

Continuation of [d8a84b0](https://github.com/tara-sooo/A166944-Lean/blob/d8a84b0a98c00f921b5d94dc9b341709034d00c5/research/2026-11-26-min-direct-mandatory-cross-label-buffer-transport.md). Integrate the residence intervals of the height-by-height causal matching. This replaces the logarithmic recycling-scale bound on the delay of a macroscopic transport population by a constant multiple of the selected label scale T. The same argument charges small-label relay gain without multiplying it by the number of overlapping source-target pairs. Consequently, under the existing comparable-scale narrow-local-guard and low-gain hypotheses, the remaining cross-label supply must include Omega(X/T) disjoint actual bounded-length high-label words, with o(T) intervening small-label gain and ticks. This is a structural reduction, not yet an upper count of realizable words.

## 1. Exposure inequality for arbitrary causal matches

Use actual adaptive MIN coordinates H,p,g=p-1,w,I,d,d', with d'=d+2w-g, w<=2p<=3g for odd p>=3. The firing tick n=(H+g+d')/2. On a finite contiguous physical bin choose a finite height guard E of length M and form the full causal up-to-down matching from d422023: at each height u ignore the finitely many endpoints, match every downward transition to its immediately preceding upward transition in the same bin if present. Retain any subset of the resulting matches (or any measurable subportion of their height weights). Write W(s,t) for the retained height measure matched from upward source s to later downward target t.

For a fixed u, the intervals of physical event indices [s,t] of distinct matched pairs are disjoint. This follows from alternation at that level: after the downward event t the next upward source must be a strictly later event. Thus the associated horizon intervals [H_s,H_t) and firing-tick intervals [n_s,n_t) are separately disjoint. Summing their lengths at u and then integrating gives
    sum W(s,t)*(H_t-H_s) <= M*(H_last-H_first),           (1)
    sum W(s,t)*(n_t-n_s) <= M*(n_last-n_first).           (2)
Here first/last mean the first/last actual events of the bin, not of the retained pairs. In particular if pre-horizons are in a bin of width L, the right side of (1) is <=M L. There is no extra boundary jump term in (1) because it ends at the target PRE-horizon. All sums are finite; this integration can equally be done by splitting at finitely many plateau/guard endpoints.

More generally assign nonnegative costs c_i to the physical events of the bin. For each retained pair put C(s,t)=sum_(s<i<t)c_i, over strictly intervening events. For each u, these index sets are disjoint; therefore
    sum W(s,t)*C(s,t) <= M*sum_i c_i.                    (3)
For example for a threshold T, put E_low(s,t)=sum_(s<i<t,p_i<T) g_i and W_low(s,t)=sum_(s<i<t,p_i<T) w_i. Equation (3) gives
    sum W(s,t)*E_low(s,t) <= M G_low,
    sum W(s,t)*W_low(s,t) <= M W_low,total <=3M G_low.    (4)
G_low counts ALL physical low-label gain in this bin, not a separately filtered gain for each source. These inequalities hold for arbitrary label ranges, guard width, bin length, multipliers and total variation, with or without finite twins. They charge each event at each height at most once even if many matched pairs overlap in physical time at different heights. The same argument holds with costs on [s,t] including both endpoints; alternation prevents a shared endpoint at any fixed level. We use strict relay in (4).

## 2. Shorten the macroscopic transport to O(T)

Take the earlier macroscopic regime on canonical pre-horizons [X,Y], Y=lambda X with fixed lambda>1, C=floor sqrt(Y+1), G=O_lambda(X). Let integer T->infinity, T<=C. Bins have pre-horizon width L=ceil(T log^2 T), number O_lambda(X/L). Choose fast nonresets of labels in [T,R0] with R0<=K0 T, total selected gain F>=rho X, original guard widths M_j<=K0 T in each bin, and G_low=sum_(p<T)g=o(X), with K0,rho fixed positive constants. These hypotheses are unchanged from d8a84b0. In its expanded guards the widths Mplus_j<=K2 T, with K2 fixed. The preceding branch reduction produces retained causal cross-label weight P>=rho X/8 whose endpoints have distinct labels in [T,K3 T] and respective rise and drop >=eta*g for fixed eta>0. Both ends are actual events in the same bin. We use precisely this retained population in (1)-(4).

Since sum_j Mplus_j L<=K4 T X for some fixed K4, for any fixed D>0 the portion with H_t-H_s>D T satisfies
    P_delay>D T <= K4 X/D.                              (5)
Choose D sufficiently large depending only on the constants of the regime, so this is <=rho X/32. This uses the height-time exposure, not a new sieve or an assumption that individual labels recur faster than in cb54101. The previous log-squared upward-to-upward return scale need not shrink; it is the upward-to-downward causal transport delay that shrinks here.

Also by (4) the portion with E_low(s,t)>epsilon_X T is <=K2 G_low/epsilon_X. One may take epsilon_X=sqrt(G_low/X)+T^(-1/2), which tends to zero, and this deleted weight is o(X). On the retained pairs W_low(s,t)<=3 E_low(s,t)<=3 epsilon_X T. Thus for all sufficiently large windows there is at least rho X/16 actual matched weight satisfying
    p_s!=p_t, T<=p_s,p_t<=K3 T,
    d'_s-d_s>=eta*g_s, d_t-d'_t>=eta*g_t,
    0<H_t-H_s<=D T,
    E_low(s,t)<=epsilon_X T, W_low(s,t)<=3epsilon_X T.  (6)
The constant rho/16 is deliberately conservative: subtract rho/32 and o(X) from rho/8. The order of the restrictions is immaterial, since we only discard nonnegative weights. In particular the logarithmic gap in d8a84b0 can be reduced by a factor of order log^2 T for a macroscopic retained subpopulation. Both source and target remain in the same short bin, with shared actual causal levels. The hypotheses of this conclusion have not been forced on arbitrary infinite rays.

## 3. A bounded high-label word with small perturbation

For each pair in (6), retain the whole physical word from s through t inclusive. Delete its strictly intervening events with p<T from the displayed high-label list only (they are still part of the physical word). Write the resulting ordered high labels as p_0,...,p_m with p_0=p_s, p_m=p_t. Since the actual pre-horizon difference includes the entire gain of every event from s through the predecessor of t,
    H_t-H_s = sum_(i=0)^(m-1)(p_i-1)+E_low(s,t).       (7)
All m preceding high labels contribute >=T-1, so 1<=m<=floor(D T/(T-1)); each is <=D T+1. The target p_m<=K3 T. Thus this is a word of bounded length (at most a fixed integer J=ceil(2D)+2 for large T), all labels comparable to T with fixed constants, and distinct endpoint labels. All deleted low events together have o(T) gain and o(T) elapsed ticks, uniformly for the retained words. The whole word including the target spans at most (D+K3)T horizon length; its total gain and ticks are O(T). In particular no huge intermediate label can be hidden inside such a pair. Do not assert that low labels are absent or that the remaining high list is itself a valid MIN trajectory after deletion.

The exact endpoint clock equation is
    2(n_t-n_s)=H_t-H_s + (p_t-1)-(p_s-1) + d'_t-d'_s.
Writing W_hi for the sum of waits of the high events AFTER s through t inclusive, the relation becomes
    2 W_hi = sum_(i=1)^m(p_i-1)+d'_t-d'_s
                     + E_low -2 W_low.                (8)
The final term has absolute value <=5 E_low since w<=3g and the term lies in [-5 E_low,E_low]. Thus (7)-(8) reduce the endpoint horizon and firing-time relations to a finite-length high word with a uniform o(T) additive error. For a fixed pair of endpoint labels, sides, and shared height u, the endpoint multipliers have at most the 2 and 1 choices established in d8a84b0. If a high word and the exact integer low relay gain E_low are fixed, (7) determines the horizon gap; the prior CRT gives a unique start class mod 4 p_s p_t when compatible. A bin has width L=o(T^2), so at most one starting horizon in that class. This is an exact necessary counting constraint; we have NOT summed it over all possible high words and low relay gains with sufficient saving. In particular the deleted low gain, though o(T), need not be bounded independently of T.

## 4. Pack disjoint actual recovery words

Group all surviving height weights by their actual ordered pair of events, so each word occurs once with its weight W(s,t)>0. This weight is <=min(3p_s+1,p_t-3)<=K3 T. Associate the full physical interval [H_s,H_t+g_t). Its length is <=(D+K3)T. At any fixed horizon point x, every interval containing it must have a source start in (x-(D+K3)T,x]. Distinct such source starts differ by at least T-1: each is an actual event of label >=T, whose own jump must be completed before the next. Hence there are at most 2+floor((D+K3)T/(T-1)) possible sources. For each source, distinct targets have label >=T and pre-horizons in (H_s,H_s+D T], so their number is at most 1+floor(D T/(T-1)). The maximum number of these full physical intervals covering a point is therefore bounded by a fixed integer Q depending only on D,K3. This holds across bins as well; alternatively color inside each bin, since event sets in distinct bins are disjoint even if a last event overshoots its bin's coordinate boundary.

Order the intervals by start; greedy interval coloring with Q colors is possible because at every start there are at most Q-1 earlier intervals still active if all intervals sharing that start are processed in any fixed order. Each color consists of disjoint whole-event words. The color of largest total matched weight has >=(rho/(16Q))X. Thus there is a collection of pairwise event-disjoint actual words satisfying (6)-(8), with total retained matched weight Omega(X). Because each word has weight O(T), the number is Omega(X/T); because each contains at least two high events and no events are reused, their number is also O(X/T). This is stronger than the earlier local existence in Omega(X/L) bins: the main residual transport has a positive-density collection at the physical label scale T, not merely at the logarithmic return scale. It does not say that the words have identical labels, the same deficit baseline, or a fixed common sequence across roots.

## 5. Remaining obstruction

The reduction uses the actual level matching and no assumption of small ordinary total variation. It leaves a concrete finite-length transition problem: exclude or bound the supply of these bounded high words with distinct endpoints, strong opposite drift, common causal heights, and o(T) low relay. The number of high labels and the arithmetic baseline may vary substantially between words. The preceding capped or uncapped near-flat pair sieves do not apply directly, because ordinary endpoint differences need not be o(T), and a downward target need not be rough. The conditions F>=rho X, M_j=O(T), and low gain o(X) remain conditional and the general small/middle crossing alternatives and upward supply remain unresolved. No twin-infinitude contradiction or Lean kernel verification.

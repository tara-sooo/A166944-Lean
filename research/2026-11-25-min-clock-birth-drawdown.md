## 2. Exact four-way deficit conservation

For a born pair let d0=d_v, d1=its deficit at the earlier recorded stop, and dmin the MINIMUM of the deficits on the entire actual common recorded suffix INCLUDING both endpoints. Define
    I=d0-dmin, T=dmin, U=d1-dmin.
The ordinary common edge upward and downward drift sums A,D satisfy D+d1=A+d0. More precisely at each integer level, the common indicator 1_{d>=lambda} alternates upward/downward crossings. Match every downward crossing to the latest unmatched preceding upward crossing at that level if one exists since birth; otherwise it spends the initial level at birth. Precisely I initial levels are spent (lambda between dmin+1 and d0). Exactly T initial levels remain unspent. Precisely U upward crossings remain unmatched at the end (one at each level dmin<lambda<=d1). Thus with L the number of matched upward/downward crossings, the exact identities are
    D=I+L, A=L+U, d0=I+T, d1=U+T.                 (1)
Sum over all actual born ordered pairs to obtain the same identities in aggregate. They refine the previous exact flux identity. I is bounded both by d0 and by the common-suffix total selected gain: each downward step loses at most its gain. L is not the total positive drift, and need not include every positive-drift event. It is the actual matched part of it. This matching does not use cutoff-clipped waits; it uses the full recorded suffix including crossing event as before.

For a birth pair with same nonzero incoming label p and distinct predecessor states u,u', define J_pair=min((d_u-d_v)_+,(d_u'-d_v)_+) at nonreset v, and zero otherwise. This is the nonreset entry correction per ordered pair from the previous paper. Define actual debit
    C_pair=I+J_pair.
If J_pair>0 then d_v+J_pair<=min(d_u,d_u')<=12B+6, otherwise C_pair<=d_v. Thus C_pair<=12B+6 always. Also J_pair<=p-3<=d_v/2 at a nonreset downward entry; C_pair<=1.5 d_v. These inequalities hold for p=3 too since its drift is nonnegative. With C_init=sum_pairs C_pair, and L_shared=sum_pairs L, any subset of nonreset downward witness groups has second moment
    Q_down,nr <= NG + L_shared + C_init.           (2)
This follows from the exact diagonal/common-edge/entry partition and D=I+L; all reset descents in D are an upper envelope. The previous birth budget F_nr=sum d_v b_v+J_entry,nr is replaced by C_init=F_nr-sum T. The previous upper estimate with A_shared also replaced by L_shared=A_shared-sum U. Both subtractions are nonnegative and exact for actual suffixes, not estimated from separate maxima. For the short-wait gain moment G_cheap,nr (same p, same nonreset post-state, both full waits<=eps(p-1), ordered offdiagonal) and actual late energy C_cheap,nr of those pairs, the earlier proof gives
    (1-2eps) G_cheap,nr <= L_shared+C_init,
    C_cheap,nr <= eps/[2(1-2eps)] (L_shared+C_init). (3)
No unrestricted late energy upper bound follows unless the right hand side is controlled. L_shared<=A_shared<=24 E_+,off^full as before. More exactly only actual matched upward level incidences need to be counted, which could give a smaller upstream estimate. No claim that they all lie in the late evaluation window.

## 3. Small-birth and source-distance debit quotas

The previous truncated convex potential bound pays all C_pair at births with total vertex mass n_v<=m or d_v<=m for O(NBm). Recall sum b_v at n_v<=m<=2mN (meaning sum over these vertices at most 2mN), total b_v=O(NB) by source separation, and C_pair<=min(12B+6,1.5d_v).

NEW: use source separation even when BOTH n_v and d_v are large. Every born pair has original distance h=|M-M'|<=G/2<8B. Partition h into bands H_j/2<h<=H_j, H_j=8B/2^j, j>=0. At each pair's unique birth pay up to
    c_j=B m/[H_j(j+1)^2]
of its ACTUAL debit C_pair (possibly a fractional amount of a level). There are O(N H_j) possible ordered original pairs in a nonempty band. Therefore the sum paid over all bands is O(NBm), unconditionally, irrespective of birth mass, incoming label, return existence or later suffix duration. In particular all pairs with C_pair<=c_j are entirely paid even at a heavy high-deficit birth. This is not a time-tail estimate: it pays initial levels that are actually spent, plus the same-label entry correction, regardless of when they are spent. At an arbitrary actual pair one can charge this quota to any subset of initial-spent/entry level incidences, up to its cap. For the upper bound (2) it suffices to subtract the quota from the debit algebraically. It does not remove all later matched-upward contributions of a paid pair.

Use small-birth payment first, then source cap on remaining pairs, so no double count. With m=u^(5/2), sum of these charges is o(XB u³).

## 4. Mixed-label arithmetic and adaptive deficit quota

At a nonreset v with d_v>6, two distinct positive incoming labels p,q imply both divide J+2s and both divide (d_v-6s)/2, s=t mod2. Hence pq divides J+2s, 2pq divides d_v-6s>0. This holds also for p=3 by the congruence identity. Necessarily pq<=d_v/2. (The d_v<=6 case was paid by small-deficit budget.) There is NO J_pair correction for different labels. For fixed d band D_j/2<d_v<=D_j, D_j=(12B+6)/2^j, and unordered labels p<q, the number of possible labeled birth keys (v,p,q) is
    O(X D_j/(p²q²)).
Indeed for each parity there are O(D_j/(pq)) positive integers k with d_v=6s+2pq k in the band, and O(X/(pq)) possible even J satisfying J+2s=0 mod pq in [2X,7X), since pq=O(B). Imposing t=(J+d_v)/2 has parity s only reduces the count. In particular the total raw deficit over all such candidate keys is O(X D_j²/(p²q²)). These are relaxed arithmetic supports; no MIN reachability or ancestor mass assumed.

Let ell=1+log u, Y=max(3,ceil(B^1/8)), and define per mixed key cap on the ORDERED cross product 2 n_{v,p} n_{v,q},
    c(v,p,q)= B m pq / [D_j² (j+1)² h(p,q)],
    h(p,q)=1 if p,q>=Y, else ell².
Here n_{v,p} counts all incoming children of label p. All p/q cross pairs are born at v. Pay a uniform fraction f=min(1,c/(2 n_{v,p} n_{v,q})) of their remaining debit after earlier payments. Because each pair's debit<=d_v in this case, each key pays at most D_j c. Sum the arithmetic bound times this cap: for labels both>=Y, sum 1/p over [Y,B] is O(1); over all labels sum 1/p=O(ell). Therefore both groups have total paid <=O(XBm sum_j (j+1)^-2)=O(XBm). This uses only standard prime reciprocal upper bounds, no two-prime correlation or independence. The cap is adaptive in the deficit, incoming prime product, and deficit band. It can be less than 1; fractional payment still valid. When both p,q polynomial and pq is order B with d_v order B, it permits a cross mass of order m; for smaller deficits the permitted cap increases relative to the same prime product. A useful interpretation is that a mixed-label heavy birth surviving all these payments must have both significant actual debit and actual mass product exceeding the cap if its residual is positive. It is not a general upper bound on that product. For d_v<=6 all mixed debit is small-birth paid for asymptotic m>=6.

Together define C_rest by paying small birth first, source caps second, mixed fractional caps last. Then (2)-(3) use
    C_init = C_rest + O(XB u^(5/2)),
where equality here means the nonnegative difference has that upper bound. No double subtraction or independently masked linear clock loss. No bound on L_shared or unrestricted C_rest is asserted. A further useful direct inequality for same-label p with both full waits>=eps p and d_v<=Kp is C_pair<=1.5Kp, whereas full pair occupation energy at their common predecessor horizon r w_i/(2r) w_j/(2r)>=eps² p/4. Thus that entry debit can be paid by <=6K eps^-2 times the actual full same-label birth-pair occupation. This is only a conditional full-energy relation, not used in audit, and not a bound on same-label short waits with large d_v/p.

## 6. Remaining

The actual spent birth budget is much smaller than the raw one in finite data. The adaptive mixed-label arithmetic is unconditional but the largest finite residual is overwhelmingly same-label. Next focus on same-label different-pretime birth debit: exploit predecessor incoming labels, waiting meeting and actual minimum-deficit destination; also estimate only matched upward crossings rather than all full positive energy.

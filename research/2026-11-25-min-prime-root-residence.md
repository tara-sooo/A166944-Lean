## 2. Exact disjoint deficit-band residence decomposition

Use D_0=ceil(B/sqrt(u)) (min8), D_j=2^j D_0 through all possible deficits <=5H+6. Retain the low sector d<2D_0 separately. Partition the higher deficit hits into bands I_j=[2D_j,4D_j), so each hit belongs to just one band. For a fixed band j and real level lambda in (D_j,2D_j), restrict the smaller-source common recorded suffix to its maximal consecutive runs of states with d>lambda. Endpoints of level intervals have measure zero; one can use any consistent strict convention. Each actual hit of band I_j is above every such lambda and lies in exactly one run. The origin of a run is either the pair's first physical birth b (when the suffix starts above lambda), or the actual upward crossing edge v->w with d_v<lambda<d_w. A run stops before the next recorded vertex at or below lambda, or at the actual smaller-source stop. No incomplete terminal run is lost. Up/down oscillation does not give multiple origins to a hit at one level.

Let A_e(j,lambda,o) be the sum of ACTUAL w_e over hits in band I_j in the run with origin o. Then exactly
    E_e(I_j) = (1/D_j) integral_(D_j)^(2D_j) sum_o A_e(j,lambda,o) d lambda.
This is an exact positive allocation, with no signed cancellation. In particular the natural tail-weighted birth and upward drift terms of a1c35db, computed with the same actual pair weights, upper bound respectively D_j times the birth and ascent parts of this identity. They count the entire tail after birth/positive layer drift, whereas residence counts only that run and that band's hits. The exact per-pair tail identity is separately verified for these actual hit holes.

For fixed M,j,lambda and origin vertex (birth) or crossing edge (ascent), define the full ORIGINAL NATURAL residence envelope rho as twice the sum of h_M in band I_j from that vertex/edge target through its consecutive above-lambda run, up to its next downcross or actual stop. Then A_e<=rho<=3H. This envelope is independent of which larger partner is being considered, of its first meeting if the origin is a shared crossing, and of earlier payment masks. It depends on the actual deterministic future of the smaller source, not a relaxed arithmetic candidate. A zero envelope has zero actual contribution. For finite exact integration partition the lambda interval by all recorded deficit values; the run and its weights are constant in each open subinterval. Different pair histories may produce different refinements; the true proof and audit use the common refinement per origin whenever comparing budgets across partners.

## 3. Absolute adaptive ascent and birth rank budgets

### Shared ascent origins

At a completed actual positive-drift edge v->w on M, order all actual larger source companions at v by increasing source. For a shared pair let k be its position in that list, so at most m actual partners have k<=m. The larger partner traverses w by stop order. At a level lambda crossed upwards in (D_j,2D_j), pay the actual residence portion A_e(j,lambda,v->w) when
    k <= floor(3 L_H D_j/rho).
No minimum cap; ignore rho=0. This is a PARTIAL pair payment on a specified band/layer, not payment of its entire remaining suffix. At fixed source, edge, band and level, the total admitted actual weight is <=rho*floor(3 L_H D_j/rho)<=3 L_H D_j. Normalize by D_j and integrate. The length of the admissible upward layer interval is
    [f_j(d_w)-f_j(d_v)]_+,
    f_j(d)=min(D_j,(d-D_j)_+).
By the disjoint consecutive band partition of a1c35db, the sum of these lengths across all j at any one edge is <=(d_w-d_v)_+. Therefore all such payments have total
    <=3 L_H sum_(M,v->w) (d_w-d_v)_+
    <=15 L_H N H = O(F/u^.25).                       (1)
No summable band penalty is needed here, since the layer amplitudes are already disjoint. No factor for number of actual hits or for number of level bands; all source multiplicities are accounted for by the outer sum over original M and the admitted rank. This improves a fixed donor cap derived from rho<=3H: a smaller actual natural residence permits proportionally more partners, even for a nonhigh upward donor. Every unadmitted positive ascent residence satisfies k*rho>3 L_H D_j. This is a concrete joint rank/local-residence obstruction, not a claim that all thick donors obey a smaller product. 

Arithmetic on the actual donor: positive drift requires 2W>p-1, and W<=2r gives r>(p-1)/4. Thus for sufficiently large analytic X every positive-drift donor with p>=Q is itself a natural high event (Q->infinity and sqrt(log p)>5 eventually). Non-natural-high positive donors then have p<Q. At the finite diagnostic definition r>p/2, a non-natural-high donor need not have p<Q. The finite code class name smallLabel denotes NOT natural high, not a certified p<Q condition.

### Physical birth origins

Let n=n_M(b)=1+# larger actual original companions at birth vertex b of focal M, n_-=n_M(prev(b)) (use 1 at the original root). By persistence, n-n_- exactly counts the newly acquired actual larger partners with FIRST physical birth there. Sort just these new companions in increasing source, and let k_new be the given partner's rank. There is no physical birth with n=n_-; the prime source injection is singleton. Define g_M(b)=log(n/n_-)>0. Along each M,
    sum_(b) g_M(b) <= log N.
Use theta_j=(j+1)^(-2). For lambda with d_b>lambda pay the actual birth residence if
    k_new <= floor( H L_H theta_j g_M(b)/(u rho)).
Again at each fixed source/birth/band/level the paid total <=H L_H theta_j g_M(b)/u. The layer range has length <=D_j; normalized integration, summation over j and source/birth then give
    total <= (H L_H/u) (sum_j theta_j) N log N
          = O(N H L_H)=O(F/u^.25).                    (2)
This birth cap exploits the real newly joined cohort and log growth, not all partners in the current physical fibre, and is independent of the arithmetic birth key quota used earlier. It charges only the actual local residence portion, not necessarily every hit in that pair's entire history.

For a surviving birth residence k_new*rho > H L_H theta_j g/u. Since k_new<=n-n_- and log(n/n_-) >= (n-n_-)/n, a necessary condition is
    n*rho > H L_H theta_j/u.                          (3)
More sharply rho > H L_H theta_j g/[u(n-n_-)]. This restricts the unresolved first physical birth class by the actual local natural residence and rank, in addition to all prior CRT/phase/age conditions. It is not a uniform upper estimate of the product n*rho.

The absolute payments in (1)-(2) are disjoint across actual hit-band and level allocations (birth or ascent). They apply to arbitrary frozen actual retained weights and need no density gate recomputation or relative deletion. This provides a genuine absolute little-o budget for the stated adaptive small-product classes, without assuming any bound on the total mass of all thick physical fibres. The low deficit sector and async/medium are not covered by these payments.

## 4. Optional relative absorption of short ascent residences

This is separate from the absolute caps. For a particular shared donor edge v->w and pair e, suppose the donor itself has positive actual synchronous retained weight w_e(v)>0 and natural h_M(v)=r>0. If a run's actual band residence satisfies
    A_e(j,lambda,v->w) <= eps D_j * w_e(v)/r,
then the normalized layer payment is at most eps (w_e(v)/r) times the positive upward band length. Sum these short residence contributions over all j, using the same disjoint layer amplitudes, to get at most
    eps (w_e(v)/r) (d_w-d_v)_+ <=4 eps w_e(v).
Sum over actual pair donors. Each donor weight appears only once in this final bound. Thus the total short residence contribution is <=4 eps E_sync for the SAME frozen retained synchronous ledger. For eps<1/4 it can be absorbed by a factor (1-4 eps)^(-1) in an inequality for total synchronous energy after retaining the other classes. This is not an independent absolute o(F) payment, and is not a deletion in the numerical flow. Do not apply the relative bound to a masked-out donor (w_e(v)=0), even if it was naturally high, or replace actual residual donor energy by full natural energy and then claim residual absorption.

The finite diagnostic has a common pair fraction alpha at this stage and raw captured hit values of r or 2r. It uses the sufficient test raw band residence <=eps D_j and raw donor hit>0, which implies the displayed condition for alpha-scaled actual weights. No positive short relative class occurs in the tested final unpaid ledgers. The lemma is a paper bound; the audit's nonzero payment comes exclusively from the two absolute adaptive ranks.

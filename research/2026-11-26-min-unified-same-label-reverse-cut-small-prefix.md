## 2. Reverse grouping by actual common prefix, without pairs

Fix a destination key (E,p,q,c) and grid/sector. Take full original destination tagged events with that preceding label and arrival phase, including nonstable tags for an upper bound. For each such event f scan its original completed history backward, considering each possible source crossing e=(C,r,v) with posthorizon J=C+r-1<=E. Require E-J<L_s, p absent from the entire source-to-destination-exclusive selected label list, and R=lcm(L0, periods of that list)<=4H. The scan stops on the first violation because the suffix only grows; use b_l=P/a for a geometrically eligible low source label, b_h=B, or relax both to B. Each actual source atom K is a multiple of P in [C,J). Group by the exact source crossing (C,r), source phase v mod L0, and the entire actual intervening sequence of (label,wait) from AFTER source to BEFORE E. Do NOT require source event's own wait to agree: its postdeparture state is the start of the pursuit, and a source crossing can have different recorded input waits. The sequence key may be represented as a reverse trie. Each destination tag appears at most once per original source atom. Use the original phase-class population n of the source, not a renormalized destination subset.

Within a group G, let b count its full original tagged events, b_w count by destination wait, b_v by true destination departure, b_wv by both. The exact number of ORDERED candidate pairs with different waits AND different exits is
    A_G = b² - sum_w b_w² - sum_v b_v² + sum_(w,v) b_wv².       (1)
This is inclusion-exclusion for pairs, with diagonal automatically removed. Since their intervening sequences agree and their source phases agree, these pairs have unchanged lag up to E. Different waits with the same p give a first defect exactly at E, with nonperiodic lag. Distinct exits are necessary in the stable domain. In the relaxed group some pairs may fail original stability, focal masks, future room, or subsequent payments, which is harmless for the capacity. The exact eligible pairs before later pair-specific payments are obtained by using original stable source members in the group instead of all tags and assigning the original focal weights (zero for ineligible focal rows).

In detail let b^a, b_w^a, b_v^a, b_wv^a count only original stable source members, and W=sum_i w_i^s over them; W_w,W_v,W_wv are corresponding weighted counts. At one original source atom the exact same-label noncoincident clock before the later destination/pair payments is
    [W b^a - sum_w W_w b_w^a - sum_v W_v b_v^a
            + sum_(w,v) W_wv b_wv^a] / n.                    (2)
Divide by c_a for energy. Use weights at that atom, or cell totals W with n_k strict atoms and then sum once over the crossing. This is the exact reverse counterpart of the forward prefix tree cuts. It keeps the actual original n in the denominator and the original stable partner membership. It does not assert that arbitrary old pair-dependent masks factor into these counts. For residual support targets, (2) before such masks is a valid upper bound, and can include masks that factor in this grouping.

## 3. Pooled quadratic capacities, global comparison

Since b<=n and each focal weight per atom <=c_a P, the same-label energy from G is <=P A_G/n <=P A_G/b. For m_G=max(max_w b_w,max_v b_v), ordered candidate pairs cannot both belong to the maximum group, so A_G<=b²-m_G². Consequently
    P A_G/n <= P A_G/b
             <= P(b-m_G²/b)
             = P[2(b-m_G)-(b-m_G)²/b]
             <= 2P(b-m_G).                                  (3)
Also P A_G/b<=P b. This is a quadratic improvement of the previous per-tag two-role minority cover, even with no extra dilution by b/n. The normalized capacity P A_G/n gains another factor b/n compared with the pooled relaxed version. These estimates use ordered pairs directly, so no extra factor two for two roles.

For each fixed (E,p,q,c), sum over all groups/atoms in section 2 and average over the SAME original m grids with zero on unusable grids. Both new envelopes are <=the earlier weighted minority cover of e5d1157: choose its globally maximum-capacity wait or exit class and use 2P times the number outside that class at each group/atom, since every pair crosses its complement. Each original f is counted in at most the grid points of its accepted radical/pursuit intersection, so total 2P weight per f <= its previous U_f=2[min(G_rad(f),L_s+b_s)+P]. The same argument with P b proves the new envelope <=one half the earlier full U. The local group maxima in (3), or the exact inclusion-exclusion count, can improve further. This comparison applies to identical target phase refinements and sectors, including zero charges. It does not compare the sum of arbitrarily repeated relaxed two-label keys across all sectors.

There is also a direct GLOBAL normalized capacity bound, because the source groups encode entire common prefixes. For one grid use the union pursuit L_max=max of usable L_s, and include any original tags with the required completed word prefix, even without original stable room. Each ordered pair in one original source atom/phase class can be counted in at most one of all destination keys (E,p,q,c) across this pursuit: a first defect is unique; if selected label and waits are unequal earlier, the exact intervening prefix key cannot match at a later destination. It cannot have equal source physical slot and distinct waits at a later event by determinism. Counting the union once bounds the sum of BOTH sectors' actual charges, because sum_s w_i^s<=c_a P and the same original n is used. Thus the sum of the corresponding raw NORMALIZED capacities P A_G/n over all destination keys in the union is bounded by
    sum_(original source atoms/classes) P (n²-sum_v b_v²)/n
      <= N(H+P).
After grid averaging it is <=N(H+P_max)=(1+o(1))NT. This includes any additional allowed target restrictions, which only decrease the count. It is a genuine summed capacity estimate for the reverse common-prefix envelopes in energy units; in contrast the pooled P A_G/b envelopes have no such bound asserted. It does not yield the required coefficient <1/2 in clock units, or a small bound on B_rem. The previous log-record budget covered broader one-sided candidates without requiring common prefix grouping. Both routes remain useful.

Optional stable cohort compression: fix E and choose earlier anchor gain boundary E-D_s/2. Every actual source with a first defect at E lies in [E-D_s/3,E] eventually; both stability windows contain the whole anchor crossing to E postdeparture, and all original participating roots start before the anchor. Two eligible participants with the same actual destination recorded (t,v) must have the same actual anchor crossing, by the stable noncollision lemma of 3fe50cf6/f2a7cf2: if they first meet after distinct anchor departures, the smaller incoming branch contributes log2 to one of the two stable windows, contradiction. Determinism implies their common suffix includes their original preceding q and all relevant possible source crossings. Thus the actual eligible participants can be grouped by a single earlier actual anchor per recorded destination slot; full original tags executing that anchor and the destination form an upper cohort. This can compress reverse history grouping; formulas (1)-(3) do not need this compression and remain valid without it. No complexity claim about constructing all anchor cohorts.

## 4. Global small-prefix payment: a disjoint cut, not a union over possible destinations

Fix one original source atom/phase class and sector. Form the finite forward prefix tree of all original stable source words through the sector pursuit, inserting an intermediate LABEL node before each WAIT node. Thus children at a prefix node are selected prime labels, children at a label node are actual waits. A source slot has one actual word. No word is a proper prefix of another for the same pursuit stopping rule. Each node population b(U) is the number of original stable partner tags whose words pass through it, independent of focal weights. This includes zero-weight focal rows. The root mass <=original n. A differing-word ordered pair has exactly one first separating node: prefix node for different selected labels, label node for same label but different waits. In the actual stable domain the latter also has distinct departures; the upper estimate may ignore that additional restriction.

Choose eta=ell^(-1/4), or any eta->0. Pay all exact forward pair fractions whose first separating node has b(U)<=eta n. Stop every root-to-leaf branch at the FIRST node (prefix OR label node) with b<=eta n. These stopped groups are disjoint subsets of the original stable tags. Include the root if it already satisfies the threshold; paths never reaching a small node are ignored. Any pair paid under the rule lies in ONE common stopped group: its first separating node is common to both, and on the path to that node both have the same first-small ancestor. For group g of size b_g<=eta n, the total ordered energy of all such pairs at this source atom is <=P b_g²/n, using the old w_i^s<=c_a P. Therefore
    paid energy <= (P/n) sum_g b_g²
                 <= eta P sum_g b_g <= eta P n.             (4)
The sum counts each stopped group once, NOT one term per later small prefix or label or per possible defect label. Summing over original source classes, atoms, the two sectors and averaging over the unchanged m grids gives <=2 eta N(H+P_max)=o(NT). The constant 2 just allows separate sector cuts. Multiplication by c_a=O(1) preserves o(NT) in clock units. This pays only already remaining exact forward fractions, not any backward charges or original whole waits. The proof does not rely on large radical or late stopping, so it strengthens the earlier late-small-slot payment in a different direction. No assertion that the corresponding actual paid charge has a positive asymptotic proportion.

Consequently every remaining distinct-label first defect has a COMMON PREFIX population >eta n of original stable source tags, and every remaining same-label first defect has population >eta n in the common prefix followed by the SAME selected label (before the waits diverge). The latter is precisely the stable group b^a in the reverse classification of section 2. At a retained original good source, the old class threshold n>H/(A r u ell²) for source selected label r therefore forces
    b^a > eta H/(A r u ell²).                                  (5)
All these tags actually execute the common prefix and next selected p at E (or are at the common prehorizon E in the distinct-label case). Thus the destination refined full population b must exceed this bound in the same-label case; in the distinct-label case the common-prehorizon actual population is large, not necessarily each chosen label population. This lower bound is on the common group, not on both individual waiting subclasses. Late pairs additionally retain the earlier two-large-source-slot condition; neither mask replaces the other.

## 5. Updated sufficient objective

Keep D'_same and D'_distinct after the new small-prefix payment. They are no larger than their earlier exact counterparts and differ in total by o(NT). The reverse normalized capacity (or smaller exact weighted reverse cut with admissible targets) can restrict the same-label supports further, by comparison to the old predecessor-CRT absolute cutoff H u^nu/(u ell³); this does not change any previous payment. Under finitely many twins
    NT/2-o(NT) <= B_rem+(3+o(1))(D'_same+D'_distinct).
Thus liminf [B_rem+3(D'_same+D'_distinct)]/(NT)<1/2 suffices, implied by the previous exact residual test. The 5/2 versus 2 raw occupancy sufficient test of a3f065b remains applicable on the smaller allowed cuts. The new contribution is a global O(eta NT) payment with common-prefix mass threshold, and more precise reverse normalized capacities with a uniform summed bound, not a proof of a strict constant-margin upper bound. The remaining backward and heavy-prefix forward estimates are open. No Lean compilation. Main and independent branches not modified.

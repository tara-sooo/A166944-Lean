## 1. Fixed measures

Original roots M prime in [X,2X), origin C0=2M-2; completed original gain-stopped MIN paths, g=p-1, wait w<=2r<=3g, total gain <H, true-state deterministic evolution. Grids P_j, m=Theta(u/ell), a=u^.25, sharp good-cell capacity c_a=3(1+2/a), common L0 even primorial<=ell, A=L0/2, tau>=1/log ell ->0. Low usable grids use L_l=ceil(H A P/(a P_max ell tau²)); high uses L_h=ceil(B exp(1/tau)). All original stable source tags have the appropriate future room. Let B_rem=B_wait,rem and D_rem=D_all,rem of 0bc6ab1. No source normalization or old payment is changed. Under finitely many twins NT/2-o(NT)<=B_rem+(3+o(1))D_rem.

At an original source crossing and each crossed boundary atom K, in original phase class c of full size n, let w_i^s be sector s actual retained assigned focal clock divided uniformly across atoms, w_i=sum_s w_i^s<=c_a P. For stable tag i its word omega_i is the actual continuation through first gain crossing L_s (sector-specific). ## 2. Defect renewal gap: an unconditional sharper destination capacity

For an ordered pair actually routed to a first defect, initial lag Delta between the two true source exits is nonzero, and its period is divisible by L0 and 2q, with q the source label. Until first defect both actual paths follow the identical selected labels AND waits. Every such translated selected label r has 2r|Delta by the actual exit congruence. At the first defect at common prehorizon E, choose exactly the old nonperiodic side: focal if its actual selected label p has 2p not dividing Delta, otherwise the partner. Two-way MIN comparison guarantees that if the focal selected period divides Delta then the partner selected period cannot also divide Delta (the two completed candidates would force identical label and wait). Thus the chosen actual event's label p is absent from the entire unchanged translated prefix and is also different from q. This is an actual word assertion, not a divisibility-based sufficient condition for the whole trajectory to translate.

Fix an original destination tag and event e with prehorizon E and selected p. Let J_p^- be the posthorizon of its last completed event labelled p before e on that SAME original tag, or C0 of the tag if no such event exists. Any source crossing whose contribution is routed to this tag/event must be strictly after that previous p event in its original event order, because p cannot occur either at the source or in the translated prefix before e. Therefore K>=J_p^- and K<=E. Let G_p(e)=E-J_p^- >=0 be this renewal gap. From the old pursuit constraint, K>=E-L_s-b_s where b_l=P/a for low good sources and b_h=B for high sources (both may be relaxed to B). The low bound also holds when the chosen destination side is the partner, because the shared source label q<=P/a is guaranteed by the low focal geometry. Hence the original source boundaries on this tag lie in the intersection
    [J_p^-,E] intersect [E-L_s-b_s,E].
In either ordered role each crossed original atom is used at most once by that tag and has total pair coefficient <=1 in energy units. Counting grid points in that intersection gives
    D_(P,s)(tag,e) <= 2 [min(G_p(e), L_s+b_s)+P].       (1)
The two roles are included. The same proof gives a combined two-sector cap on the same grid by replacing the lengths by their maximum, because the joint kernel has a common column capacity; sector-specific bounds can still be tighter when summed at an event only receiving one sector. This is not a bound on actual wait.

Define at a projected destination (E,p) the explicit renewal capacity
    U_s(E,p)=2 mean_P sum_(original tag events e at E,p)
                                [min(G_p(e), L_s+b_s)+P],
with zero on unusable grids for that sector, and the common denominator m. Then Dbar_s(E,p)<=U_s(E,p), pointwise also after old payments. Each summand is <=the old 2(L_s+b_s+P) cap. The previous absolute thin-charge thresholds remain applicable: if U_s(E,p) is at most the old threshold then no residual charge at this key survives. This permits construction of a smaller admissible projected target superset for the joint estimate without knowing exact pairs. More generally cap the contribution to any selected set by sum of these capacities, or take its minimum with the tree bound below.

There is a global renewal estimate for one fixed label p: along one tag the intervals [J_p^-,E] for successive events labelled p are disjoint, and their total length <=H. Hence sum of min(G_p(e),L_s+b_s) over all such events on the tag <=H. The P overhead costs at most P times their count, which is <=H/(p-1) on a tag. This is an upper bound per label, not a summable global estimate over all p. No inference of o(NT) from it. The genuine gain in (1) is when actual same-label recurrences, or the root-to-first-occurrence distance, are short compared with the pursuit length. No claim that all destinations have short recurrence.

## 3. Forward prefix tree: exact label-specific cut without enumerating tag pairs

At one original crossing atom/phase class and sector s construct a trie of the actual stable slot words of length defined by first gain crossing L_s. Its vertices are common prefixes; a child is labelled by the actual next edge (p,w). No word is a strict prefix of another because gain is determined by the selected labels and both stop at the same threshold. At a node U with common prehorizon E, all tags in the subtree have the same projected horizon and share the same sequence of waits since source, so their current arrival time differences equal their source departure time differences. For each child x let p_x be its label, W_x=sum w_i^s over focal tags in it, and N_x count all stable partner tags in it. Include zero-weight focal slots as potential partners. These counts are from actual one-slot histories, not from pairs. A pair of different words is separated at exactly one node, its lowest common prefix; ordered pairs with tags in distinct children are precisely the first defects at that node.

For ordered distinct children x,y define
    T_xy = W_x N_y,
    E_xy = sum_(r mod 2p_x) W_x(r) N_y(r),
where the counts are subdivided by their SOURCE departure v modulo 2p_x. Then T_xy-E_xy is exactly the weighted pair mass for which the focal side x is nonperiodic. For E_xy the focal is periodic, and the partner must be nonperiodic by the same two-way MIN lemma. Thus for any sector-specific set of projected admissible destination keys S_s, write I_x=1_{(E,p_x) in S_s} and I_y analogously. The EXACT forward clock restricted to that target and before later pair-specific masks is
    F_tree(S_s)= (1/n) sum_nodes sum_(ordered x!=y)
                         [ I_x (T_xy-E_xy) + I_y E_xy ].     (2)
There is no overcount even when both labels are admissible. In particular two children with the same label but different waits cannot have any pair with lag divisible by that period (then both would be periodic). Formula (2) permits distinct label-specific targets rather than projecting both selected labels onto the same prehorizon, as required in 0bc6ab1's intersection bound. The sparse residue sums can be computed without matching individual tags; this does not guarantee a speedup for arbitrary huge tries or all target construction costs.

A prefix radical can further simplify (2): R_U=lcm(L0,2q,2r for labels r in the prefix). Any source exit difference among slots at this node is divisible by R_U. If R_U>4H only one actual slot remains; if 2p_x|R_U, E_xy=T_xy for all y, and x cannot be a chosen nonperiodic destination. At most one child at a branching node has its period dividing R_U, again by two-way MIN comparison of completed candidates. This is the trie version of the renewal restriction. It uses exact integer lcm; a capped implementation treats >4H only as a flag, not as an actual divisor.

If targets include every destination then (2) equals the full differing-word clock sum. If targets are projected admissible supersets after old thin payment and (1), then (2) is an upper bound on the exact residual forward clock. No claim that the same formula incorporates arbitrary tag-dependent old pair masks without additional bookkeeping.

## 4. Backward reverse tree and pooled long waiting episodes

At each full physical source slot G of size b, all source members have a common true state just before source exit. Trace their actual true histories to it. The induced merge tree has unique first meeting U for each unordered distinct pair, at which its two distinct incoming branches are x,y. It is a tree directed toward the common endpoint, including injection as singleton branch, because original continuing trajectories are deterministic. For each node count b_x of source members in branch x and W_x^s=sum w_i^s of those members. Weights can be zero for any masked focal source. Its original compatible fraction a_omega/n is fixed per source slot and sector. The resampled compatible pair weight is w_i^s a_omega/(n b). Therefore the exact backward clock before pair-specific masks for a set of allowed first-meeting nodes and unordered branch pairs is
    B_tree = sum_source slots, sectors [a_omega/(n b)]
                  sum_allowed U,{x,y} (W_x^s b_y + W_y^s b_x).      (3)
No diagonal is included. This is a disjoint partition by first meeting, not a count of all ancestors. The old relative parent/branch population tests depend on these induced tree counts, and can be incorporated directly. The old depth condition depends on U and source horizon. The common-anchor membership mask can be included by counting only the original eligible members in the branch sums (keep b and original parent/branch-size tests frozen). Root gap mask may be omitted for an upper bound, or its exclusion counted using weighted sorted root lists for two branches. Similarly old refined waiting-arrival keys can be tested by further partitioning the incoming wait branch according to the actual previous arrival and label; the branch-size test still uses the full original induced incoming branch, not a refined subpopulation. If old destination masks depend on the larger chosen incoming branch, use the same branch-size comparison/tie rule; for arbitrary remaining pair predicates it is valid to relax them to allowed supersets. Formula (3) is exact for the stated node/branch pair predicates; it is NOT an assertion that the finite audit below exercises all possible masks or all birth types.

For the formerly dominant long-age wait-jump sector, there is a simpler sum not requiring explicit true ticks or all ancestor nodes. Inside G consider an earlier completed waiting episode V=(J,q,v), partition its members from G by their actual recorded arrival times t_1<...<t_d. Let b_i and W_i^s be group counts and focal weights; b_i^J,W_i^{s,J} count just those arriving by a jump (not root injection). Any two tags in that episode with different arrival times first meet exactly when the later one arrives: if they had already met earlier, deterministic continuation would give identical arrival times. They both wait until the common departure v. Thus the first meeting is long-age wait-jump iff t_j-t_i>R and the later arrival is by jump. For chosen episodes or true first-meeting horizons passing the target test, the exact clock of these ordered pairs is
    [a_omega/(n b)] sum_j [
       b_j^J sum_(i:t_i<t_j-R) W_i^s
       + W_j^{s,J} sum_(i:t_i<t_j-R) b_i ].                  (4)
A true birth target depending on t_j or the later jump label can be incorporated by selecting the appropriate later-arrival subgroup, without changing the disjointness of earlier groups. Distinct labels within an arrival time can be subdivided similarly if necessary. The two inner cumulative sums can be computed by a single increasing pointer after sorting arrival times, so the summation step is linear in number of distinct arrival groups. This is stronger than bounding all pairs by (1) of 0bc6ab1: it counts actual surviving source membership and actual assigned focal weights, not merely upper lifetime counts. It still requires tracing each source tag's history, and is not a scale-free bound on total charge.

## 5. Combined upper bound and remaining analytic goal

Sum (2) and (3) over all original source classes/atoms/grids, using both sectors' original weights and the same m denominator. Use (4) for the long-wait subset of (3), not an additional term. Let S_s contain all possible residual forward keys, for example by using the old support tests and new renewal capacity (1); let allowed backward nodes/branch pairs contain all residual true birth keys, by the previous arithmetic/lifetime/branch masks. Define Q_tree as the sum of the two resulting upper measures in clock units.

Then
    B_rem+c_a D_rem <= Q_tree <= Qbar(T_B,T_D),                (5)
where T_B consists of the allowed true birth states and T_D the projection of allowed S_s to prehorizons, in the reachability bound of 0bc6ab1. For the second inequality, every backward counted pair is within the old A kernel and both histories visit its allowed state with the old depth restriction (include that restriction in (3)); every forward counted pair is within the F kernel and both histories have an event at the selected prehorizon within their respective pursuit lengths. The exact same original source weights and sector common-word argument apply. The diagonal omitted from (3) was already paid in the exact residual. This is a pointwise strengthening of the previous direct upper bound for matched target sets, not a new logically weaker sufficient criterion than the exact residual condition. Unlike binary reachability, it excludes every pair whose first meeting/first defect is outside the target even if both histories visit some target independently. In particular no union over all visits of a tag is required.

Under finitely many twins NT/2-o(NT)<=Q_tree. Thus proving liminf Q_tree/(NT)<1/2 for complete admissible supersets would close this route. Neither the combined constant margin nor a uniform analytic bound for the distribution of renewal gaps or marked tree cuts has been established. The new upper bound is directly evaluable from one-tag trajectories, grouped weights and target predicates; its magnitude in the required asymptotic regime is the remaining issue. No claim that finite diagnostic ratios establish it. All old exact residual measures are unchanged; no claim that the new capacities remove a positive constant fraction. Main and independent source branches were not modified. No Lean compilation.

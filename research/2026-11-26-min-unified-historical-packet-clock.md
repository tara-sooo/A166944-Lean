New: an alternative upper envelope of the whole backward clock with the old individual maturity strips and a static historical packet partition. Its eligible-only regularized complete multipartite normalization is superadditive at arbitrary merges and monotone at individual eligibility insertion; its terminal retirement surplus is o(NT). Thus positive merge dilution is absorbed by simultaneous cross-pair activation in THIS relaxed graph, and the sufficient criterion has only nonnegative credits. We explicitly relax old pair-specific ages, actual-large to historical-large, and other static pair/rank masks; cannot claim the same sign in XIII's exact static graph or a monotone improvement over it. No strict final contraction or Lean verification.

## 1. Fixed historical packets encode the historical-large condition

Keep original ensemble and original full actual/historical component forest (including retired tags as abstract members only). Let L_old=floor(B^(1-ell^(-1/2)/4)) be original actual-large incoming branch threshold from ed06617/23a3d09; do not confuse it with persistent witness length L. Let dmax=ceil(20u/ell)+2 as XI. At each actual true vertex merge incoming historical root sets, distinct components. A component is small when historical size <L_old. Once a union reaches size >=L_old, it stays large in the historical forest. At each vertex whose union is large, put ALL incoming small histories (if any) in ONE new packet; existing packets of large incoming histories are preserved without change. At a final historical component still small, put all its members in one packet. These are disjoint sets of original roots, irrespective of retirement. Every packet has size <=P=dmax L_old, since only small incoming histories are placed in a new packet, number of actual incoming branches bounded by dmax (root injection included). A retired member is assigned a color but is never extended as an actual path.

For any original pair that really meets, its two original historical incoming components at its FIRST true meeting are distinct. If both are small, they are united while small (later enter the same packet), or are put into the same new packet at that vertex. If at least one is large, its already fixed packet is distinct from the other history's packets or newly assigned packet. Therefore the historical condition "at least one incoming history has size >=L_old" is EXACTLY inequality of packet labels for pairs that meet. Pairs which never meet need not have this characterization and never enter a common physical cohort. The old actual-large condition implies the historical one, not conversely. Original true first-meeting can be inside a wait, not just at common completed exit. This construction uses full historical forest, not an induced survivor forest. In particular it remains valid for a static pair whose historical first meeting predates its first eligible arrival.

## 2. Regularized eligible-only graph is a valid upper envelope

Keep the original individual eligibility interval E_i=[M_i^++h,F_i-h) or empty, including original non-Bad, nonmoving and individual maturity conditions, h=eta H. Write a_i its left endpoint and e_i=F_i-h. Relax all other pair conditions to distinct packets and same actual completed arrival edge. This does NOT preserve original pair-specific first-birth age or induced rank tests; the graph is deliberately larger. Every surviving old pair lies in it, since old true birth age >>B ensures same complete arrival edge. No new root trajectories or actual extension. At every x of an actual arrival edge, let n be number of individually eligible tags there, with packet counts m_c summing to n. Let
    A=n²-sum_c m_c²,
    a_R=A/(n+R), zero for n=0,
    R=sqrt(P b0) (round to a positive integer).
The original b0 from the frozen endpoint ledger satisfies P=o(b0): L_old/B <=exp(-u/(4 sqrt(ell))), dmax=O(u/ell), whereas b0>=B/u^O(1) (see ed06617/23a3d09 parameters; L0<=ell, tau<=1 eventually). Hence P/R,R/b0=o(1). This is a NEW regularizing denominator, not the cutoff max(b0,b) of old kernels. It is not claimed to dominate pointwise without an error.

After whole nonnegative integral unfreezing as XII-XIII, its denominator is d0=max(b0,b_exit), where b_exit is full original physical exit mass. Since n<=b_exit and original pair numerator A_old<=A, we have
    A_old/d0 <= A/(n+R) + (R/b0)n.                     (1)
Indeed if n+R<=d0 immediate; otherwise difference on A_old is <=A_old R/[d0(n+R)]<=Rn/b0. Integrate with omega<=3, sum n gain <=NH, error O((R/b0)NH)=o(NT). All old domains and errors unchanged. Thus W_B<=V_R+o(NT) for full backward envelope, V_R=sum_edges integral a_R omega; set U_R=sum integral a_R. U_R<=L_ind=sum_i |E_i|<=NH. More exactly uncovered eligible gain is integral(n-a_R)=integral [nR+sum m_c²]/(n+R). No hard gate on n; smoothing avoids the derivative jump of max(b0,n).

## 3. Merge and individual-eligibility activation are monotone

At every actual completed merge, the eligible incoming populations are disjoint subsets of the outgoing eligible tags (process expiring tags first and individual starts last). On an arbitrary fixed color system, define n_j, m_jc and diversity F_j=n_j-sum_c m_jc²/n_j for nonempty incoming populations. For n=sum n_j, weighted Cauchy-Schwarz gives
    sum_c (sum_j m_jc)²/n <= sum_j sum_c m_jc²/n_j.
Consequently F_out>=sum_j F_j. Since n/(n+R)>=n_j/(n_j+R) and F_j>=0,
    a_R(out) = n/(n+R) F_out >= sum_j a_R(in_j).       (2)
This works EVEN IF packet colors overlap across incoming arrival cohorts. No need to locate packet formation at a completed exit or to assume colors disjoint. The previous dilution and immediate cross activation together have nonnegative change. Deficit is common at actual poststate (t,J), so their combined signed contribution to 2V_R is NONPOSITIVE. This is a property of the complete multipartite relaxation, not of arbitrary static pair graphs.

At individual eligibility insertion, add one member of a packet with old population m, current n, A=n²-sum m_c². Exact increment
    Delta a_R=[2(n-m)(n+R)-A]/[(n+R)(n+R+1)].
Using A<=n²-m² proves Delta a_R>=(n-m)(n-m+2R)/[(n+R)(n+R+1)]>=0.
Using m<=P and A<=n² gives the further lower bound
    Delta a_R >= 1-(R/(n+R))²-(2P+1)/(n+R+1).          (3)
For starts with n>=ell R before insertion, this is 1-o(1) uniformly. At simultaneous starts use any fixed order, count each tag once. Root injection, if individually eligible already, can be treated as a singleton insertion; original positive strips actually exclude it. Let C_merge=sum d_v [a_R(out)-sum a_R(in)]>=0 before individual starts; C_start=sum d_i(a_i) Delta a_R>=0. In particular
    C_start >= (1-o(1)) sum_{dense individual starts} d_i(a_i).   (4)
The d at interior of event is linear interpolation, not actual raw wait tick; this is exactly the integration variable of the clock upper. There is no independent lower bound on dense start count or deficit. Activation of pair-specific ages is not being counted here; those conditions were relaxed.

## 4. Uniform retirement surplus bound without a hard gate

At a virtual retirement on an arrival edge let n=s+k, k eligible tags retire, survivor packet counts m'_c<=m_c. Write S2=sum m_c², S2'=sum (m'_c)². Since
    a_R(n,m)=n-R+(R²-S2)/(n+R),
the excess of the drop over k is
    delta-k=R²[1/(n+R)-1/(s+R)] - S2/(n+R)+S2'/(s+R)
           <= k S2'/[(s+R)(n+R)]
           <= k P/(n+R) <= k P/R.                     (5)
This includes s=0 (then surplus nonpositive). The drop itself is nonnegative by insertion monotonicity in reverse. No assumption that retired tags are evenly distributed across colors or origin ranks. Weight by nonnegative actual interpolated deficit at expiry, sum each eligible tag exactly once. Let Z_elig=sum_{nonempty E_i} d_i(e_i), R_ret=sum d delta and S_R=R_ret-Z_elig. From (5), S_R<= (P/R) Z_elig=o(NT) using d<=6+5H, P/R=o(1). Retain exact signed S_R if useful. Let Z_not=sum_{empty E_i} d_i(F_i). Slope>=-1 gives Z_elig+Z_not<=Z_all+Nh. Unlike XIII full virtual survival graph, terminal sum includes ONLY individually eligible tags; terminal deficits of all others remain a credit. No terminal accounting across multiple copies of graph.

## 5. New sufficient criterion with no positive change terms

All edge weights a_R piecewise constant, d continuous piecewise affine with 2omega=1+d'. Exact summation over the actual arrival-edge graph gives
    2V_R = U_R - C_merge - C_start + Z_elig + S_R.      (6)
Use eligible starts after merging, expire before merging, each physical edge's boundary values cancel with actual successor values except changes. No virtual ghost trajectories. This exact identity is for the new relaxed upper, NOT the original backward clock or XIII's earlier graph. Combining (1), (5), exact total clock 2W_all=G_all+Z_all-6N, finite-twin G_all>=NT, and unchanged original forward upper 2c_a(D_l+D_h) gives
    NT-o(NT) <= U_R - C_merge - C_start - Z_not
                                      +2c_a(D_l+D_h). (7)
Sufficient strict normalized liminf of [U_R-C_merge-C_start-Z_not+6(D_l+D_h)]/(NT)<1. One may retain exact S_R and Z_elig+Z_not-Z_all for sharper finite criterion. In particular a coarser sufficient criterion using only original tagwise eligible lengths is
    liminf [L_ind-C_merge-C_start-Z_not+6(D_l+D_h)]/(NT)<1.
Write Q_R=L_ind-U_R>=0; under finite twins L_ind<=NH, so it suffices to prove a fixed positive normalized gap between (NH-L_ind)+Q_R+C_merge+C_start+Z_not and 6(D_l+D_h). These are explicitly nonnegative credits; no separately uncontrolled positive M or S_ret remains in THIS route. However age/rank/static relaxations can make U_R much larger than the earlier U_graph, and no comparison in either direction with XIII's complete objective is claimed. Take minimum of valid whole-clock upper bounds, not sum of their credits. In particular P=o(b0) does NOT imply a fixed positive packet-loss share. This is a genuine new sufficient scalar, no strict constant established. Need evaluate original individual maturity intervals, forward terms and dense start deficit; can also seek a sharper denominator/packet structure retaining further old pair masks.

## 6. Latest direct variation-cover restriction, same actual eligible intervals

Direct 9101eac strengthens the prior repeated-cofactor criterion to moving adjusted strata. With XI's enlarged cube threshold A3 and actual expensive w>=g/16, q is prime, w>6, so direct spacing/variation proof applies without needing its original w>=g/4. On ANY actual contiguous complete-event block in an individual's eligible interval, chosen cube-certified (possibly actual hybrid-crossing) labels p>=A3, filtered consecutive cofactor half-gaps m=2,3,4 have ordinary post-to-post variation>=2p-6. Direct depth-two cover gives total p-weight of these short returns <=(16+o(1)) times V_rel, where V_rel sums |d'-d| on all block events with |d'-d|>g/8. Under finite twins m=1 disappears for large X (both prime cofactors >=A3). Therefore the total original waits of endpoints of short returns in these chosen lists is O(V_rel), uniformly in support size. For general cutoff K, stratum-changing m<=K returns have p-weight O(K V_{>1/(2K)}). Aggregate along each original tag's actual eligible disjoint blocks, no multiplication by packet or birth count. Since a_R<=n, the same actual focal wait bound also upper-bounds the corresponding part of the relaxed graph clock if the selections are made per physical ray and each eligible focal tag executes all the needed return intervals inside its eligibility. Otherwise restrict to those tags explicitly. A small total relative variation budget would pay these returns, not all certified visits. Direct adaptive eight-support moving-stratum inequality also applies to actual long eligible subblocks: high selected gain above 2G/3 forces twin cofactor or weighted discovery/variation excess. No small relative variation or bounded support hypothesis follows merely from (7); this is a separate direct supply restriction, not a proof of the remaining gap.

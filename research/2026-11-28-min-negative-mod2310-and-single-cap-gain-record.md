## 1. Universal wait/gain inequality, strengthened twice

For a complete event starting at even center x and signed clock n (birth or immediately after preceding event), write p for the winning odd prime, w for its positive wait, g=p-1, target (x-g,n+w). Actual w<=2p by the verified first-event calendar bound. For a specified odd prime p eligible spatially, the next clock has residue 0 mod 2p if p|x+1, or p+2 mod 2p if p|x-1. These alternatives are disjoint for odd p. The restricted priority among a set of primes is lexicographic by (wait,p). An actual p in that set must be the restricted winner; an actual p larger than all of that set must fire strictly earlier than its winner, if one exists.

Previous mod30 certificate (2021e5a, research/min_local_negative_mod30_gain_potential_audit.mjs) gives W<=9G/4+8 for arbitrary contiguous complete-event blocks. Improve this using Q=210 and exact priority of 3,5,7. ## 3. Whole-root deletion of slow top events under an arbitrary physical cap

This is a new uniform bound using the independently derived two-form Selberg upper sieve in 0e9b47b, section 3, rather than the old multi-form GoodFieldEstimate. Use synchronized windows of width W=6 floor(T/6), T=o(R), canonical roots N~R/6. All selected prefixes (including pre-observation) have age <=1.5T and gain <=3T+6 by the checked core. FullCapped means <=M live roots at any physical state per window. Each site/key can be visited by at most an absolute number of windows: roots satisfying x<=A<=x+3T+6 span O(T) spatial width. In what follows M is any positive integer; constants do not grow with M.

Two choices of horizon:
 (I) original T~sqrt R exp(-sqrt L), L=log R;
 (II) enlarged T~sqrt R/L^B for any fixed B>0.
Set Y=floor(T/(log L)^a), fixed 0<a<=1, U=floor(R^delta), fixed 0<delta<1/4. Call a completed event TOP if p>=Y, and slow TOP if its FULL wait from previous event or birth is >=U. For all large R, U<<Y. An event that occurs on the transition out of the last live state need NOT be counted as completed for this proof. Only those with arrival still live are counted, ensuring source state cap applies.

For a slow TOP key (source x, firing clock n, winner p), both neighbors x+-1 have all odd prime factors >=U/2, since full wait is <=2q for each such factor. The source is in one spatial class for each of two signs x+-1 divisible by p. Write the corresponding neighbor as pr, then r and pr+-2 are free of primes <=U/4. Since p in [Y,3T+7], r in an interval of length O(R/p), with log(R/p) asymptotic to L/2. Sift these two forms by odd primes up to min((R/p)^(1/12),U/4). They have distinct forbidden residues (determinant +-2, p larger than cutoff); independent Selberg bound O(R/(p L^2)) uniformly in p. Floor and endpoint cases absorbed, r>1 for large R. For fixed source and sign, firing clock n lies in an interval of length O(T) from the pre-firing height and in a single residue mod2p. Hence O(1+T/p)=O(T/p) possible keys. Chebyshev dyadic prime counting gives sum_{p>=Y}p^-2=O(1/(Y log Y)); therefore
    # slow TOP physical keys <= C RT/(Y L^3)
                            = O(R (log L)^a/L^3).          (5)
This counts possible keys, not just observed ones, and is uniform in selection. Cap at each source previous tick, and constant window overlap, imply at most C M roots per key. Charge each root hitting any slow TOP key to its first such key. Delete EVERY selected root whose prefix (with arrival live) contains one, setting its death age to zero. This preserves FullCapped and all other deterministic trajectories. Crucially it bounds the entire lost observed lifespan, not just time spent at the slow keys:
    # removed <= C M R (log L)^a/L^3,
    lost V <= C M NT (log L)^a/L^3.                         (6)
We do not claim this is a bound on all uncapped roots or on all deaths of a greedy policy.

Every retained TOP event has full wait <U and there are at most Ktop=floor((3T+6)/(Y-1))=O((log L)^a) by gain. Sum TOP waits <=N Ktop U=o(NT L^-C0) for any fixed C0. Delete TOP events from the event list only for accounting, not from the actual evolution; the low events split into at most Ktop+1 contiguous actual complete-event blocks. By (2), their total wait <=(17/8)G_{<Y}+(395/12)(Ktop+1) per root. The total additive block error is o(NT L^-C0) as well.

## 4. Terminal idle contribution and a simpler uniform analytic bridge

For each retained root with positive observed life, take its actual last live age. Let W be the time of its last completed firing (0 if none). The observed live ticks are at most W plus the observed portion of the final idle run plus one (birth/indexing). This overcounts pre-observation completed waits, which is harmless for an upper bound. Split the final observed idle into short (<H0) and long (>=H0), choosing H0=floor(T/L^4). Short mass <=N H0. Every long phase center lies in the rough set with both neighbor least factors >=H0/2. Count only its final idle ticks while selected alive: at most M per site/clock/window; O(1) windows per site. Thus long idle mass <=C MT |S_H0|. This does not assume the same roots stay alive through retirement, and also includes death during a phase. For horizon (I), previous independent sieve gives
    |S_H0| <= twinCount(I)+O(R/L^2.5).
For (II), use the same semiprime decomposition: both neighbors have at most two prime factors with multiplicity; composite side has a prime q in [H0/2,sqrt(2R+1)]. For fixed q, r and qr+-2 sift to O(R/(q L^2)) with cutoff (R/q)^(1/12)<H0/2. Chebyshev dyadic sum over q has size O((1+log(sqrt(2R+1)/H0))/L)=O(log L/L), so
    |S_H0| <= twinCount(I)+O_B(R log L/L^3).                 (7)
Here I=[R-3T-6,2R]; use H0/4 as sieve threshold to avoid boundary equality. No lower sieve and no multi-form transfer hypotheses.

Combine (2),(5)-(7), the deletion loss, and block/top errors. For a policy d let V(d)=liveTicks, and define an additive nonnegative state cost
    inc_Y(s)=17*(winner(s)-1) if 0<winner(s)<Y, else 0,
with reward D=8 per observed tick. The EXACT existing rootNet/policyNet convention counts pathCost through prefixAge=min(deathAge,observeAge+T), including the outgoing tick of the last live state. This cost dominates 17 times the low gain of events completed with live arrivals, so the upper bound remains valid even if that final outgoing tick fires. Costs of deleted roots are nonnegative. Write P(d)=policyNet inc_Y 8 R T d. Then, uniformly for FullCapped d and M>=1,
    P(d)/(8MNT) <= C twinCount(I)/N + O(L^-2.5)             (I)
    P(d)/(8MNT) <= C twinCount(I)/N + O_B(log L/L^3)        (II)
because NH0/(MNT)<=L^-4, (6) normalized is O((log L)^a/L^3), and remaining errors are superpolylog-small. This is a new paper-level UNCONDITIONAL uniform upper bridge for this DIFFERENT additive objective. The old good-field upper still has its independent multi-form/terminal review obligation; that is not needed by this new bridge. No positive asymptotic lower for this objective follows from the upper.

## 5. Reuse the exact kernel-verified optimizer; remove growing cap as a requirement

The generic checked theorems optimalNetDeath_fullCapped, optimalNetDeath_optimal, optimalNetDeath_formula and optimalNet_record_sharp apply directly with inc_Y and D=8 (no new state-cost solver, no history debt approximation). Define
    P*_M = max_{FullCapped d} P(d)
          = sum_{physical observed bins b} sum_{t=0}^7 min(M,m_{b,t}),
where m_{b,t} counts FULL paths with recordProfit>t in that bin. This identity is finite and exact by the existing core; no new Lean run verifying sections 1-4 or the application wrapper. The record profit at any tick is an integer from 0 to 8. For M=1 the inner sum equals the maximum record profit among roots in that bin (0 for empty). Moreover min(M,m)/M<=min(1,m) for any M>=1. Thus P*_M/M<=P*_1, and P*_M/M is nonincreasing in M. The uniform upper bridge has exactly the same M scaling, so within this route no growing cap or second-moment upper bound is required to state the sufficient lower target: take M=1. In (II) it suffices to prove, along unbounded R,
    P*_1/(8NT) = omega(log L/L^3),                         (8)
or more precisely an unbounded limsup of the ratio. Equivalently since positive record values are 1..8, the count of physical bins with at least one positive full-path record increment needs to be omega(NT log L/L^3). This differs from the previous G_M/(NT)>>L^-.25 condition and is NOT asserted to be mathematically easier merely because the exponent is larger. At M~L^2.1, the new upper residual in (II) is O(log L/L^.9) after normalization by 8NT. At the original horizon it is O(L^-.4). A lower of L^-.25 would suffice in either case. In both cases the required analytic upper is now elementary two-form sieve plus (1), not the old history-based scans.

A direct lower control: if a slab center A is canonical and all odd factors of A+-1 exceed 3T+7, its full trajectory through retirement is stationary by checked height label bound. It incurs zero cost and contributes 8T to the M=1 optimizer at its own site (all such sites distinct, so simultaneously feasible). Thus P*_1>=8T times their count. But near-square lower bounds for those rough centers are precisely where parity/semiprime obstruction reappears. This does not establish (8). Direct fan/CRT constructions at polynomial exponents below 1/2 do not prove (8) at horizon (II). The remaining task is to construct a larger supply of actual record bins through deep histories or to find a different gain witness.

## 6. Super-square-root check, and why simple extension does not close the proof

For a fixed theta in (1/2,1), take T~R^theta and Y~T/2. Every firing in a canonical prefix has p<=3T+7=o(R), so a twin source (neighbors both prime ~R) cannot fire; every completed wait is at most 2S. TOP count O(1), so total TOP wait O(N sqrt R) without any sieve or deletion. A non-twin final idle also <=2S (up to one birth/arrival tick). Low blocks as above. Under finite twins (for sufficiently large slabs with none), P(d)<=O(N sqrt R) even without a cap; with twins, add C MT twinCount for capped policies. This gives a deterministic stronger threshold at super-square-root time, but now a lower requires a record gain of order larger than sqrt R per average root, not the R^delta-scale waits already constructed. Merely extending the observation time therefore does not supply a proof. Use (8) as the primary new additive route, alongside the strict single-root witness (4).

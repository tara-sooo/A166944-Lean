## 1. Direct weighted top waits instead of whole-root deletion

Write L=log R, T~sqrt(R)/L^B with fixed B>0, N~R/6 canonical roots, synchronized windows width W=6 floor(T/6). FullCapped includes all pre-observation states. Let Y=T/L^b with any fixed b>0, rounded down, and U=floor(R^delta), fixed 0<delta<1/4. Use old reward D=8 and cost 17(p-1) for 0<p<Y, zero otherwise. The actual canonical gain and height bounds confine all prefix centers to I=[R-3T-6,2R], ages <=3T/2, labels <=3T+7. Each physical key (x,n,p) belongs to O(1) windows, since all roots visiting x lie in [x,x+3T+6].

Previous checkpoint removed all selected roots hitting a slow TOP key. Instead count the FULL wait of each completed TOP firing (arrival live, source live); the final outgoing firing may be dropped from this upper accounting since its actual cost is nonnegative. If full wait w>=U, both neighbors of source are U/2-rough by the first-event calendar bound. For fixed p>=Y, one neighbor is pr, other pr+-2; r and pr+-2 have no odd prime factor <=U/4. Independent two-form Selberg sieve from 0e9b47b gives O(R/(p L^2)) sources uniformly, using cutoff min((R/p)^(1/12),U/4)<p. For each source, the height strip and clock congruence give O(T/p) possible firing clocks. There are O(RT/(p^2 L^2)) slow keys of label p. At most C M selected roots complete each key by cap on the source just before firing. Each contributes wait <=2p. Hence
    sum_slow_TOP w <= C M RT/L^2 * sum_{Y<=p<=3T+7} 1/p
                   <= C M RT (1+log(T/Y))/L^3.                  (1)
The second inequality is elementary dyadic prime counting, uniform for log Y comparable to L. No root deletion; all pre-observation completed waits included. Fast TOP waits total <=N U(3T+6)/(Y-1), by gain. All non-TOP completed events split into at most 1+(3T+6)/(Y-1) contiguous blocks per root; use the mod2310 inequality W<=17G/8+395/12 from c0e45b0. Its finite certificate is not needed in section 2 below.

Final idle <H0=floor(T/L^4) costs at most NH0; long idle center lies in H0/2-rough set, with <=CMT ticks per site across all windows. Independent semiprime sieve from 0e9b47b gives #rough<=twinCount(I)+O_B(R log L/L^3). Thus for all M>=1, fixed b>0, old reward/cost with enlarged cutoff Y,
    P*_M/(8MNT) <= C twinCount(I)/N + O_{B,b}(log L/L^3).       (2)
Previously Y=T/(log L)^a was required for this error by whole-root trimming. The new direct sum allows arbitrarily large FIXED powers L^b in the denominator, hence a pointwise smaller cost, with the same log-power upper. It even permits Y=T exp(-L^gamma) for fixed 0<gamma<1 with error O(L^{gamma-3}+log L/L^3), provided delta is fixed below 1/4. At original T~sqrt(R) exp(-sqrt L), terminal residual L^-2.5 dominates this for gamma<=.5. This is an improved tradeoff, not a positive lower. No change to the old cost or optimizer other than the parameter Y.

## 2. Calendar-gap cost with reward from birth; no top scan and no gain potential

For a fixed even x>2, define E_x to be the doubly infinite set of all eligible firing clocks for the static center x: the progression 0 mod2p for each odd prime divisor p of x+1, and p+2 mod2p for each odd prime divisor p of x-1. At a clock f in E_x, let
    ell(x,f)=f-max{e in E_x:e<f}.
The previous calendar event is strictly before f, regardless of ties; ell is a positive integer, <=2 qmin(x), where qmin is the least odd prime divisor of either neighbor. Equivalently ell is the minimum of positive backward distances to each congruence class. If an actual stationary phase began at time a (birth or after arrival at x) and fires at f, it cannot have a static eligible clock strictly between a and f. Thus its full wait w=f-a <=ell(x,f). Equality is possible if arrival is at the previous eligible clock; the previous actual event may have been at a different center.

Use reward D=48 for EVERY tick of a selected prefix from its own birth until the same common retirement of its window (including pre-observation). This deliberately changes the objective, not the recurrence. For fixed K=floor(T/L^c), c>3 fixed, call x rough if qmin(x)>=K. Charge at outgoing tick of a firing at f:
    C_cal(x,f,p)=0                 if x rough,
                 D ell(x,f)       otherwise.
No charge on idle ticks. This is a nonnegative additive state cost (f=n+1, p=winner(s)). Define Q_cal(d)=sum_A[D prefixAge(d,A)-pathCost_Ccal(A,prefixAge)]. Cap still applies to every live tick, including pre-observation. Unlike rootNet in the checked core, reward starts at birth. Section 4 proves the exact optimizer mathematically for the new reward, rather than claiming the old Lean theorem already has this definition.

Decompose each prefix INCLUDING its last outgoing transition into completed waiting phases and its last idle tail, so k=sum w+I exactly. Completed nonrough phases have D w<=cost; completed rough phases have no charge. Every tick of a completed rough phase is at the rough source, selected alive up to and including the tick before its firing, even if arrival is dead. The final idle tail at nonrough source has I<=2qmin(x)<2K. If rough, all its ticks are charged to the same capped rough occupation. For all policies and M>=1,
    Q_cal(d)/D <= C M T |S_K| + 2 K N,                       (3)
where S_K={x in I even:qmin(x)>=K}. Each site can be visited by O(1) windows and each birth-to-retirement span <=3T/2; no assumption on number of distinct visits/phase arrivals. Use (7) of c0e45b0 with threshold K (or K/2 to absorb equality):
    |S_K| <= twinCount(I)+O_{B,c}(R log L/L^3).
Since K/T<=L^-c, (3) gives the same uniform normalized upper as (2) with Q_cal/(DMNT). There is NO reliance on mod2310, multi-form scan, or a high-prime firing count in this bridge.

An improved gap is available without any arithmetic relaxation. Fix window J and a physical phase at x ending at firing f<=retirement, and let a_min(J,x,f) be the earliest arrival at x in this phase by ANY full canonical path of that window. Define ell_act=f-a_min. Every selected completed wait in this phase is <=ell_act<=ell. Thus C_act=D ell_act at nonrough sources, zero at rough sources, gives (3) verbatim and Q_act^*>=Q_cal^*. C_act may depend on window and full reachability; once computed it is shared by all roots at that physical bin, not adaptive to a selected policy. It is not a state-only inc across all windows. Unreachable phases can have arbitrary cost since no selected root reaches them. This version removes calendar time before the first actual arrival. It can be computed by an unweighted phase reachability pass.

## 3. Hybrid cost dominates the enlarged old objective up to negligible error

For comparison to section 1, use the certified (paper/computational, not new Lean) mod2310 potential Phi with 0<=Phi<=1580 and
    D w-102(p-1)<=Phi(after)-Phi(before), D=48.
For nonrough completed events, let g=ell or ell_act, and set
    C_hyb=min(102(p-1), D g+1580);
for rough events set zero. For any actual phase start and end, D w-DeltaPhi<=both terms inside min, because w<=g and DeltaPhi>=-1580. Hence the same inequality holds for C_hyb. On consecutive nonrough completed phases the potential differences telescope. The number of separated nonrough blocks per root is at most 1+(3T+6)/(K-1), because every rough firing has p>=K. The residual overhead is at most 1580 N[1+(3T+6)/(K-1)], negligible divided by DNT for fixed c. The other contributions are exactly the rough/idle upper of (3). Thus both hybrid versions obey the same upper (2). Using the actual gap only decreases the cost further.

Choose Y=T/L^b and K=T/L^{b+4}, fixed b>0. Compare on ANY full-capped policy to 6 times the old reward-8 objective, including its pre-observation costs. Birth reward adds nonnegative reward. For p<Y the hybrid cost is <=102(p-1), the scaled old cost; for p>=Y the possible extra cost is <=2DK+1580. There are <=(3T+6)/(Y-1) such events per root. Thus
    Q_hyb(d) >= 6P_old(d) - N(2DK+1580)(3T+6)/(Y-1).
Taking maxima under the same prefix cap,
    Q_hyb,M^*/(DMNT) >= P_old,M^*/(8MNT)-O(L^-4/M).          (4)
This proves that the new hybrid route does not sacrifice an asymptotic lower at the scale log L/L^3. The arrival-only objective has a simpler upper and lower cost than the calendar-only version, but no general comparison of arrival-only to the old gain objective is asserted. The hybrid comparison uses the prior finite potential; pure calendar/actual-gap upper does not. All objectives are different from the old GoodFieldEstimate.

## 4. Exact minimum-debt and phase-release optimizer for cap one

For each window let r(n) be a common nondecreasing integer reward clock. Birth reward uses r(n)=D n (possibly negative absolute values); old observation reward uses r(n)=D max(0,n-obsStart). A root earns r(n+1)-r(n) each tick while alive; cost c(x,n) shared by all at state (x,n) in the window, nonnegative. Full paths end at common retirement e. Net along prefix is sum reward minus cost, starting 0; reflected debt d=runningPeak-net is nonnegative. For a live state the outgoing increment a=r(n+1)-r(n)-c, and
    profit=max(0,a-d),  d'=max(0,d-a).                       (5)
These generic identities were already checked for rootNet. Here the arbitrary common reward-clock extension is a paper proof, not a new kernel theorem.

At cap1, keep one minimum-debt representative at each occupied physical state, admitting newborns with d=0. At the next clock, transport each kept representative by actual tick; choose min debt among arrivals and newborns. Induction proves the kept representative has minimum debt among ALL full-path ancestors of that state: each predecessor minimum is present, and the update in (5) is monotone. No resurrection, since only representatives that were live one tick earlier or newborns are admitted. All full paths have common retirement per window; no unequal expiry issue. Every kept tick contributes the maximum full-path record profit at the bin. Conversely any capped prefix policy has net <=sum of its record profits <=sum of full-path per-bin maxima. Greedy prefix collection gives equality after truncating each selected root at its own peak, preserving cap and collecting exactly its summed record increments. This proves finite optimum = sum of maxima for cap1 under birth reward or old reward, window-dependent shared costs included. It is not necessary to track multiplicities or identities to compute the VALUE. For M>=1 the same pointwise upper remains; this section proves exact cap1, not a new cap-M theorem for arbitrary reward.

Now consider a stationary physical phase ending at next calendar firing f (or at retirement). For each arrival i at a_i with debt d_i, set lambda_i=r(a_i)+d_i. Store ONE number
    lambda=min_i lambda_i.
During idle ticks the minimum reflected debt at state n is max(0,min_{a_i<=n}lambda_i-r(n)). For record profit one may take min over ALL arrivals in the phase: a future arrival has lambda_i>=r(a_i)>=r(n+1) and cannot give positive earlier profit. Thus idle record mass at tick n equals
    [r(n+1)-max(r(n),lambda)]_+.
If last idle arrival clock t is f-1 for a firing phase or e for a terminal idle phase, its total idle record profit is exactly [r(t)-lambda]_+. The first possible occupied clock has reward <=lambda, hence no omitted lower-end term. For firing at f, including f=e, add
    [r(f)-r(f-1)-c-max(0,lambda-r(f-1))]_+.
If f<e, the outgoing minimum-debt representative arrives at the lower center with release
    lambda_out = max(r(f), r(f-1)+c, lambda+c).              (6)
Derive by adding r(f) to the debt update in (5). At a newborn arrival use lambda_birth=r(birthClock); at a merger simply take the minimum of releases. Equations (5)-(6) handle partial-unit profits exactly. A phase can begin empty and is identified by its next actual static firing clock, not merely by center. At retirement include the last outgoing firing charge if present. Process all firings at f before inserting their arrivals or births at f; an arrival at a site which also fires at f belongs to the NEW phase. This preserves exact physical tick semantics.

Hence cap1 value is determined by a scalar min/max-plus recursion on the acyclic reached phase graph. The unweighted reachability pass gives ell_act; the weighted pass needs only the minimum release per phase. No enumeration of root paths at each tick, no second moment. Formula (6) is also an explicit max over accumulated future costs along any individual phase path: iterating max(lambda+c, barrier) yields max(initial release+all costs, each barrier+subsequent costs). Taking min over valid incoming paths gives the same release. This is a new concrete local analytic target: a long phase with lambda sufficiently before r(t) contributes positive profit, and incoming branches compete by min, not by sum of multiplicities. It still requires an asymptotic lower on the population of such reached phases, not just independent congruence-compatible spatial paths.

## 5. An actual growing-scale lower for the birth-credit objective, and its shortfall

Reuse the dimension-two lower fundamental lemma already applied in 34aea0c and 67d4716, at a sufficiently small FIXED delta>0. Let H=floor(R^delta), much smaller than T. On canonical roots A=6j in [R,2R] (j in interval of length ~R/6), sieve 6j+-1 by primes >=5 up to 2H+7. For squarefree d on those primes, there are exactly 2^omega(d) bad classes, remainder <=2^omega(d). At level sqrt(R), sum remainder O(sqrt(R) log R); choose delta so s=log(level)/log(cutoff) is sufficiently large for positive lower fundamental lemma factor. The product of local densities is comparable to 1/(log R)^2 by Mertens. Therefore >=c_delta R/L^2 canonical roots have both neighbors free of odd factors <=2H+7. Checked height-local bound p<=2H+7 makes each such root idle through age H (or direct clock pins). Retire exactly at age H, before any cost; kill all other roots at birth. Distinct selected centers never move, so simultaneous full cap1 holds even before observation. This yields for all four birth-credit objectives, at the ACTUAL enlarged horizon T~sqrt R/L^B,
    Q_1^* >= c_delta D R^{1+delta}/L^2,
    Q_1^*/(DNT) >= c'_delta R^{delta-1/2} L^{B-2}.          (7)
This is an unconditional asymptotic lower at the long-horizon optimization problem (short selected lives), not just a finite sample. It is exponentially smaller in L than the required log L/L^3. The argument cannot set delta near 1/2: the lower sieve fails there, and the earlier non-twin semiprime upper is the same obstruction. No claim of a meaningful breakthrough of the target lower. A potential next step is to exploit (6) to propagate releases from these many polynomial-length initial phases into later phases, without requiring all roots to stay alive or all histories to have cheap total gain. The missing inequality would have to amplify this lower to near-square-root occupied record mass; neither (6) nor (7) alone supplies such an amplification.

## 1. Frozen ensemble and orientation

Keep dyadic X, B=ceil(sqrt(7X)), u=log B, ell=1+log u, N=Theta(X/u), T=floor(X/u^.25), H=T+B, original gain-stopped completed MIN prime-root paths. Each original tag has total gain <H, each completed event has gain g=p-1 and wait 1<=w<=2r(C)<=2p<=3g. Under finitely many twins, eventually W_all>=N(T-6)/2. All old actual and pair masks through 5653cda remain frozen. The old source grids P_j have common averaging denominator m, P_max<=B^.25, a=u^.25, c_a=3(1+2/a), original phase wheel L0 largest even primorial<=ell. Parameter tau>=1/log ell, tau->0, default tau=1/log ell. Define Y=B^.1, L_h=ceil(B exp(1/tau)). High focal waits retained by d7cc573 have selected label >=Y and w>=tau g, and their good right-witness source crossings use the high pursuit. The high source witness may be small nonself (q<=P/a, focal label<=P/a) or large self (q>=aP, source is focal event). High focal sector refers to these original assigned waits, NOT the label of the eventual defect. The latter can be small. Low focal sector is unchanged.

At an original source crossing (C,q) of n_k strict boundary atoms K in [C,J), J=C+q-1, each focal tag i has original assigned high clock W_i^h at the source cell, distributed uniformly across n_k atoms. Each actual stable differing-word partner tag in the original phase class n has energy W_i^h/(c_a n_k n) per ordered pair per atom. The sum over partners is <=W_i^h/(c_a n_k), even if the old rule selects the partner as the actual nonperiodic destination. The sum over all atoms of this source crossing is <=W_i^h/c_a. Do not count the original whole wait for each crossed boundary. The original assigned good high focal event waits belong to one right source cell per grid, with no reuse across crossings.

For every exact residual high forward charge, record in addition to the old chosen nonperiodic event its actual FOCAL member's event f at the common first-defect prehorizon E. Its selected label is r_f (use p_f in formulas below, to distinguish from least eligible factor r(E)). If the old chosen side is partner, these events may have different labels. This is an exact refinement/reindexing of old remaining nonnegative charges; preserve the old chosen destination, predecessor-pair keys, sector, all old payments and ordering. No change to the previous normalization. In particular the new bound is not a direct improvement by tau² for arbitrary charge at the OLD chosen nonperiodic key receiving many partner-role contributions.

## 2. A deterministic one-role capacity in clock coordinates

Fix an original tag and a focal actual first-defect event f at E. On this tag scan its own completed events before f backwards, starting with radical L0. Stop BEFORE the first event whose inclusion makes the lcm of periods >4H or whose posthorizon J satisfies E-J>=L_h. This suffix is contiguous, since radical and backwards distance are monotone. No excluded selected label is needed for the focal event; it may itself be periodic under the original lag. Let C_* be the earliest accepted prehorizon, or E if empty. Each accepted event has gain <=B, hence E-C_*<L_h+B. For every actual source crossing of this tag sending high charge to f, the entire selected label list from its source inclusive to f exclusive is a translated prefix with periods dividing its own pair's nonzero lag |Delta|<=4H. Thus its source event lies in the accepted suffix. Although different charges can have DIFFERENT lags, this one maximal suffix is well defined and its total radical <=4H. It is not necessary that there be one lag shared by all contributing pairs. The statement holds also if the focal event is not chosen by the nonperiodic-side rule.

Define A_h(P,f) as the sum of the original high assigned cell clocks W_i^h over all original crossings in this suffix on the tag, using zero when the original source predicates fail. This is computed from individual tag data, not pair comparisons. Then
    D_h,focal(P,f) <= A_h(P,f)/c_a.                    (1)
Each contributing source row has total weight <= its actual assigned clock/c_a, regardless of the number of partners and of multiple atoms at a crossing. This is ONE role; unlike the old destination cap, no extra factor 2. In particular A_h/c_a<=L_h+B+P by counting strict atoms with old per-tag cap c_a P. The new cap can also be intersected with more restrictive original target or heavy-prefix predicates, without changing its validity.

Except for the possible initial cell fringe before C_*, every assigned high focal wait summed in A_h is an actual event of the accepted suffix: right cells end at their designated source crossing. If a cell straddles C_* its preceding noncrossing events have total gain <=P, hence total actual wait <=3P. There is at most ONE such initial fringe; an earlier crossing before C_* cannot also be the right witness of a focal wait in a later cell. Large self source has no assigned nonself focal waits. Therefore
    A_h(P,f) <= sum_(actual events e with C_*<=C_e<E,
                        p_e>=Y, w_e>=tau(p_e-1)) w_e +3P.    (2)
The earliest accepted event need not cross a grid boundary. If it does not, the same bound follows by taking the first actual accepted crossing's cell; only its portion before C_* lies outside the suffix. If there are no crossings then A_h=0. This is a deterministic inequality for the frozen assigned waits.

## 3. Apply the previous local sieve to the entire maximal suffix

At most log(4H)/log Y=20+o(1) distinct labels >=Y divide the radical of that suffix. For each such label p, selected at prehorizon C, an expensive high wait requires r(C)>=tau(p-1)/2. On ANY interval of prehorizons of length at most L_h+B+O(1), the old two-form upper sieve of d7cc573 gives
    #eligible C with fixed p and r(C)>=tau(p-1)/2
          << tau² L_h/p.                             (3)
For clarity, use C=p j-sigma, sigma=+/-1; j and p j-2sigma avoid small odd primes, excluding 2 and p, with cutoff z=floor(min(p^(1/40),(L_h/p)^(1/40))). For p>=B^.1 and p<=B, z<tau(p-1)/2 eventually, log(2+z)>>1/tau since L_h/p>=exp(1/tau), 1/tau<=log ell. The uniform old two-form estimate O((L_h/p)/log²(2+z)+z^8) is O(tau² L_h/p): the error z^8<=(L_h/p)^.2 is absorbed since exp(.8/tau) tau²->infinity. Constants absolute and uniform in the interval and p and allowed tau. We rely on the previously established upper sieve, not an empirical density or a lower prime-pair estimate. The path's prehorizons strictly increase, so every eligible C contributes at most one event for that fixed label on the tag. Since w<=2p, its total high wait is O(tau² L_h). The sum over <=20+o(1) labels retains this order. Also P_max=o(tau² L_h), since tau² exp(1/tau)->infinity and P_max<<B. It follows that for every grid and original tag/event
    D_h,focal(P,f) <= A_h(P,f)/c_a << tau² L_h.          (4)
One can bound by the minimum of the actual A_h/c_a, the local sieve envelope and the earlier raw one-role cap; the analytic gain is O(tau²) relative to O(L_h), uniform even if the backward gain interval is long. Equivalently all sources sending high focal charge to a given f occupy only O(tau² L_h) high-wait clock on that original history. This is a distance/clock bound, not a reduction in the actual pursuit length for all trajectories. It does not require the destination label to be high. It also holds if some source cohorts have small-prefix mass and are already paid; all old masks only remove charge.

A related source-side interpretation: a translated prefix prior to first defect during a high pursuit accumulates at most O(tau² L_h) expensive-high waits on either member (same labels/waits). The prior high-richness lemma supplies >=tau L_h/2 expensive-high waits to the whole focal pursuit, so the first defect occurs before all but an O(tau) fraction of that high-wait mass has been accumulated, up to the single defect edge and subsequent suffix. More precisely the PRE-defect mass / total pursuit high mass is O(tau); the defect event itself is not included in the pre-defect bound. This is not a statement about the fraction of geometric gain or about independent defects on one tag.

## 4. Exact focal projected reindexing and stronger threshold

Let F_h(E,p_f) be the grid-averaged exact remaining high energy grouped by the focal actual first-defect event's projected (E,p_f), irrespective of which side the old rule chose. Use the identical average denominator m. Then
    sum_(E,p_f) F_h(E,p_f) = D_h,old,rem,
    F_h(E,p_f) <= C tau² L_h n(E,p_f)                 (5)
for an absolute constant C and original full projected tag population n(E,p_f). The first equality holds because this is a partition, not an added transport. Pointwise F_h need not be <= the OLD energy at that key; old chosen-side payments are applied first and frozen. One may retain both keys to combine capacities.

The earlier graded support bound sum_(E eligible at fixed p) h(r(E))<<X/p with h(r)=log²(2+r)/(1+log log(2+r))² applies also to the focal selected label, including p<=y0. Pay exact F_h(E,p) if
    F_h(E,p) <= H tau h(r(E))/(u ell).                 (6)
As sum_(p<=B) 1/p=O(ell), total payment O(XH tau/u)=O(tau NT). This is energy in the full constant-coefficient route; no diverging multiplier. The same old support refinements apply on p>=sqrt(P_min), where reciprocal sum O(1) permits removing ell, and on rough r using the previously proved rough support; not needed here. By (5) every focal key with
    n(E,p) <= (1/C) H h(r(E))/(tau L_h u ell)          (7)
is paid, where C is any fixed sufficiently large constant satisfying (5). Relative to applying (6) with the old O(L_h) population capacity, this increases the order of the allowable population cutoff by tau^(-2). In particular for tau=1/log ell and L_h~B ell, (7) is of order B (log ell) h(r)/(u^(5/4) ell²), up to fixed constants and H=Theta(B²/u^.25). The older same absolute threshold and raw cap gave order B h(r)/(u^(5/4) ell² log ell). This is a sufficient all-mass cutoff, not a necessary lower bound on all large projected groups.

## 5. Combine with two-label support without changing old normalization

Keep original high-sector refined chosen-side pair keys:
* distinct labels p,q at common E, as in a3f065b;
* same label p at E, immediately preceding common q!=p, as in e5d1157.

For distinct labels split each exact remaining pair-key charge into the two possible focal orientations, F^(p) and F^(q); the superscript is the focal selected label, not the chosen side. Same-label has only one focal label. Each orientation-specific charge obeys the old bilateral or minority capacity, and in addition the uniform (5) on the corresponding focal population. Thus for a fixed (E,p,q) in the distinct-label case,
    D_h(E;p,q) <= min(old bilateral cap,
                                C tau² L_h [n(E,p)+n(E,q)]),
with stronger orientation-specific min before adding. For same label the analogous bound is min(old minority/reverse cut cap, C tau² L_h n(E,p)); one may restrict n to the actual preceding-q event subset. If phase refinement is used, sum actual phase populations once. Do not sum the relaxed right sides over all pair keys and infer (5) again; (5) bounds the exact focal aggregate by one source-row capacity, and individual relaxed bounds may overlap. The old thin cutoffs apply to exact original keys; additionally pay each orientation-specific exact residual separately at the SAME pair cutoff H u^nu/(u ell³), where nu is number of the two labels >=B/u. The number of orientations is at most two, and the already proved O(X/(pq)) spatial support means total extra payment O(NT/ell). In the two-high band the high-pursuit focal population cutoff is consequently
    n(E,p_f) <= H u/(C tau² L_h ell³)
              = Theta(B u^.75 (log ell)^2/ell^4)
for default tau and fixed sufficiently large C. This improves the earlier high-pursuit O(B u^.75/ell^4) threshold on the FOCAL side. The old bilateral cutoff still applies on a small opposite group even if the focal group is very large. No tau² improvement on the partner-side capacity claimed.

## 6. Updated sufficient condition and remaining gap

Let D_h,new be the exact residual high energy after new focal-key and orientation-specific payments (apply masks sequentially, no double payment). Then D_h,new<=D_h,old,rem, and their difference O(tau NT+NT/ell)=o(NT). Low forward energy D_l,rem and backward B_rem unchanged. Under finitely many twins, the existing certificate gives
    NT/2-o(NT) <= B_rem+(3+o(1))(D_l,rem+D_h,new).
Therefore liminf [B_rem+3(D_l,rem+D_h,new)]/(NT)<1/2 is sufficient. The new source-clock envelopes also allow sharper target supersets (using both original and focal labels where needed), to use in the residue-exact tree estimates. The new heavy-common-prefix mask of 5653cda still holds simultaneously, and can be included in A_h by restricting source crossings whose allowed first-separation group has mass >eta n. No bound established on the total mass of those heavy groups or the summed high focal capacity tight enough to prove the global constant margin. The extra payments themselves are little-o. The progress is a local uniform vanishing-factor capacity improvement with an exact global reindexing and larger all-mass thresholds, not a proof that high forward energy is o(NT). Main and independent branches unchanged. No Lean compilation.

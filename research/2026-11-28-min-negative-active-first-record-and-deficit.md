## 1. First-record deficits: an exact obstruction for every canonical history

Let H>=21 integer and consider a canonical orbit from A divisible6, h0=3, until its FIRST hit of H. Assume the path remains in the positive even spatial slab and all neighbor factors/calendars are defined. Let k be the number of completed moving events before that hit. For j=1,...,k, denote winner p_j, full positive wait w_j, postheight h_j and deficit d_j=H-h_j>=1. Put d0=H-3. All pre-tick heights before the first-H hit are <=H-1. Because the height identity is h_j=h_(j-1)+w_j-(p_j-1)/2, the pre-tick height is h_j+(p_j-3)/2. Hence
    p_j <= 2d_j+1.
Also w_j<=2p_j from the static eligible calendar at the source (it suffices that p_j itself has period2p_j). Thus
    d_(j-1) = d_j+w_j-(p_j-1)/2
             <= d_j+(3p_j+1)/2 <=4d_j+2.
Equivalently d_j+2/3 >=(d_(j-1)+2/3)/4. This requires no label lower bound, number-of-events restriction or random model. It also applies to a fixed prime-cutoff graph along any canonical path that has a next selected winner.

A sharper first-event estimate: for p1>=11 the first postheight has pin m p1+b, b=0 or3, m>=0. Since h1<=3+(3p1+1)/2<2p1, m<=1, so h1<=p1+3. Combining with p1<=2(H-h1)+1 gives 3h1<=2H+4. For p1<=7 the simple bound h1<=14 is already sufficient for H>=21. Therefore d1+2/3 >=(H-2)/3. The final idle interval has I=d_k, since first H is reached by an idle tick (all moving ticks have nonpositive height change), and I<2qmin(x) at its destination x, by the full next-wait calendar. Integrality gives I<=2qmin-1. Combining:

In particular the last moving label p_k<=2I+1<=4qmin(x)-1. If k=0, I=H-3 implies qmin>=(H-2)/2. For k=1, qmin>=(H-1)/6; k=2, qmin>=(H-2)/24+1/6. For H polynomial and qmin<H/L^6 (L=log R), (1) forces k>=6 log L/log 4-O(1). Thus a deep active-record witness cannot be obtained by merely raising the height of a fixed-length event prefix. This is separate from the previous W/G lower bound on total displacement. Equation (1) bounds actual event count even for a low-gain ancestor not chosen by min-plus.

## 3. Consequences for the intended .4 transport and audit

The previous active-sink diagnostic at H=.4 was qmin<H/16; the construction now shows such sinks exist with polynomial height and positive log-power density for every sufficiently large R, not just finite proxies. It does not overcome the known 2/7 single-source exponent barrier or prove .4. Thus this is a validation of the active target and a rigorous obstruction to simply extending fixed-length CRT schedules, not a new plan to spend most effort on them. The remaining main problem is cumulative transport losses/weighted residual at growing height and density R/L². No change to user's accepted .4 operational milestone.

## 2. Label-sensitive local sieve

For a tagged focal or partner actual first-defect event f at prehorizon E, form the maximal backward accepted suffix of completed events before f as in 2fdd238: include an earlier event with posthorizon J only if E-J<L_h and the lcm of all accepted periods and L0 stays <=4H. Its total length is <L_h+B. Any source crossing of an actual routed pair, on either member, is in this suffix because the translated prefix INCLUDING the source label has every period dividing the original nonzero lag of magnitude <=4H. Different pairs can have different lags; the maximal suffix still has its own bounded radical. At most log(4H)/log Y=20+o(1) distinct high primes divide it.

For any label p>=Y and fixed sign at an eligible C, r(C)>=tau(p-1)/2 is necessary for an expensive selected wait. The old dimension-two upper sieve on the forms j and p j-2sigma at C=p j-sigma gives, uniformly in an interval of length <=L_h+B+O(1),
    #C << (L_h/p)/log²(2+z)+z^8,
    z=floor(min(p^(1/40),(L_h/p)^(1/40))).
Here z<tau(p-1)/2 for large X, and p is excluded from the small-prime sieve. More explicitly, putting
    d(p)=min(log p, log(L_h/p)),
one has log(2+z)>>d(p) and the error is absorbed in (L_h/p)/d(p)^2. Indeed z^8<=(L_h/p)^.2, d(p)<=log(L_h/p), and L_h/p>=exp(1/tau)->infinity uniformly. Thus eligible C count is O(L_h/[p d(p)^2]). A single original tag visits each C at most once. Multiplying by 2p bounds the weighted sum of these candidate source events by O(L_h/d(p)^2). This is an upper bound also if the actual wait of this particular tag is NOT expensive, as long as r(C) satisfies the displayed necessary condition.

For Y<=p<=B^.75, d(p)>=u/10, since L_h>=B. Consequently the bound is O(L_h/u²) per label. For B^.75<p<=B, d(p)>>1/tau and the old O(tau² L_h) bound holds (there are at most two such labels in the bounded radical eventually). More generally the fixed exponent .75 may be replaced by any fixed beta in (.25,1), with constants depending on beta. We use .75 to fix the partition. These estimates use the earlier proved local sieve, not an assertion of uniform distribution of actual trajectory states.

## 3. Focal capacities by source band

For one fixed original tag/focal first-defect event f, the sum of all routed contributions from a source crossing, over partners and atoms, is <=its original assigned band clock/c_a. This is the one-role row budget. All such crossings belong to its maximal backward suffix. Nonself focal waits are contained in that suffix except for at most one initial right-cell fringe, whose total wait is <=3P by the old strict-boundary cell geometry; all nonself high labels are <=P_max<=B^.25. Thus, with the bounded number of distinct high labels in the suffix,
    F_nonself(P,f) << L_h/u² + P_max << L_h/u².
The fringe can contain labels outside the suffix's radical; it is bounded separately, and P_max=o(L_h/u²). There is no need to assume the nonself source crossing itself has a high label. For medium-self and top-self the assigned original wait is the source crossing's own wait, so there is NO fringe and the source label is in the suffix's radical. By section 2,
    F_medium(P,f) << L_h/u²,
    F_top(P,f) << tau² L_h.                             (1)
All these are in the OLD energy units, uniform over original tagged events and grids; no chosen-side restriction on f's own label. This proves a stronger focal result than 2fdd238 for the first two bands.

## 4. Partner-role capacities for self sources

Fix instead an original partner tag and its actual first-defect event f. For each possible source crossing (C,q) on this tag, every focal tag sending a self-band charge at this source has the SAME projected selected source label q, and expensive high wait w_i>=tau(q-1). Therefore the common spatial r(C) satisfies r(C)>=tau(q-1)/2, even if the partner's own wait is short. The original per-atom column sum over focal tags is at most 2q/(c_a n_k), since each contributing row has clock <=2q/n_k and there are at most n rows in the original phase. Sum over n_k atoms at most 2q/c_a. This is the entire incoming self-band energy of this one partner at this source, not a count of individual partners. Its actual source crossing must be in the partner's maximal accepted suffix as above. Apply section 2 to the possible spatial source positions C on this partner history, with their actual labels q; no requirement that the partner itself have an expensive high wait. Thus
    P_medium(P,f) << L_h/u²,
    P_top(P,f) << tau² L_h.                             (2)
These bounds apply to each role separately, and both roles together with the sum of the constants. They also hold if some row or column charge was already paid. There is no proof of (2) for the nonself band: the original high focal wait can lie before the shared source crossing and need not occur on the partner history. For that band retain the previous raw partner cap and the stronger focal cap (1). This distinction is necessary even with full stability, which preserves cohorts within each physical trajectory but does not assert identical histories before different source slots.

## 5. Stronger support masks and minority capacities

For each band let K_nonself=K_medium=L_h/u², K_top=tau² L_h, with fixed sufficiently large common constant C. Averaging (1) over the same m grids, the projected FOCAL key charge at (E,p) is <=C K_band n(E,p), where n(E,p) is the full original projected population. For self bands the partner projected charge has the same bound by (2). The exact band key reindex sums to the old residual band energy, not a duplicate transport. At fixed p use the earlier graded support sum_E h(r(E))<<X/p, h(r)=log²(2+r)/(1+loglog(2+r))², and pay a focal-key band charge <= H tau h(r(E))/(u ell). Across three bands total O(tau NT), since sum_p 1/p=O(ell). Apply after earlier masks, never reintroduce their paid fractions. For the first two bands every focal key with n(E,p)<=H tau u h(r(E))/(C L_h ell) is payable, an order tau² u² times larger threshold than using 2fdd238's tau² L_h cap at the identical absolute cutoff. One may additionally apply partner-key payments for self bands at the same total order.

For the existing distinct-label pair keys (E,p,q) keep the exact chosen label and the focal orientation, now also recording the band. For medium-self and top-self, the two actual label groups each have one role, so the exact orientation-specific energy is <= C K_band min(n(E,p),n(E,q)); use the minimum with earlier bilateral capacities. Nonself instead has the stronger bound C K_nonself n(E,p_focal) and the old opposite-group cap. For same-label p=p with preceding q!=p, both role capacities of each actual tagged event in the refined group are <=C K_band (adjust C for their sum) in self bands. The noncollision and distinct-wait restrictions of e5d1157 remain. Choosing a maximum wait or exit class as an independent set in each destination phase gives
    D_same,band(E;p,q) <= C K_band mu(E;p,q)
for self bands, where mu is the old full-population minority count summed over phases. This uses the same vertex-cover proof with the new combined row/column per-tag capacities. For nonself, one may use the minimum of earlier minority/reverse cut bounds and the new one-role focal-population bound; the improved minority coefficient is not proved for that band.

Fixed distinct pair labels p,q have O(X/(pq)) spatial support for both the distinct-label and same-label-with-predecessor keys (earlier CRT results). Put Q=B/u and nu=#labels>=Q. Pay each exact remaining band/orientation pair charge <=H u^nu/(u ell³), the earlier pair threshold. Three bands and at most two orientations change only a fixed factor; sum over reciprocal primes using sum_high 1/p=O(ell/u) gives total O(NT/ell). In the two-high pair band the corresponding population threshold for nonself focal, medium-self either side, or medium-self same-label minority is
    H u³/(C L_h ell³).
For default tau=1/log ell and L_h~B ell, its order is B u^(11/4)/ell^4. The top-self bilateral or minority cutoff is of order B u^.75 (log ell)^2/ell^4, and the previous focal-only result had that latter order. These are sufficient all-mass deletions with a sufficiently small absolute prefactor, not upper bounds on all actual group sizes. The medium-source classification concerns the original focal label, not the two labels at the destination; a two-high destination pair can arise from any of the three bands. No assumption that these labels equal the source label.

## 6. Remaining goal

All new extra payments on exact surviving high charges sum to O(tau NT+NT/ell)=o(NT). Low forward and backward birth remain unchanged. With D_h,new the exact new residual, the previous conditional lower bound NT/2-o(NT)<=B_rem+(3+o(1))(D_l,rem+D_h,new) holds under finitely many twins. This yields the same sufficient threshold liminf [B_rem+3(D_l,rem+D_h,new)]/(NT)<1/2 with a no-larger exact objective. The new uniform coefficient L_h/u² is a genuine stronger local capacity for medium and nonself focal sources, including both roles of medium self and minority covers; it does not imply that their summed charges are o(NT). No summation over arbitrarily overlapping relaxed pair capacities is justified. The required constant-margin global contraction remains open. Main and independent source branches were not modified. No Lean compilation.

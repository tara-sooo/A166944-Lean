## 2. Variable-mass reverse Kraft lemma

At a valid even posthorizon J define candidate nonLOW odd primes
    P_y(J)={p:y<p<=B, p divides J or J+2}.
For a nonempty candidate set put d_J=|P_y(J)|, L_J=sum_(q in P_y(J)) log q, and
    pi_J(p)=1/(2d_J)+log p/(2L_J).
These are enumeration weights, not probabilities of actual MIN selection. They sum to 1. Actual incoming nonLOW jump p lies in this set. Since d_J=O(u/ell) and L_J<=log(J(J+2))=O(u), uniformly
    f_J(p):=L0/pi_J(p) <= min(2 L0 d_J, C L0 u/log p),
    f_J(p)>=L0.
Choose an absolute C sufficiently large. LOW-only contiguous gain is <L0 by the old even-multiple barrier, including partial initial/final blocks.

For a tagged recorded suffix from ancestor horizon D to current C (D<=C), define
    kappa(D,C)=L0 product_(nonLOW actual events from D up to C) f_J(p).
A finite relaxed reverse tree from fixed C encodes each such suffix by LOW offsets and nonLOW labels. At each node choose even offset 0<=e<L0 with weight 2/L0, then either stop with probability1/2 at candidate ancestor D, or continue with probability1/2 choosing p from P_y(D) with weight pi_D(p) and reversing to D-p+1. Discard invalid branches. Strictly decreasing horizons on continuation guarantee total terminal weight <=1 (or finite truncation and monotone limit). Each compressed reverse word w terminating at D has weight 1/kappa_w. In particular the sum of 1/kappa_w over distinct words is <=1. A word determines its ancestor D and all chosen labels and intermediate posthorizons; actual histories with that same word have the same kappa. No true-time information is required.

For every tag i at its current C, define its score Z_i(C) as the minimum of:
  * kappa(C0(i),C), the suffix from its own root with mass cost 1;
  * A(D) kappa(D,C) over ALL its recorded ancestor horizons D, including C.
All counts are full original counts. Choose epsilon=ell^(-1/2), Theta=B^(1-epsilon). Call the tag WEIGHTED MOVING if Z_i(C)<=Theta. At fixed C choose one witnessing suffix for every such tag. Group by the actual compressed reverse word (do not split the same word into separate root and population words). If any witness of a given word is a population witness, A(D) kappa_w<=Theta and ALL tags in this group visited D, including any root witnesses, so the total group size <=A(D)<=Theta/kappa_w. If all witnesses are roots there is at most one original prime root at D, and kappa_w<=Theta, again group size <=Theta/kappa_w. Sum over reverse words:
    A_wmov(C) <= Theta sum_w 1/kappa_w <= Theta.         (1)
Thus the simpler preliminary 2Theta bound can be tightened to Theta by pooling root/population witnesses. At physical ticks the same bound applies. This is valid for arbitrary depth and for different actual ancestor populations; no sum over possible starting mass scales. It also proves a root-only bound independently of the moving-start version.

The exact one-tag recurrence uses original data:
    Z_i(C0)=L0,
    Z_i(J)=min(L0 A(J), f_J(p) Z_i(C)),
where f_J(p)=1 on LOW jumps. The root term is included in the running previous score. This recurrence follows by multiplying every previous ancestor's suffix factor by the new jump factor, and adding the new current ancestor. Between recorded jumps Z is constant. A transition from nonmoving to weighted moving can occur only at a projected light horizon A(J)<=Theta/L0. Conversely every such horizon is weighted moving. If no new light horizon is visited, weighted moving can cease but cannot restart. An expiry (weighted moving to nonmoving) necessarily occurs across a nonLOW jump, since LOW factor1 cannot increase the score.

The previous moving predicate of 595b5b0 (m0=B^.75, k=floor(u/(8ell)), distance <=k+1 nonLOW jumps from an eligible start) implies this one eventually: its witness has mass <=m0+1 and kappa<=L0(O(u))^(k+1), product B^(7/8+o(1))<Theta. We nevertheless retain all old masks and counts. The stronger threshold Theta has only a subpolynomial relative gap from B, but B^(-epsilon) beats every fixed inverse power of u, so the existing logarithmic losses remain absorbed.

## 3. Whole-clock payments and longer ancestry requirement

No recomputation of source masses or weights. Pay all these exact surviving fractions; overlaps only paid once. Now both actual source roles and original focal waits of all surviving charges satisfy Z>Theta.

In particular their current A(C)>Theta/L0. At EVERY recorded ancestor D they satisfy
    log A(D)+log L0+sum_(nonLOW suffix) log f_J(p)
                       > (1-epsilon)u,                 (2)
and at own root the same lower bound holds with log A replaced by zero. This differs from the former fixed-depth requirement. For example if A(D)<=B^.75 and all nonLOW suffix labels >=B^alpha with a fixed alpha>0, then f_J(p)<=C L0/alpha, hence the number k_D of these jumps must satisfy
    k_D > [(.25-epsilon)u-log L0]/log(C L0/alpha).
For a root start replace .25 by 1. This is order at least u/log(2ell) eventually (L0<=ell), substantially larger than the former u/(8ell) count in such high-label suffixes. ## 4. Weighted final runs within each original source cohort

Fix a positive original backward source slot G of full b members and common predeparture S=(v-1,C), and one original strict grid atom. Its induced true merge tree contains all histories through S. The preceding payments allow retaining only pairs with both participants weighted-nonmoving at S, but use full original G in counts/normalization. Define new light threshold A(D)<=Theta/L0. Pay pairs with any common projected light horizon at or after first meeting U and no later than S: for a fixed member such partners all visited its own last light horizon, so at most Theta/L0 of them; total O(NT Theta/(L0 b_min))=o(NT). Root injection cannot occur again for a tag already present. This includes earlier light masks.

Each member has a latest eligible start (own root or such a light horizon) through S. Its final weighted-moving run is the contiguous interval from that start through its last weighted-moving true state, clipped to S. This is contiguous by the recurrence and absence of later light starts. At a pair's first meeting with no later light, if a member is weighted-moving it is in this final run; otherwise it stays weighted-nonmoving through S. Apply the source-cohort final-expiry identity of 595b5b0 with bound (1) in place of M_*: the number of unordered pairs with intersecting final runs is <=b(Theta-1) (or <=b Theta without rounding). Thus every both-moving first meeting with no later light can be paid at O(NT Theta/b_min)=o(NT), independently of absolute depth or earlier reset count. Both-nonmoving first meetings remain. For mixed first meetings define h_G(V) as the number of members of G present and weighted-nonmoving at V. The earlier mixed small-expiry payment transfers verbatim: at the moving member i's final expiry E_i its already nonmoving partner is present and nonmoving, so if h_G(E_i)<=eta b, eta=ell^(-1/4), its total number of such partners is <=eta b. Sum over i and apply the exact 2 c_a P/b unordered cap; total O(eta NT). All old depth-based payments stay first, these are extra exact payments under the new predicate. This proof does not need a global count of arbitrary moving runs.

## 5. Reindex mixed residual at the actual expiry jump

For a surviving mixed pair (one weighted-moving at first meeting U, other nonmoving, neither moving at source, no later light), the moving member expires at a unique completed nonLOW jump before or at arrival to the original source prehorizon. Call this actual event f=(C_f,p,v,J_f=C_f+p-1). Its predecessor true state (v-1,C_f) is still weighted-moving for that tag and its poststate is weighted-nonmoving. Both pair members execute the SAME physical jump f, since they met earlier and share the actual suffix. The partner was nonmoving at the predecessor and remains nonmoving at the poststate. This is an effective nonmoving-cohort injection, not a new physical first meeting; preserve the exact old birth and all its earlier masks, adding the expiry key as a partition of mixed surviving fractions. For a fixed unordered pair and source the moving member is unique. No pair weight added or moved between sources.

For a full original physical exit slot f, define disjoint sets E_f of original tags executing f that are weighted-moving immediately before and nonmoving immediately after, and O_f of original tags executing f that are nonmoving on both sides. Every mixed pair reindexed to f belongs to E_f x O_f. E_f has size <=Theta by (1). For x>=J_f set
    L_E(x)=# {i in E_f: C0(i)+H>x},
    L_O(x)=# {i in O_f: C0(i)+H>x}.
Actual terminal horizons can instead be used, which is tighter. Both vanish for x>=J_f+H. At a strict source boundary K>=J_f, the actual common continuation from f crosses K at most once. The two subset populations of its original source slot satisfy x_E<=L_E(K), x_O<=L_O(K), x_E+x_O<=b and b>=b_min. Thus the exact original mixed clock at this boundary/key <=2 c_a P x_E x_O/b <=2 c_a P L_E L_O/max(b_min,L_E+L_O). The latter function is monotone in both arguments. Average over the original grids to get the explicit lifetime upper envelope
    B_mixed(f) <= 2 c_a mean_P P sum_(J_f<=K<J_f+H)
                              L_E(K)L_O(K)/max(b_min,L_E(K)+L_O(K)).   (3)
The sum is over grid multiples. In particular <=2 c_a (H+P_max)|E_f||O_f|/max(b_min,|E_f|+|O_f|). This is stronger than a separate linear capacity on the newly expiring tags. It aggregates all different original first meetings reindexed to this actual common expiry. There is no equality between the full E_f x O_f and actual eligible pairs. In (3) one can require K>=J_f+D_s/3, where D_s=floor(T/(u ell^6)): the old birth age restriction C_source-C_U>=D_s/2 holds; the gain from the mixed birth to its expiry jump endpoint is O(Bu), since its moving witness at U has score<=Theta, each nonLOW factor>=L0, every witness that is still valid at expiry predecessor starts no later than U if no later light and no new roots, and moving duration from the latest light start through expiry is bounded by O(log Theta/log L0) nonLOW jumps, each gain<=B, plus LOW blocks <L0. More explicitly, for any moving witness at the expiry predecessor its start is at or before latest eligible start <=U; its number of nonLOW events up to predecessor <=log(Theta/L0)/log L0, so the portion after U has this bound even if the minimizing witness switched. The extra expiry jump adds at most B. Hence O(Bu)=o(D_s), proving the claimed age restriction eventually. No assertion that expiry is a physical birth or that all old tags at that state were already nonmoving at the birth.

At a fixed expiry p>y, poststate (v,J_f) has s=v mod2 and p|J_f+2s, p|k where k=(2v-J_f-6s)/2>=0, from actual MIN exit congruences. Thus RESET keys k=0 have O(X/p) support, nonreset k>0 have O(XH/p²) support. No assumption of roughness or expensive wait at the expiry jump. Reindex exact mixed residual by f (one physical slot per (J_f,p,v)), and pay its total if it is <= the following absolute threshold:
    RESET: H u^[p>=B/u]/(u ell²);
    nonreset: p u^[p>=B/u]/(u ell²).
By the old reciprocal prime sums O(ell) and O(ell/u) for the high band, summing support times threshold gives O(XH/(u ell))=O(NT/ell) in each case. This is an additional allowed expiry-key payment after earlier exact birth-key payments, not a second copy of the mixed charge. One may use (3), or its actual-lifetime and ancestry-age refinement, to exclude a key if the envelope is below its cutoff. The total upper bound for the mixed remainder can be taken as the minimum of the old actual-birth envelope and the summed allowed expiry envelope. No estimate of the summed expiry envelope strong enough for a constant margin has been obtained. Both-nonmoving first births retain their old routing and all earlier masks.

## 6. New residual

Apply all weighted source payments, both-moving and small mixed payments, and the mixed expiry-key cut sequentially. Total o(NT), since Theta/B=B^(-epsilon) dominates polynomial logarithmic factors, eta->0. Under finitely many twins
    NT/2-o(NT) <= B_new+(3+o(1))(D_l,new+D_h,new).
The corresponding strict liminf <1/2 suffices, with objective no larger than before. Equations (1)-(2) improve the earlier fixed-depth ancestry requirement, and (3) plus expiry support gives a new way to evaluate the formerly mixed sector. Neither the aggregate nonmoving births, mixed expiry charge nor the remaining forward energy has been bounded by a sufficiently small constant. No Lean compilation. Main and independent branches unmodified.

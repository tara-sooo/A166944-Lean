# Negative MIN: order-free high-height persistence and an additional height increment near 2/7

2026-11-28 JST; Issue #10. Continue from c20b70c398f0e2165e08da694790dacc1b724d50. Goal 4.85, canonical high-height occupation and eventual unconditional twin-prime proof. Core c9b2d47f7cf473e3f8768a7c9292d4b2205c536a (614 declarations) unchanged. Two paper deductions: (A) a full-density canonical high-source family with a COMMON elapsed high-height interval, using a shell bound rather than counting histories; (B) a slightly thinner family with an actual strictly increasing height for a second quiet phase, by a polylogarithmic progression restriction in the bilinear lower sieve. ## 1. Input and a useful order-free eligibility identity

Use the HIGH family from c20b70c, derived from Wu (2004) Lemma 2.5 and the ordinary well-factorable linear lower sieve. For L=log R, fixed theta1=.2855, theta2=.2856, there are >>R/L² distinct canonical A=pv-1 in [R,2R], with p,v prime, R^theta1<=p<=R^theta2, v=7 mod12, v=1 mod5,7, and P^-(pv-2)>=4R^theta2. The first event is exactly p at age (3p-7)/2, destination x0=p(v-1), clock n0=p-x0/2, height p. Initial source is non-twin. The source roughness excludes p=2 mod3, hence A divisible6. The first-event proof uses canonical height bound p'<=2age+7, not a probabilistic selection of p.

For any subsequent eligible label q at tick n0+u while the current position is x0-g, with g even and nonnegative, put s=+1 for even tick, -1 for odd, b_s=0 or3 respectively. Eligibility alone implies
    q | F(p,u,g,b_s) := 2p+2u-g+1-2b_s,
    q | p(v-1)-g+s.                                      (1)
These follow from the same height/clock pins used earlier, or directly from q|n (plus), q|n-2 (minus). If g<p-1, q=p is impossible: g even, so 0<=g<p-1 cannot be +/-1 mod p. In that range, F is positive provided u>=1, and q<=F=O(p+u). For fixed p,u,g,b_s, the candidates q above any threshold Z are among prime divisors of the single positive integer F. There are at most log F/log Z such candidates. For q!=p the second pin fixes v in one residue modulo q. We use this NECESSARY eligibility condition to remove bad source roots; it is not a sufficient condition for winning. In particular the method does not require any assumption about the sequence of prior labels or their order.

## 2. Shell control of cumulative displacement and common elapsed high height

Fix any real a>=1. Set H=floor(L^a), M=ceil(L^(a+8)), and
    J=floor(L/(10 log(2M))), D_j=(2M)^j for 0<=j<=J.
Thus J~L/[10(a+8)log L], D_J<=R^.1, H>=J for large R. For each j<J, call a root exceptional if there is any even 0<=g<=D_j, any integer 1<=u<=H, and a prime q>=M D_j satisfying (1) for either sign. (This allows more candidates than actual histories and more than actual eligibility.) Since D_J<<p and H<<p, F is positive O(p), q<=O(p), q!=p. For fixed p,u,g and sign, the total reciprocal q weight is O(L/(M D_j)), by the divisor count (we use the cruder O(L) bound). For each q, count v in [(R+1)/p,(2R+1)/p] in its residue: O(R/(pq)), since pq<=O(R^(2theta2))=o(R). Ignore primality, source roughness and fixed AP for this upper. There are O(D_j) g values including zero. Sum u, signs and p with sum_{R^theta1<=p<=R^theta2}1/p=O(1) by Mertens. Each shell's bad root count is O(R H L/M). Summing J<=L shells gives
    B_shell << R H L²/M = O(R/L^6)=o(R/L²).                (2)
All constants can depend on fixed a and the original exponents. No union over words, no factor depending exponentially on H or J.

On any nonexceptional actual path, after j<J further firings, suppose g<=D_j (true at j=0). If the next firing occurs within the first H ticks after the high arrival, (2)'s exclusion implies q<M D_j, so
    g_new <= D_j+M D_j <= D_(j+1).
Idle ticks do not increase g. Therefore follow the actual path to the earlier of H ticks and completion of its J-th further firing, INCLUDING the landing of that J-th event. Every visited position is in [x0-D_J,x0]. Every visited height is
    h=p+u-g/2 >= p-D_J/2=(1-o(1))p.                       (3)
The retained connector contains at least J+1 states, since J actual events take at least J ticks, and H>=J. In particular it contains the entire common relative interval u=0,...,J. This guarantees actual elapsed time J after the first event for every root; it is not a bound on the time needed to complete J events. For H=L^a more generally the connector either reaches that common H still in the high-height rectangle or has completed J additional events there (possibly exactly at H). This explicit time-or-event-depth alternative allows J of order log R/loglog R, which is much larger than the earlier word-count depth of order loglog R/logloglog R, but DOES NOT assert that every path completes J events by H. Nor does (3) imply that the height increases above p; it might decrease by o(p).

## 3. Physical separation over the whole shell connector

Two distinct HIGH roots have first anchors (x0,n0) and (x0',n0') with h0=p and h0'=q. If their connector states coincide, monotonic positions imply |x0'-x0|<=D_J and clocks imply |n0'-n0|<=H. Put d=x0'-x0; then |q-p|<=H+D_J/2. If p=q, both x0,x0' multiples of p and D_J<p force x0=x0', hence identical roots, impossible. For p!=q and fixed d, spatial pins x0=0 modp, x0+d=0 modq prescribe one CRT class. For fixed p,d, at most O(H+D_J+1) integer candidates q, with q~p. The number of x0 in the slab per such pair is O(R/p²) since R/p² grows polynomially. Sum d and p, even over all integer p>=R^theta1:
    E_near << R^(1-theta1)(D_J+1)(H+D_J+1)
            = O(R^.9145) (for large R).                  (4)
Here H<=R^.1 eventually. Count near pairs before selecting connectors; delete both endpoints of every near pair. Loss o(R/L²). Together with (2) this retains >>R/L² actual HIGH roots with pairwise disjoint PHYSICAL STATE sets through the entire stopped connector, and in particular through common postarrival relative elapsed J~L/[10(a+8)log L]. Their high-state count is >>R J/L² >> R/(L log L). Original elapsed ages are <=(3/2)R^.2856+H <=2R^.2856 for sufficiently large R. All statements concern postarrival states (not the first waiting interval); there is no common signed clock assumption. If twin centers are finite, every such state is non-twin for sufficiently large R, by its position being of order R. Without that hypothesis, if a fixed positive fraction of retained roots reach a twin during the connector, they force >> R^.9/L² distinct twin centers in that slab enlarged by O(R^.2856): a given twin y can have x0 within [y,y+D_J], and each x0 has O(1) possible first labels p>=R^theta1 dividing it, each fixing A=x0+p-1. This is only a dichotomy; the premise of the twin-rich case is not established. The shell theorem itself does not need a no-twin condition.

## 4. A second quiet phase giving an actual positive height increment at the same first-label exponent

Fix 0<epsilon<1. Choose a small constant c1>0 and K=floor(c1 log L). Let
    B=12 prod_{5<=ell<K prime} ell.
Choose c1 so small that B<=L^epsilon for large R (Chebyshev sum log ell <=C K, and choose C c1<epsilon/2). B includes 5,7 eventually. Use a fixed residue v0 modulo B with v0=7 mod12 and v0=1 mod every prime 5<=ell<K. This class is reduced. It varies with R but B is at most a fixed power of log R. For any p in the earlier HIGH source range, if v is prime in this AP and pv-2 is rough as before, then A canonical, x0=p(v-1) is divisible by all odd primes<K, and hence x0±1 are K-rough. The source remains composite on the plus side.

Repeat the product-sieve construction from c20b70c in the same disjoint dyadic boxes P<p<=1.2P, 1.1 R/P<v<=1.4 R/P, using alpha_v=prime indicator in this AP modulo B and beta_p=UNRESTRICTED smaller prime indicator. The primary input is Wu Lemma2.5, verified again in its original PDF pp221-222. It only imposes SW on beta, not alpha. Its constants are uniform for bounded alpha and the same beta SW condition; B appears only in alpha. Ordinary Siegel-Walfisz gives the unsifted prime pair total in a box
    B_P ~ const R/[phi(B)L²]
uniformly for B<=L^epsilon and the specified p range, because log(R/P)~L. Level D=R^.5713, cutoff 4R^.2856 and fixed approximation margin as before. The ordinary linear lower main coefficient is independent of B. The coprimality correction is still O(B_P L/R^theta1); weighted distribution errors O_A(R/L^A) are negligible relative to B_P/L since phi(B)<=L^epsilon. Thus for some absolute fixed lower coefficient a0>0 (depending on the fixed sieve exponents but not B or c1) each box yields >=a0 R/[phi(B)L³] distinct roots for all sufficiently large R. Summing >>L boxes produces
    N_B >> R/[phi(B)L²] >= R/L^(2+epsilon).               (5)
More precisely the last inequality has an implied positive constant. No fixed residue restriction on beta, no extra product distribution theorem, no individual growing polynomial modulus asymptotic. The use of SW here is the ordinary prime theorem for log-power moduli, which is already the input to Wu's beta condition.

At the high arrival x0,n0, roughness to K by itself is insufficient: height p permits an immediate large jump. Choose H0=floor(c2 K) with a sufficiently small FIXED c2>0 to be determined. Suppose a second firing occurs at 1<=u<=H0; prior displacement from x0 is zero. Its label q>=K, q!=p, satisfies
    q|2p+2u+1-2b_s,  v=1-s/p mod q.                    (6)
We bound all such roots, retaining the source primality and roughness and the fixed AP. Work per dyadic p box with X=R/P. For each p,q satisfying (6), v lies in at most one class modulo Bq since q>=K is outside the factors of B. Sieve v and pv-2 on that progression, both R^theta1-rough. In parameter length O(X/(Bq)), which is polynomially large uniformly since q<=O(P) by (6) and B<=L^epsilon, the elementary two-form Selberg upper gives
    count <= C X/[B q L²] * (B/phi(B))²
          = C X/[phi(B) q L²] * (B/phi(B)).              (7)
Uniform constant C independent of B. Justification: omit primes <=5 and those dividing B p q. The two affine forms have distinct roots at every remaining prime (cross-determinant proportional to 2 Bq), density 2/ell. Actual omitted-prime loss prod_{ell|B p q,ell>=7}(1-2/ell)^(-1) <= C(B/phi(B))² by the convergent O(ell^-2) ratio, and factors from large p,q bounded. Apply the same restricted Euler-product Selberg denominator argument as 54a7bf6, dimension two, with cutoff min(R^theta1/2,parameter_length^1/12); log level >>L uniformly. Error O(length^.5) absorbed. Non-reduced v class at q can be discarded (v>q); ignoring it by an upper is also harmless.

Split q into small K<=q<=R^.05 and large q>R^.05. For small q, (6) fixes p in one residue mod q. If residue zero, no prime p~P>q. Otherwise the elementary dimension-one progression upper gives O(P/(q L)) such primes uniformly (parameter P/q polynomially large, omit q). Combining with (7) and summing both signs and u:
    B_small,box << R/[phi(B)L³] * (B/phi(B)) H0
                   * sum_{q>=K prime} 1/q²
               << R/[phi(B)L³] * H0/K.                 (8)
Indeed Mertens B/phi(B)<<log K, and Chebyshev/dyadic summation sum_{q>=K prime}1/q²<<1/(K log K). The constants are independent of B,K,c1,c2. For large q, for each p,u,b_s the positive integer in (6) has O(1) prime factors >R^.05, hence sum of reciprocals O(R^(-.05)). Sum (7) over prime p with #p<<P/L, and u/signs, to get
    B_large,box << R/[phi(B)L³] * (B/phi(B)) H0 R^(-.05)
                = o(R/[phi(B)L³]).                       (9)
Both bounds count potential early eligibility, not just the actual winning label, and may count roots multiple times (upper only). Pick c2 sufficiently small relative to a0 and the uniform constant in (8); for large R, (8)+(9) removes at most half the per-box lower. Thus after all boxes we still have >>R/[phi(B)L²] actual high anchors with zero subsequent displacement through common relative H0~log L and exactly
    h(x0,n0+H0)=p+H0.                                  (10)
This is a strict additive height increment on actual canonical trajectories. It is caused by a quiet interval, NOT by a proven second completed firing with positive height change. The increment is only of order loglog R; the leading exponent remains .2855.

We can ensure the destination is non-twin as well: if x0 is twin, the four forms v,pv-2,p(v-1)-1,p(v-1)+1 are all R^theta1-rough. For fixed p the uniform dimension-four upper in e51533d gives O(R(log L)^4/(pL^4)), since all determinants nonzero for p>=11, all coefficient and determinant magnitudes polynomial, and v interval polynomial. Sum reciprocal p=O(1) to get O(R(log L)^4/L^4)=o(R/L^(2+epsilon)) for epsilon<1. The restriction v in the varying AP only decreases that exceptional count. Removing these roots leaves both visited centers non-twin unconditionally. To separate the second quiet phases physically, apply (4) with D=0,H=H0: same x0 and |n0'-n0|<=H0 imply distinct p,q and q near p, count O(R^(1-theta1)(H0+1))=o(R/L^(2+epsilon)). Remove pair endpoints. The resulting positive lower has disjoint physical states through H0 with strict height increment. Every state in (10) has at least one canonical ancestor by construction, hence is a positive term of the exact reverse count from c20b70c; this is actual reachability at the larger height, not an inference from eligibility alone. No need to assume the reverse ancestry function has uniform density elsewhere.

## 5. What is still missing and verification

The shell method removes the earlier signed-word cost for this time-or-depth alternative; it does not imply that there are many long postarrival phases of polynomial duration. The second quiet method gives a true but logarithmic height increment without weakening the first label exponent; it does not generate a new polynomial exponent. To achieve .4 or the square-root horizon, one needs a mechanism to repeat height increments with substantial size and sufficient occupation, or to show a fundamentally different dynamic certificate. We have not shown the canonical occupation of top local ports from e9f611d, or the .40 gain witness, or 4.85. The unreviewed .295 draft is unused.
